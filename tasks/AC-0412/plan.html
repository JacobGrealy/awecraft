AC-0412 — where do the orbital faceting and vertical streaks come from?

Coordinator contract. Medium builder applies it with tasks/templates/implement.md. Establish, then choose between two different fixes. The ticket forbids picking a plausible one.

1. What is known

From the AC-0384 round-19 and round-21 assessments, re-confirmed by round 22: the satellite body from orbit carries sixteen-metre faceting and vertical streak artifacts. The pastel and low-contrast half was fixed separately (the mesh winding repair), so what remains is these two. The assessment characterises them as bake character — a 16 m facet is the size of the texel the bake produces at the body's scale, and streaks are what a bake produces when its sampling grid is coarser than the relief it carries.

2. WHY THIS TICKET MAY BE PARTLY STALE — read before measuring anything

AC-0469 stage D REBUILT the far-tier bake since this was filed. `FACE_GRID` was a literal 64 (the old grid's whole face, covering ~16% of a chart face and charting 329 chunks past its edge); it now derives from `SphereMath.CHUNKS_PER_FACE` = 393, the bake covers 100% of each chart face (154,449 slots, 1,699,726 records), and its own notes record the texel budget as NPIX 1024 → ~2.6 texels per chunk, 6.136 m/texel — "unchanged in metres". So the texel may be 6.1 m, not 16 m, and the coverage is now complete where it was 16%. Every number in the ticket's framing must be re-measured on the current tree before it is reasoned about. AC-0426 also changed the bake's sampling (the bake worker's per-pixel centre-cell read) — check whether that is the "coordinate derivation" the ticket hypothesised.

3. What to establish (the ticket's own list, in its own order)

  1. 3.1 The bake's actual resolution on the current tree: texels per face, metres per texel, records per face, and how a texel's footprint compares with the terrain relief over that same area. Report the numbers.
  2. 3.2 Whether the streaks come from THE BAKE or from THE SAMPLING. The ticket names three candidate sampling causes: a mip level, a filter mode, or a coordinate derivation that collapses along one axis. These are different fixes and the evidence must choose. A decisive test is to sample the SAME bake with the filter/mip/coordinate term isolated (as AC-0402 did for the grey planet — isolate one term at a time rather than guessing).
  3. 3.3 Which artifact is which: are the facets and the streaks one cause or two? State it.

5. Decision and cost, stated honestly

The ticket requires the cost to be stated before accepting a fix: a finer bake costs bake time and memory at load — and AC-0469 stage D measured the CURRENT bake at 1,269 s and 4.60 GiB peak per seed, so "finer" is expensive in a way this ticket could not have known. A better sampling of the existing bake costs nothing at load and may not help; a different filtering costs almost nothing. So the cheap options must be tried and reported before the expensive one is proposed — and if the expensive one is genuinely required, its cost must be given against the measured 1,269 s / 4.60 GiB baseline, not as "slower".

7. Acceptance

Current bake resolution measured, the artifact cause localised with an isolating experiment, a cheap fix tried first with a stated metric, and any expensive option costed against the measured bake baseline.

8. Risks

(1) Reasoning from the ticket's 16 m figure when the bake has since been rebuilt — 3.1 exists to prevent that. (2) Jumping to a finer bake because it sounds like the cause, when the ticket explicitly says a better sampling may cost nothing. (3) Reporting "looks smoother" with no metric — the assertions must be measurable, and streaks in particular need a metric along their own axis.
