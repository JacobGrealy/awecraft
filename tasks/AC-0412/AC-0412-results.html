3.3 — facets vs streaks: one cause or two

Two causes in principle, neither a dominant visible artifact on the current bake. The "16 m facets" are the 16 m displacement-mesh grid (relief floor); the streaks are the chainless texture sampling. Isolated at 2600 m nadir: mesh 16 → mesh 256 (R1→R2, same sampling) removes only 1–7 % of the grain (0.0089/0.0095 → 0.0088/0.0093 disc; 0.0113/0.0137 → 0.0107/0.0128 central), and the lag-1..8 scan of R1 is monotonic — no visible 2.2 px 16 m grid at all. The faceted coastline look in the captures is the 6 m texel aliasing the 1 m terrain edges (bake floor) — identical in the mesh256 frame. So the ticket's strong artifacts were an old-bake + old-sampling interaction; the ticket is stale, as §2 warned.

Fix — cheap option tried first, measured; expensive option costed

Tried (cheap, landed, switch-gated): re-land the AC-0384 r5 sampling state — filter_linear_mipmap_anisotropic + generate_mipmaps() on both texture-creation sites, behind AWECRAFT_SAT_MIP (default ON; =0 reproduces the pre-AC-0412 sampling exactly). Cost: zero load-time work beyond the one-time chain build at texture load; +≈1.33 MiB/face texture memory (chain above the 4 MiB base) ≈ +16 MiB total. Measured: grain_x −3.9 % at 2600 m limb, −0 to −2 % at 1600 m limb (A/B pairs above, before/after captures r2b/r3b and r4a/r4b); streakdir and colrow unchanged; disc grain (0.0074) is now below its own source face's grain (0.0098) — the correct anti-aliased regime (r5's diagnostic was the disc being grainer than its source).

Costed (expensive, NOT required — the residual is the floor): (a) Finer bake (e.g. 2048/face): the 1,699,726-record payload (the dominant sD cost) is unchanged; the per-face pixel stage quadruples (O(npix²)) → ≥ the measured 1,269 s / 4.60 GiB baseline per seed, ≈1.3–2.3× it in practice, +≈200 MiB load texture memory (16 MiB/face base), and it invalidates every seed's cache. Gain: it only subdivides the same centre-cell undersampling — sub-6 m relief that is 0.42 px on screen and not resolvable from orbit. (b) Better sampling of the existing bake (footprint average instead of centre cell): a full re-bake at the SAME cost baseline (1,269 s / 4.60 GiB, cache invalidated) for a sub-1 m/texel smoothing — below the visual threshold. (c) Finer mesh (4 m): 4× vertices (1.7 M → 6.8 M), a large render-cost hit on a 2–5 FPS box, for the measured 1–7 % of grain. None justified; the measured floor is what it is.

Separate finding (out of fence): the canonical seed-44 ship still ships the pre-AC-0469-sD piece-1 asset (6.136 m 1:1 texel, the old chart with the 329-chunk edge bleed) — it is the only place the ticket's artifacts survive today, and it predates the current pipeline; re-baking/re-shipping it is a coordinator/asset decision. The runtime bake for any non-canonical seed (and the code-side chain, which this fix also applies to the cache/res load path) is current.
