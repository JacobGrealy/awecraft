AC-0412 — deviations from plan.html
====================================

D1. The ticket's 16 m figure is a MESH number, not a texel number (the stale
    premise §2 warned about, confirmed by re-measurement).
    - 16 m = MESH_STEP (the displacement mesh vertex spacing, satellite_body.gd
      MESH_STEP=16.0): 15.988 m on the full axis, 7.994 m on the halved axis.
    - The bake's texel is NOT 16 m: it is 6.136 m on the full axis (W/1024) and
      3.068 m on the halved axis ((W/2)/1024). The "16 m facet" is a 16 m
      displacement-mesh facet, a different subsystem from the bake's texels.
    - I re-measured every number on the current tree before reasoning (3.1),
      exactly as §2 requires, rather than reasoning from the ticket's figure.

D2. The per-pixel centre-cell read came from AC-0469 stage D (fc1a6ac), not
    AC-0426 (the plan's §2 attribution). AC-0426 was the light-floor clamp.
    This changes the provenance of the "coordinate derivation" candidate but
    not its disposition: I inspected it and it is a monotone, per-coordinate
    linear read (no axis collapse) - not the streak cause.

D3. Render recipe deviation. The plan's recipe assumed ~1200 s renders. On this
    box:
    - A cache-hit render finished in ~200-400 s wall (body LOADED ~164 s game
      time + 600-frame drain), not ~1200 s.
    - The FIRST bake-inclusive run (seed 5432, AWECRAFT_SNAP_DRAIN=60000) hung:
      the planet preset waits the body LOADED then drains EXACTLY
      AWECRAFT_SNAP_DRAIN frames, so a 60000-frame drain at ~2 FPS would never
      finish in budget. I killed it (the bake cache had already been written,
      so the 1,269 s bake was preserved) and re-ran with drain 600. All
      subsequent renders used drain 600 and completed.
    - HOME was pointed at the workspace (.scratch/AC-0412/home) instead of
      /tmp/dsh_home because the sandbox gives a fresh /tmp per bash call; the
      Godot 4.7.1 user:// path here is $HOME/.local/share/<project>/ (not
      app_userdata), so the bake cache p0_r4000_s5432_h persists across calls.

D4. The bake ran on seed 5432, not canonical 44. Canonical seed 44 loads the
    SHIPPED pre-AC-0469-sD piece-1 asset (no runtime bake), which is exactly the
    stale bake that carries the ticket's artifact. Seed 5432 forces the CURRENT
    bake pipeline to run so 3.1-3.3 are measured on the current tree. The
    shipped seed-44 asset is a separate (stale-asset) finding, out of my fence
    (shipped satellite assets are not to be touched).

D5. The shipped fix defaults ON (AWECRAFT_SAT_MIP=1 / chain present), matching
    the AC-0384 r5 precedent that shipped the fix on by default. AWECRAFT_SAT_MIP=0
    reproduces the pre-AC-0412 sampling exactly (a mipmap-filtering sampler over
    a chainless texture is a no-op). If the coordinator prefers a conservative
    default, it is a one-line flip of the var mip_chain default.

D6. satellite_bake_worker.gd was read but NOT edited. The bake content is
    measured isotropic (no 16 m periodicity, no streak structure), so no bake
    edit is named by 3.1/3.2; the residual is a sampling floor, and the cheap
    fix (the mip chain) lives on the sampler side (shader + texture creation),
    not the bake.
