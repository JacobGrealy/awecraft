3. The fix (what changed)

One file: godot/player/player.gd (+41 lines, no other file touched — harness.gd is byte-identical to HEAD, the probe removed at closeout).

  • _ac0479_warp_point(fx, fy, fz, R) — the exact per-vertex spherical warp, the same construction the mesh/collision consume: (R + fy) * (SphereMath.home_point(fx, fz, R) / R) - (0, R, 0).
  • _ac0479_warp_basis(fx, fy, fz, R) — central-difference that warp at the cell centre along each flat axis (ε = 0.1) and orthonormalize() — rotation only, no scale/offset to “fit” (per plan §4.1).
  • _update_interaction home-pair branch (_anchor.face <= 1): the box is now placed at the cell's warped centre (_ac0479_warp_point) and oriented by the warp Jacobian (_ac0479_warp_basis) instead of position-only on world_pos_of_flat. The face-pair branch (face > 1) is unchanged (out of scope, §6).

The AC-0420 idle-creep damper, friction, and movement speed are untouched.

6. Deviations & findings

Full text in AC-0479-deviations.txt. Summary:

  • D1 (justified): 4.1's Jacobian is sampled on the exact warp (SphereMath.home_point), not on world_pos_of_flat as the plan's literal wording says. world_pos_of_flat is the per-column rigid facet approximation — the frame under test; sampling it at ±ε would return the misoriented facet basis and not fix the defect. The plan's intent (match the block's actual warped orientation) is met.
  • D2: home-vs-face split uses the existing _anchor.face discriminator the aim code already uses.
  • D3 (finding, out of scope): face-pair (face>1) highlight placement feeds face-chunk CELL coords into world_pos_of_flat (which expects flat block coords) — a unit/domain mismatch. The aim box past the home patch was wrong before and remains wrong; this ticket's fence is the home-pair box (the user's report/screenshot is home terrain).
  • D4 (finding, out of scope): the flat frame itself (world_pos_of_flat/ flat_of_world_pos) diverges from the sphere by metres at large D (farB D≈310 m: a player standing on the warped surface reads back as flat y 158.6 vs the block-grid top 159.0, ~0.4 m round-trip gap; the probe's farB site embeds the player and freezes the walk test). The 4.1 fix removes the box's dependence on the facet map, so the box is right at every D in the home pair; the flat-frame divergence at distance is a separate, larger issue (the flat frame itself), untouched per the fences.
  • D5 (finding, out of scope): WorldGen.terrain_height (generator.gd:76) — the GDScript “mirror” of surface height — is STALE; the live C++ surface_h (gdext/src/gen.cpp:1038) diverges from it by 3–23 m. Site selection / analytic height must use the live streamed grid.
  • D6: 4.2 made no code change — the 3.3 measurement names no fix (see §4, gate c).
