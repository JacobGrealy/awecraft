AC-0479 deviations — plan.html vs. what was applied

D1. 4.1 Jacobian sampled on the EXACT warp, not on world_pos_of_flat.
    The plan's 4.1 says: "derive the local basis from the warp's
    Jacobian at the cell (sample world_pos_of_flat at the cell centre
    and at ±ε along each axis...)". world_pos_of_flat is NOT the warp:
    it is the per-column RIGID facet map (SphereMath.flat_to_world ->
    column_transform, sphere_math.gd:387/419) — the very approximation
    the magnitude test (r7, gate a) proved misaligns the box. Sampling
    IT at ±ε returns the facet basis (the current, misoriented frame)
    and would not fix the defect. The warp the mesh and collision
    actually consume is chunk._warp_home_cell_point
    (chunk.gd:1941): (R+ly)*(home_point(fx,fz,R)/R) - (0,R,0), where
    home_point is the cube-map sphere map (sphere_math.gd:379). The
    fix samples THAT (helpers _ac0479_warp_point/_ac0479_warp_basis in
    player.gd, central differences ε=0.1, orthonormalized — rotation
    only, no scale/offset-to-fit, per the plan's prohibition).
    Effect: the plan's intent (box matches the block's actual warped
    orientation) is met; the plan's named sampling function was not,
    because it is the approximation under test.

D2. The highlight's home-pair branch is selected via _anchor face.
    The plan's anchor list names _update_interaction:3175-3190 as the
    "position-only placement" and does not specify how to distinguish
    the home pair from the face pairs for the new orientation. The
    existing aim code already branches on int(_anchor.get("face",0)) > 1
    (player.gd aim_hit :2795); the fix uses the same discriminator
    (face <= 1 -> exact-sphere placement; face > 1 -> unchanged
    world_pos_of_flat path, out of scope).

D3. Finding, reported only (out of scope, not fixed): face-pair
    (face > 1) highlight placement feeds FACE-CHUNK CELL coordinates
    into world_pos_of_flat, which expects flat block coordinates — a
    unit/domain mismatch (face cells are ~6 m wide). The aim box past
    the home patch was therefore wrong before and remains wrong; this
    ticket's fence is the home-pair box. (Consistent with the plan's
    scope: the user report and screenshot are home-terrain.)

D4. Finding, reported only (out of scope): the flat frame itself
    (world_pos_of_flat / flat_of_world_pos, the per-column rigid net)
    diverges from the home_point sphere by metres at large D — at
    D~310 m a player standing on the warped surface of block y=158
    reads back as flat y=158.6 vs the block-grid top 159.0 (~0.4 m
    round-trip gap; the probe's farB site at D=310 m embeds the
    player and freezes the walk test). Anything still routed through
    the flat frame at distance (aim DDA origin, teleports, the face
    placement of D3) inherits this. The 4.1 fix removes the BOX's
    dependence on the facet map (exact sphere construction), so the
    box is correct at every D in the home pair; the flat-frame
    divergence at distance is a separate, larger issue (the flat
    frame itself) and is not touched here per the fences.

D5. Finding, reported only (out of scope): WorldGen.terrain_height
    (godot/world/generator.gd:76) — the GDScript "mirror" of the
    surface height — is STALE. The live C++ surface_h (gdext/src/
    gen.cpp:1038) is a coarse-3D-field trilinear formula with a
    calibration remap; the mirror diverges from the live terrain by
    3-23 m (r6: 8/10 analytic site candidates rejected live-vs-
    analytic, both signs). Any site selection / analytic height query
    must use the live streamed grid, not the mirror.

D6. 4.2: no code change. The plan says "the minimal honest fix is
    whichever the measurement in 3.3 names". The 3.3 measurement
    (r7/r9, 5 s stand + 2 s walk, at D=11.3 m and D=153 m):
    standing drift 0.0 mm/s (spawn) and 0.68-0.71 mm/s UPHILL (farA,
    i.e. anti-downhill = numerical settling, far below the AC-0420
    idle-creep damper's 0.2 m/s band, which zeros it in normal play);
    walk veer 0.007 deg (farA), far under the ~2.5 deg terrain-noise
    floor seen at the spawn control (2.48 deg on a walk line with no
    flatness guarantee). No non-zero DOWNHILL standing drift was
    measured, so the damper-widening candidate is not triggered, and
    per the fences no friction/speed/damper change was made. The
    user's "slides in the direction the selection block is angled" is
    consistent with the box/facet tilt vs the warped terrain — the
    visual reference the 4.1 fix removes; the physics is unchanged by
    design.
