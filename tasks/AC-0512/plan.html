AC-0512 — measure the pop-in and gaps that fog was hiding

Coordinator contract (written by me; filed by another agent). Medium builder applies it with tasks/templates/implement.md. MEASUREMENT ONLY. The deliverable is a diagnosis of what the gaps actually are, not a fix.

1. Why this exists now

"I want the remaining pop-in and gaps measured after fog and mist are removed, so the fix addresses the actual missing landscape rather than disguising it."

AC-0510 removed the fog and the mist one round before and MEASURED WHAT THEY WERE HIDING: per-chunk slab edges at the frontier (wall-jump 9.5 → 20.0 mean, max 153 → 249) and part of the aerial perspective (contrast 7.75 → 17.97). It reported those as findings and did not restore the fog. This ticket is the follow-up: work out what those gaps ARE before anyone tries to close them.

2. THE CORE TASK — distinguish five different things that all look like "a gap"

The filing names five causes, and confusing them is how a fix gets built for the wrong one:

  1. ABSENT DRAWABLE CHUNKS — the geometry was never built.
  2. DISTANCE DISCARD — it was built and then culled by distance.
  3. SOURCE RESOURCE DELAY — the data exists but the draw is late (published but not yet drawn).
  4. GEOMETRY MISMATCH — both surfaces draw but their heights disagree (a step or a lip).
  5. ENDPOINT COLOUR MISMATCH — the geometry agrees but the colours do not.

For each observed gap, say WHICH of the five it is, with the evidence that discriminates it. A gap that exists in the geometry stream but not in the image is (2) or (3); one visible in both but with matching colour is (4); one with matching silhouette but a colour step is (5); one with nothing behind it is (1). This classification IS the deliverable, and it must be per-gap, not an aggregate impression.

3. What to measure

  • SIGNED surface error and PROJECTED displacement between the satellite tier and the detail tier — signed, because a step up and a step down are different bugs and a magnitude would hide which. Projected, because a 1 m error seen at 900 m is a different defect from 1 m at 30 m.
  • On all of these cases, named by the filing: coast, cliff, home/face seams, and all four near tiers. The seam case matters especially — this session has already found a kilometres-scale face-boundary bug (AC-0482) that the home face could not show.
  • The AC-0510 frontier numerics as the starting point (wall-jump 9.5 → 20.0, contrast 7.75 → 17.97), so the new work extends a measured baseline rather than replacing it.

4. The two fixtures the filing requires (both are gates, not extras)

  1. A 5-SECOND HELD-PUBLICATION FIXTURE. Hold publication for a known interval and observe what the player sees during it. That isolates source resource delay (3) from the geometric causes — the data exists but is deliberately late, so anything visible during the hold is cause (3) by construction.
  2. AN INJECTED MISSING-FALLBACK NEGATIVE CONTROL. Expected: the exterior-gap counter RISES. This is the meter-sensitivity proof, and it is mandatory for the same reason it was mandatory in AC-0484, AC-0485 and AC-0527 — an instrument never shown to be able to register the defect is not evidence. If the counter does not rise when the fallback is removed, the counter cannot measure this class of defect at all and that is the finding.

9. Acceptance

Every observed gap classified into one of the five causes with discriminating evidence; signed and projected error measured on the named terrain cases and all four near tiers; the 5 s held-publication fixture reported; the missing-fallback negative control demonstrably raising the gap counter (or a plain statement that it cannot); instruments inert when off with outputs unchanged; full metadata, development-only labelling and a Windows handover.

10. Risks

(1) Aggregating the gaps instead of classifying them, which makes the later fix a guess. (2) Reporting an unsigned magnitude, hiding whether the surface is high or low. (3) A negative control that does not rise, quietly not mentioned. (4) Reinstating fog to make the picture look better — forbidden. (5) Trusting stale anchors. (6) Re-rendering speculatively at ~20 min a shot. (7) Presenting llvmpipe numbers as the product.
