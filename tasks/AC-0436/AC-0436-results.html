2 — What was done: the moon as the sun's antipode

Direction-to-moon = DayNight.sun_direction(t) itself (the sun sits at −sun_direction, so the antipode sits at +sun_direction — whose y is positive exactly when the sun is below the horizon). Intensity: DayNight.MOON_L := 0.25 — one home in core/daynight.gd; every consumer reads it, none restate it. Night weight = (1 − day(t)) × switch. Switch: Settings moonlight, default ON (the shipped night is the filed defect — the ambient_sky/cloud_gate/viewmodel_occlude re-ruling), with AWECRAFT_MOON=0|1 env preload (world.gd, written without save), an Options row, and sanitize_bool. Contract: at day or OFF every added term is exactly 0.0 — 0.0·x=0.0, x+0.0=x, mix(x,y,0)=x in IEEE 754 → the pre-AC-0436 paths bit-identical by construction, proven by measurement (§4).

3 — Deliberate deviations from the plan (each with its reason)

  • No MoonLight DirectionalLight3D node (plan component 6 dropped): the chunk shaders are unshaded — they never see engine lights — and a light node would double-light the LIT satellite body (engine ambient × its u_day re-weight), which would have shown up in the 2600 orbit acceptance frame. The moon IS the shader terms (that is the "show what it lights"); lit materials (mobs, viewmodel, drops) keep the existing 0.25 ambient at night — they were never the reported defect.
  • The moon halo stays at amount 0.3 (the 0.3→0.45 aero.gd bump dropped): aero.gd is a core pure-logic helper that is switch-blind by design — bumping the amount there would have changed the OFF state, breaking the bit-identical contract. The full-brightness disc (the shader's mix(sun_amount, 1.0, u_moon)) carries the look instead.
  • MOON_L calibrated 0.40 → 0.25 after the pipeline math: 0.40 put the night top faces at ~80% of day luma — dusk, not night. 0.25 puts them at ~45% of day (the atlas greens) — readable terrain that still reads as night.

6 — Deliberate non-actions

  • The tone mapping is untouched — day is correct under it (98.41 before/after, bit-identical), and re-tuning a global curve to fix night would have been the day-flatten failure mode the ticket names. The night was fixed where the defect lives: there was no light source, and now there is.
  • The star mesh is untouched (500 stars, main.gd — fenced) — the density comes from the shader's size lift.
  • No moon shadows — the chunk path never casts shadows; shadow maps are not worth the cost for ambient night light (the sun's light owns the daytime shadows).
  • No C++ changes, no .so rebuild — the fix is entirely GDScript + shaders; godot/bin/libchunkio.so (built from HEAD a5e04ac) is current.
