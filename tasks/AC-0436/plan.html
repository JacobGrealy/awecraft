AC-0436 — make the night a world (plan)

Goal

Re-take the assessment's frames (r24 recipe: seed 44, user config R24/F83, 1280×720, Forward+/lavapipe, no AWECRAFT_LOGIC) and beat them: the night ground horizon reads as terrain, the night planet from orbit reads as a world, the star field reads as a sky, and the interface stops being the brightest object — with luminance + star counts before/after, and the DAY frames measured too (a fix that greys the day has failed).

The two named causes — confirmed how

  1. Tone-mapping crush — CONFIRMED PARTIALLY, CORRECTED. Measured the r24 assessment frames (display-space luma): night ground region (y440–600) averages 4.0/255 with 90.7% of pixels below 20; day ground averages 98.4/255. So the ~0.20 light floor does not literally die to 0 at 8 bits — it survives at a few sRGB units. What kills it: the night floor is 5× the day level before tone mapping, and the active ACES tonemap (Aero.TONEMAP_MODE, exposure 0.85 — note ARCHITECTURE §6's "Linear restored" line is stale against the code) crushes that 0.02–0.08-linear band to ~5–11/255. Mechanism confirmed, "pure black" claim corrected to "a few units, i.e. black to the eye". No change to tone mapping is planned — the day is right under it and the fix must not touch it.
  2. No moon at all — CONFIRMED. The only DirectionalLight3D is the sun (main.gd, fence-locked): at night sun_energy = 0.1 and it points below the horizon (light travels upward — it lights nothing). Chunk shaders scale all sky light by u_day = DayNight.day(t) = exactly 0.0 at night (the AC-0426 u_amb term is 0.0 at night by construction). The satellite body's night factor is the flat 0.18 floor with no directional term (u_day_gain = 0). The sky pass does draw a small moon disc (Aero.sky_uniforms body-flip, MOON_CORE/MOON_HALO, ~6 px, and at amount 0.3 the disc itself multiplies to ~0.3 brightness — a dim grey dot), but nothing in the lighting model answers to it. So: a moon exists as a dot; it exists as a light source not at all. That is what this task builds.

What I will do — a moon with a light source, behind one switch

Setting moonlight (default ON; OFF = the bit-identical pre-fix path by IEEE construction; env AWECRAFT_MOON=0|1; Options row). Default ON because the acceptance frames are the default-setting frames and the defect IS the shipped night (the ambient_sky/cloud_gate precedent — the four fixes that shipped OFF were all re-ruled ON at HEAD; this one is filed as the disqualifying gap, so it ships in). The moon is the sun's antipode (same convention the sky pass's body-flip already uses), so it is above the horizon exactly when the sun is not. Moon convention: to_moon = DayNight.sun_direction(t); light travels along -to_moon.

Day-flatten guard

Every added term is multiplied by night · switch (or a mix(..., 0.0) that is exactly the old value), so at day (or OFF) the shaders evaluate the pre-fix expressions bit-for-bit. The day frames (ground-horizon-day, 2600-day) are measured before AND after: a day luma shift > 2/255 in the ground region is a failure to report, not a re-baseline.
