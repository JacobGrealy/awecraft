1. What changed

godot/world/world.gd — the queue priority (band buckets + drain + drain window intact)

sitechange
_enqueue_build (the append) NEW: push_front instead of append — the newest ahead entry lands at the FRONT of its band bucket. The drain's pool scan (_collect_pool) walks each bucket in index order, so the just-queued ahead chunk is now the first-scanned entry of its band: it wins the within-band order and the PICK_POOL_CAP (512) tie-break against the older entries already sitting in the band. Before, new ahead chunks were appended to the END of their band bucket, so the drain worked through the older entries first.
_rec_merge_want_step + _rec_merge_ring_step (the recenter rebuild) NEW: push_front instead of append — each recenter's fresh ahead chunks (the new stream set, i.e. the ahead edge in the flight direction) and its fresh band-3 feed entries (collar ∪ circle ring) go to the front of their band bucket, ahead of the re-bucketed older entries (_rec_merge_old_step unchanged — old entries keep their relative order).
_cap_queue_depth() (new) + _build_q_n lockstep counter (new) NEW: the total queued BUILD depth is capped to the render circle count (circle_count(): 797 at R16, 7845 at R50). When full, the farthest band is evicted first (highest non-empty bucket; oldest entries within the band = the tail, since the newest sit at the front). The counter is kept in lockstep at every mutation site (_enqueue_build, _remove_entry, _drop_queued, _convert_data_to_build, _strip_candidate_builds; recomputed at the recenter merge swap); a verifying scan self-heals any drift before an eviction can run. Enforced at _enqueue_build and at the merge finalization.
the band-3 (data_only) feed exemption The cap counts BUILD entries (the meshable work — exactly the circle). The band-3 data-only entries are exempt: they are the 8-neighbor build gate's fuel (AC-0160: the collar ∪ circle-ring data feeds the circle-edge chunks' 8-neighborhood) and do no mesh work — evicting them strands the circle edge unbuilt until the next recenter re-queues the feed (at R16 the 148 band-3 entries are exactly the 945−797 excess, so a literal total-cap eviction would have broken the R16 build). The exemption keeps the TOTAL queue bounded by the stream set (circle ∪ collar ∪ ring: 945 at R16, 8265 at R50) while the build queue stays at the circle count. Build entries are unique keys over the meshable set (exactly the circle), so the cap is a hard guard: it fires only on a bookkeeping drift (the scan self-heals it) or a bug that re-queues past the circle — the queue then stays bounded instead of growing to the full stream set. (At R50 the guard is what holds the 8265-entry stream queue to the 7845 build depth — the regime the R50 gate will measure, PENDING AC-0206.)
unchanged the drain itself (scored pick, unit/time budgets, windowed pool scan, startup/loading paths), the band-bucket structure, the _drain_win_b window, the AC-0219 1-mesh/frame handoff cap, the AC-0213 small-move budget + ahead-ring debounce, the 8-neighbor _build_ready gate, all handoff/removal bookkeeping.

Reading. At R16 the numbers are equal, not just non-regressed: with the circle already built, the steady streaming queue stays shallow (total ≤ 136, build depth ≤ 32 vs the 797 cap) because the recenter rebuilds drop the behind-edge and the drain (3-wide TM pool, ~20 builds/s) keeps up — so even before, the scored look-ahead pick delivered the forward edge while moving. The AC-0222 change is what hardens the deep-queue regime: at R50 class depth (8265-entry stream queue vs the 7845 build cap) the old far-first scan + the 512 pool cap starved the newest ahead entries (they sat at the END of their band bucket, beyond the pool cut, behind the older entries) — the "appears only after stopping" symptom. Now the newest ahead entries lead their band's scan and the build depth is hard-capped at the circle count with farthest-band-first eviction. The 50x phase exercises the debounced-rebuild regime (AC-0213): the first recenter's rebuild queues the new circle edge and the drain lands it (263 m ahead) while the player is still moving. 6. Scope notes / caveats

  • R50 was NOT run (PENDING — the .so crashes in the coordinator env, AC-0206); the Windows build was NOT run. The cap/eviction is the R50-class guard by construction (8265-entry stream queue → 7845 build depth); the R50 queue-no-stall gate will measure it when AC-0206 resolves.
  • The band-3 feed exemption (see §1) is a deliberate deviation from a literal "total queue depth ≤ circle count": evicting the 148 band-3 feed entries at R16 would strand the circle-edge 8-neighbor gate (built_all would fail with the player stationary). The build queue is capped at the circle count exactly as specified; the total stays bounded by the stream set. Flagging for the coordinator's review.
  • The R16 arm's build phase runs with loading_active = false (the arm re-centers directly, no loading window) — both before and after runs use the identical code path; the A/B is controlled.
  • Fly phases: 4x = 9 recenters (full rebuilds, 17 m/s → ~0.9 s/chunk), 50x = 27 recenters (debounced after the first, AC-0213 — pre-warm 5x5 ring + the first rebuild's edge entries). The ahead evidence counts FIRST appearances only (reband swaps are tracked separately as rebuilds, 0 in every run).
