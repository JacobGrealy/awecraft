Root causes (AC-0075 finding 2): (a) every recenter re-inserts O(r) new entries through _insert_sorted, which re-walks/resorts the whole queue — at r=50 that's ~101 new entries × 12 290-entry queue ≈ the 7 397 ms; (b) recenter EAGER-STUBS every chunk node in radius + both rings (≈10 609 bare nodes) up front. Both must die. The plan's "<1 ms recenter" claim is only valid at r≤8 (≤361 entries) — assume nothing about queue cost at r=50 (10 201 in-radius + rings ≈ 13 500 entries).

Requirements

  1. Scored queue (P1.1) — entry score = manhattan(dist) + lookPenalty + collPenalty per plan §3.1: lookPenalty = (1 − angleAlignment)·K (K ≈ 0.75·distScale; ±70° of player yaw sorts first), r+1/r+2 ring entries data-only sorting behind everything in-radius. Re-scored at recenter + throttled look updates (~2×/s max). The drain keeps its first-eligible forward scan (data-ready gating via _build_ready). High-r structural fix: the data structure must make RECENTER + LOOK-UPDATE O(k · log n) or O(n) with a tiny constant (k = new entries ≤ 2r+1), NOT O(n²) — e.g. bucket-per-distance-band arrays with within-band scoring, or a heap; a plain re-sort of 13 500 GDScript entries at every crossing is the anti-pattern. Drain step stays O(1)-amortized (forward scan with an advancing cursor or equivalent — do not scan from index 0 over ineligible entries each frame).
  2. Two-stage unload hysteresis (P1.2) — at Chebyshev r+1: chunk becomes a candidate — kill its mesh + collision NOW (frees the expensive CPU/GPU/physics memory immediately; data stays) and drop its pending light/fluid/tex work; at r+2 it is freed only after 2 consecutive recenter events (no flap: re-entering r clears candidate at once; boundary jitter = 0 frees); on free, clear ALL per-chunk maps: build_queue/queued keys, light_pending/set, and — new — fluid_dirty + tex_refresh. Flush loops must skip candidate/freed chunks (extend the existing null-checks at world.gd:81-84, 97-99.). Re-entry after candidate = rebuild (with edits re-applied), NOT re-generate (data survived).
  3. Kill the recenter O(n²) cost (NEW from baseline) — recenter must NOT re-sort/re-insert the whole queue: existing entries keep their band; only the ≤2(r+1) new entries are inserted and the freed-band entries removed (O(k·log n)); and stop eager-stubbing the entire radius: create stub nodes lazily (at data-gen time) or prove eager stubbing is not the cost (measure; report which of (a)/(b) dominated the 7 397 ms). Gate: recenter_ms < 100 ms at r=50 (from 7 397) and ≤ 2 ms at r=4 (regression guard).
  4. Budget envs (P1.7) — AWECRAFT_DRAIN_MS (default 30) and AWECRAFT_GEN_BUDGET (per-frame gen-work budget ms, default per current behavior) read at startup, documented in the results. They are tuning knobs for weak-web-HW, not a behavior switch.
  5. Hardening (P1.8) — player bucket placement passes create=true to set_fluid (player.gd ~820) so placement onto a not-yet-resident chunk creates it (matches set_block's create=true). 1 line + covered by the buckets harness (which must stay green).
