5. The cause, demonstrated

timeday(t)sun_energyambient_energynear L (unshaded)engine light on the satellite (lit)engine/near ratio
day1.0001.0500.6501.0001.7001.700
dusk0.3430.4260.3870.4740.8131.714
night0.0000.1000.2500.4500.3500.778

The near endpoint's L is a scalar applied to the atlas colour inside an unshaded shader. The satellite endpoint's decoded colour is multiplied by the engine's sun (sun_energy(t)) plus the engine's ambient, whose colour is the day sky (ambient_light_color, linear (0.235, 0.60, 0.83) — blue-dominant), on top of its own u_day factor, and it is mixed with u_air (the same sky colour) by haz_a. Two endpoints, two shading models: one receives no engine light, the other receives an engine light with a blue ambient plus an air mix. The ratio is time-of-day dependent, so the parity cannot hold at day, dusk and night at once.

5b. The demonstrated cause (measured, not assumed)

Satellite tier, 300 m, day, grass — 185,521 px, mean over the cell: rendered normalized linear (0.600, 0.843, 0.819), luma 0.790, against the shipped bake's own intent for the same texel set (0.037, 0.135, 0.015), luma 0.107 (measured from the shipped PNGs by decode.py). The satellite endpoint renders its own terrain +0.68 luma brighter with the hue pulled from green-dominant to blue-green-dominant — toward the ambient/air colour — at a mean haz_a of only 0.087, so the AC-0511 air mix is not the term doing it. Same cell at night: (0.0036, 0.0100, 0.0087), luma 0.0085 — a factor ~22 darker than the matched near-tier pair (sand, luma 0.103). Opposite sign at two times of day for the same material: a multiplicative engine light with a blue additive ambient is the only term in the shipped satellite path that behaves that way. That is suspect (2) — lit satellite vs unshaded near endpoints (the AC-0480 shading-model split), time-of-day dependent. Suspect (1) measured clean, (3) and (4) measured clean (§6).

Where the correction belongs: in the shading endpoint, not the palette. Narrow form, PROPOSED for the coordinator to resolve — not applied by this ticket: make the satellite body's terrain term unshaded the way the near tier is (its own u_day / u_moon factor is its light) so the engine's sun and blue ambient stop multiplying it, keeping haz_a as the single atmospheric term. No palette, bake, moon-gain, air-colour, Options, fog or wash change is implicated, and nothing is landed here.

6. Suspects (1), (3), (4) — measured

  • (1) baked satellite RGB decode vs the height data — VERIFIED, not the cause. All 12 shipped faces, 12582912 texels: 28700 (0.23 %) decode to a hue that is NOT a scalar multiple of one of the five FCC colours; the ocean rule against the height channel is exact — 761766 sub-SEA texels, 0 of them not water-coloured; 0 land texels read as water. Height channel present on all 12 faces (616 texels at alpha 0, none saturated).
  • (3)/(4) companding and tone mapping — single-companding, small. The bake encodes with the piecewise sRGB curve, the shader decodes with pow(2.2) (u_srgb_pre=1 on Forward+), the pipeline encodes with the piecewise curve and tone maps ONCE with Linear (tones_mapper=0). The round trip measures out_linear ≈ x^1.0303 — a ~3.0 % deviation in linear, the same on every channel, so it cannot produce a hue split. No source is pre-tone-mapped; no double companding of the satellite terrain term. (The near tier's AC-0242 pow(2.2) pre-encode is a deliberate round-trip convention, measured here as x^0.917 — a ~6 % lift, an order of magnitude below the light term in §5.)
Prohibitions respected. No fog/mist/wash/haz_d, no palette, bake, moon-gain or Options change, no sky/limb/cloud change, no generation, grid, save, collision, simulation, light-settle or streaming-queue change. No git add/commit. The dev isolation was removed before close.
