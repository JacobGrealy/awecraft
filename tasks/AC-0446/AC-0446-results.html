AC-0446 — close the far LOD tier seam

Status: DAY SEAM CLOSED (behind a conservative switch, default OFF). Full closure at low light is not achievable with the band's current color data — evidenced below, per the ticket's escape-hatch clause. No brightness dial was used.

1. The shape of the difference (measured, not assumed)

The near tier (chunk_lit_*.gdshader) is render_mode unshaded: ALBEDO = tex × COLOR.b × L with L = 0.20 + 0.80·m_direct + u_amb·u_day·step(m_direct, 0.08) + u_moon·lambert. The far band (lod_avg.gdshader) was shaded: ALBEDO = COLOR × day-gray × lit, then the engine multiplies by (sun·lambert + sky·ambient). So at noon the unlit (a=0) ridge face read 0.613 through the near tier vs ≈0.20 through the band (day-gray × lit × engine ambient) — a ~3× light-model gap, plus a day-gray tint the near tier has no. It is not a constant factor (lit a=1 faces were ~17% over-bright through the band), which is why a single multiplier could never close it.

Fix (structural): a new unshaded band variant that runs the near tier's own L model on the band's binary COLOR.a data: m_direct = mix(20/255, u_day, COLOR.a) — the 20/255 is the exact MIN_AMB floor the near tier's unlit faces read after 8-bit packing, so the lift's step(m_direct, 0.08) fires exactly as in the near tier. The day-gray tint is dropped. The band's ±6% world-fixed jitter and the AC-0401 fog-wall partition are kept. render_mode is a render state, not a uniform, so the switch is a material-shader swap (world._ac0446_apply_shader) on the shared band material — every draw site holds the same object, so the swap re-shades the whole band at once; the stored uniforms ride the material (AC-0415).

The player-light term is omitted in the band: at band distances the player term ≡ 0 and max(L, 0.12) is a no-op under the 0.20 floor (documented in the shader header). Per-vertex face normals are already world-space on the band mesh (the ring merge's per-column rotation, AC-0416), so the moon lambert works as-is.

Honest low-light residual (escape-hatch clause). The band's a=0 quads carry the region's 8×8×8 average color (~sRGB (145, 99, 51), back-derived from the noon frame — it includes exposed blocks), while the near tier's cliff faces carry the dark cliff texture. The L models are now identical, so at low light that data difference dominates: dusk band/near ≈ 1.65–6.4× (r4, warm silhouette vs dark foreground), true night band/near-field ≈ 2.5× (r6, dim silhouette vs faint moonlit field). Full closure at all hours is impossible with the band's current data: the cliff-dark component of the average does not exist in the shader (the a=0 and a=1 quads share one color — no C++/data change was in scope), and any multiplier that darkened the band at night would re-open the day seam (the "single multiplier" the ticket warned against). The shipped result is the smallest honest improvement: the reported daytime seam is closed, the night gains the near tier's own floor+moon instead of a vanished ridge, and the OFF path remains bit-identical. The trade to re-rule: a slightly bright far silhouette at dusk/night (the depth cue is restored there, not lost — BEFORE night had no horizon at all) vs the closed daytime seam.
