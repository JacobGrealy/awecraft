AC-0421 plan — orbital look: sheen, cloud flicker, flatness, held item

SPEC: TASKS.yaml AC-0421 · CONTINUITY: continuity.md

Goal

Make the orbital view read as a matte world with relief and its weather, and nothing else: remove the glossy sheen on the satellite body, stop the clouds flickering on the near face (without reintroducing the AC-0401 near-tier/body flicker), give the sampled surface colour height-based relief (the user's own fix direction), and suppress the held item in orbit. Every look change ships behind a switch with a conservative default; the two defect fixes (sheen term, cloud ordering) ship unswitched (the flicker is not a feature to toggle).

Defect findings + approach

#User reportWhat it isApproach
1“the low lod should not look this reflective: satellite lod” The body is a lit spatial shader (render_mode is not unshaded) with a default specular; the sun's DirectionalLight3D puts a specular sheen on it — the “glossy” cast. Confirm against the before-frame. Remove the specular contribution for this material (matte terrain). If the before-frame shows the sheen is the engine light rather than a shader term, make the body unlit by the engine light (the bake already carries the lambert — the header's stated intent). Measure: the sheen region's brightness before/after.
2“z fighting preventing the clouds from showing on the face near you in orbit view, they flash in and out but they always show around the edge of the limb” The cloud volume (fragment at the outer shell R+400, no depth write) and the body (transparent, depth_draw_always, at R+H−2, H≤384) are both at the planet centre → tied in the transparent sort. The depth test favours the cloud (R+400 is always in front of R+H≤R+384 at the nadir), so the contest is the draw order: where the body draws after the cloud it (op≈1) covers it on the near face; at the limb the cloud extends beyond the body's disc so it survives. Confirm the order + the flicker (the cloud drift clock advances per frame, so multi-frame snaps show it) against the before-frames. Establish the cloud-vs-body ordering by construction (radial, the AC-0401 2 m-inset precedent): the cloud is the weather layer above the ground, so from orbit it must draw on top of the body. Preserve the AC-0401 near-tier/body radial ordering (the 2 m body inset + the fog-wall partition) — do not move the body's inset or the near-tier discard. Evidence: a clouds-on/off pair + a near-face multi-frame set showing the cloud is stably present (not hidden, not flickering).
3“the satellite view reads too flat … alter the color of the sample based on the height … baked lighting, like what minecraft does with its maps” The body's sampled colour is the baked terrain albedo with no relief cue — a flat pastel map (the finding recorded twice). The texel's alpha channel already carries the height (A = round(H·255/HMAX), HMAX 384). Implement the user's exact suggestion: modulate the sampled colour by the terrain height (higher = lighter, the map-renderer / hypsometric look), behind u_relief / Settings relief_shading (default ON — the user asked; OFF = bit-identical). Conservative magnitude. Measure the disc's luminance spread (relief) before/after.
4The tan quad (held item) in the corner of the orbital frame The viewmodel (hand + item) is camera-attached with no_depth_test (always-on-top) and renders at every altitude — it is meaningless in orbit. (The prior triage closed a report as a stale SIZE complaint; the screenshot is the authority: the defect is that it renders at all at that altitude.) Suppress the viewmodel when the radial altitude is at/above the atmosphere height Aero.atmosphere_height() (the one-home “how far up is space”): hide hand_root in orbit, restore it on descent. Surface + sub-atmosphere flight unchanged. Verify: before-frame carries the block (via the harness FPV_ITEM instrument), after-frame is clean; a headless check that the viewmodel is visible at the surface and hidden in orbit.
