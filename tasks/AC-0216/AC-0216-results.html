What changed

The C++ world gen (gdext/src/gen.cpp) takes an optional 6th arg skip (generate_flat/slabs/resl, default 0 = the pre-AC-0216 full AC-0215 density field, bit-for-bit). When skip != 0 the 150-pt per-column density evaluation is skipped free:

  • the cave field (f_cave, 441 fbm3 lattice points) is not built;
  • the top-down d > 0 scan (~150.6 points/column, measured 963,591 over the 5×5 = 6,400 cols) does not run;
  • the column is solid exactly 0..H (the AC-0215 heightmap H from the coarse 3D surface field), he = H, no caves, no lava pockets — no hidden caves built.

Unchanged in skip mode (the visible surface is still correct): H (surface), the ore fields, biomes, the aquifer (water to Sea 126 where H < 126), trees and flowers (the margin column uses H2 exactly, no cave term — the f_cave field was not built). Cumulative counters (AweGen.skip_chunks_total() / skip_cols_total(), atomics, process-wide) let the harness report how many columns skipped.

The GDScript streaming system (world.gd, main thread only — the worker has no camera) computes the flag per chunk at enqueue time via _gen_skip_flag(cx,cz): OFFSCREEN INTERIOR = band_of(...) > 1 (the band-3 data-only collar/ring — outside the render circle, never meshed until the player approaches; band 0/1 are the visible bands and always get the full density field) AND the whole column AABB fully past the camera frustum expanded by FRUSTUM_CULL_MARGIN (32 m — the exact "culled" test of the AC-0109 manual cull pass, reusing _cull_frustum_planes/_cull_col_span). With no live camera (the headless build phase before the player spawns) a throwaway camera at the recenter point facing −Z (eye at the heightmap surface + 2) is used for the decision only. The flag rides on the threadgen args (new a[5]) and the recenter burst element (new e[7]) to generate_resl. A perf_gen_skip_enq counter tracks enqueue-time skips.

The density field stays exact for the visible bands: skip=0 (the default every band-0/1 chunk and every non-streamed caller uses) is bit-for-bit the AC-0215 field — proven both standalone (the 25 skip=0 chunk FNV hashes == the pristine-HEAD gen.cpp, 25/25) and in-game (the genhash gate below). The visible surface in a lazy chunk is H exactly (the heightmap), so no surface cave-wobble is introduced there; the lazy chunk is plain solid rock to the heightmap surface (the accepted "no hidden caves" trade — the caves materialize only where the full field runs, i.e. the bands the player can see).
