The remaining r=4 problem: the walk crosses a chunk line every ~1 s, but each crossing's 9-new-chunk wall takes ~4.9 s to fully build (gen + per-chunk standalone light + per-chunk concave collision set_faces at ~10-40ms+ each) — so the radius is perpetually ~35% unbuilt while the player walks (in_radius_built_max 57-64/81). Two of those costs are fixed in LATER children (AC-0077: bulk light + collision staging). THIS child attacks the ORDER of the work: (a) the player's view direction fills first (perceived pop-in drops even though total work is unchanged), and (b) work stops being spent on the TRAILING side (behind the player, hidden by fog) — hysteresis kills trailing meshes at r+1 and stops the drain from ever building behind where it's already walking. Expect: forward wall built before trailing wall on every crossing; in_radius_built_max climbs (forward sector solid); trailing-side memory down.

Requirements

  1. Look-priority + collision-need scoring (P1.1) — plan §3.1: entry score = manhattan distance + lookPenalty + collPenalty. lookPenalty = (1 − angleAlignment)·K, K ≈ 0.75·distScale (chunks within ±70° of player look direction sort first); angle = atan2 of (chunk center − player) vs player yaw. collPenalty: chunks at Chebyshev ≤ 1 always highest priority within their band (player can be there this second). The r+1 ring entries (data_only) sort behind everything in-radius (already true by kind — keep). In the band structure: distance bands stay distance-ordered (primary key); scoring orders WITHIN/across the near bands: recompute scores on recenter + throttled on look change (~2×/s — timestamp check, not every frame); the drain's unit pick = best-scored READY entry among the first few bands (e.g. scan bands 0..min(4,…) for the mq head + the data wave) — keep it O(small). At r=50 n≈13k: recenter re-score must stay O(n) (a single pass over new buckets) — recenter_ms < 100 at r=50 is a hard no-regression gate from AC-0076.
  2. Two-stage hysteresis unload (P1.2) — plan §3.1: at Chebyshev r+1 a chunk becomes a candidate: kill its mesh AND collision NOW (the memory win; data + edits stay) and drop its pending light/fluid/tex work; it becomes freed only at r+2 after 2 consecutive recenter events (count events, not time: ~1.1 s at sprint / 3.7 s at walk). Re-entering r clears candidate state instantly → flap = 0 by construction (assert it in the boundary harness at all r). On free clear ALL per-chunk maps: queue entries, queued_keys, light_pending/set and — new — fluid_dirty + tex_refresh. Flush/refresh loops skip candidate+freed chunks (extend the existing null-checks, world.gd flush region). Re-entry after candidate = rebuild (data survives; edits re-apply on mesh) — assert a round-trip: boundary harness walks away 2+ chunks past r+1 in +X then returns within r and the marker cell + a broken block near the start still hold their edited values (the existing marker covers the far round-trip; keep it). Candidate-state lives on the chunk node (e.g. chunk.candidate + a recenter-event counter in world.gd) — chunk.gd additions allowed (state fields only, no build-logic changes). Collision note (plan §6): candidates lose collision at r+1 — the player can never be at r+1 (16+ blocks away, fog edge); the boundary walk + G5 render are the gates. Do NOT extend hysteresis beyond r+2 (no free of in-radius or r+1 data without the 2-event rule).
