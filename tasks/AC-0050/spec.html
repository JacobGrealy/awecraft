AC-0050 - user bug: crafted items must land in the VISIBLE inventory when hotbar is full (never vanish)

Report (user 2026-08-20)

"when all quick use slots are full, items from crafting should go into your inventory, but they do not show in your inventory"

Root cause (AC-0049 research G1 - tasks/AC-0049/AC-0049-research.html - READ IT FIRST)

  • UI draws hotbar inv[0..8] + 27 storage slots mapped to inv[27+idx] = inv[27..53] (godot/ui/inventory.gd:272-273, 748-750), but inv is only 36 long (godot/player/player.gd:16) - storage rows 2-3 back never-initialized indices that grow on click via _inv_set (player.gd:912-915).
  • inv_add (player.gd:797-817) caps at 36 and scans from index 0: full hotbar + partial storage >27... crafted output silently lands in hidden inv[9..26], which the UI never draws. That is the "item vanishes" bug.
  • MC-exact target: the 36 inventory = 9 hotbar + 27 storage, ALL VISIBLE. One backing, no hidden reserve, no >36.
