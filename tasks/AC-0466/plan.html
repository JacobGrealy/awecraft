AC-0466 — an arm that walks the planet and says what happens at every seam

Coordinator contract. Medium builder applies it with tasks/templates/implement.md. Measurement ticket: add one arm, change no game behaviour. This exists because AC-0463 (remove the legacy lane split) is the largest change in the tree and would be unfalsifiable without it.

1. Goal

An arm that puts the player on a chart, crosses into a neighbouring chart, and reports per boundary: the face before/after, whether the chunk under the player has a mesh AND a collision body, the seam census fields, and the crossing frame/latency — with a negative control proving the arm can fail.

2. Why now (the order that has worked)

AC-0465 preceded AC-0459 (make the producer count knowable, then flip the format). AC-0460 measured the placement before flipping the default. The lane merge follows the same rule: build the instrument first, because two half-merged streaming policies produce exactly the quiet starvation this project keeps having to hunt.

4. Edit shape

  • Start on a chart using the same boundary positioning faceedit uses, and assert the anchor face is > 1 (fail loudly, never test the home pair by accident).
  • Cross by moving the player in steps along one direction until player_anchor()["face"] CHANGES. Record the face pair, the step count, and the crossing frame time. Assert the face actually changed — a walk that never leaves the chart is a failed arm, not a pass.
  • At the crossing (the first frame the face differs) and at the destination, record for the chunk under the player: mesh_built (it has a mesh instance), body_present (collision body, use the existing slab-body predicate, not a new one), and the seam census fields used at AC-0433/AC-0462 if they are cheap to read.
  • Coverage: cross at least TWO different boundaries (e.g. 5→4 and 11→10, or however the cube map actually adjoins them — derive adjacency from SphereMath, do not guess). If a full twelve-chart lap is too slow for the 600 s budget, do a documented subset and say so; report the wall time.
  • Negative control: the arm must be able to fail. Provide a mode (env flag) that skips the mesh/collision wait and assert the arm then reports body_present false or mesh_built false at a boundary — i.e. prove the check has teeth against the AC-0145 finding that an unchecked wait let the player free-fall 148 m through unbuilt ground.
  • RESULT keys: ok, crossings [{from_face, to_face, steps, frame_ms, mesh_built, body_present, recentered}], waivers, negctrl {ran, saw_unbuilt}, frames, ms, fails.

6. Acceptance

A green, falsifiable traversal arm on the current tree, which becomes the baseline every later lane-merge slice is measured against.

7. Risks

The walk may cross a boundary faster than the destination chunk streams, producing a genuine finding rather than an arm bug — report which it is by checking whether the chunk builds after a bounded wait. Do not widen the wait silently until it goes green.
