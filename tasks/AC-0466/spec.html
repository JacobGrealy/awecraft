Registry notes (verbatim, quoted):

1) User Story
Nothing visible changes yet, but this is the instrument that makes the rest of the one-world migration safe. Today no test walks the player across a chart boundary and checks that the world stays continuous — the existing crossface arm only walks the home pair. Without a test that crosses all twelve charts and reports what happens at each seam, any later attempt to merge the two streaming lanes could break the world quietly and nobody would know.

2) Technical Details
Filed from AC-0463's inventory (round 27): the lane merge is the largest change in the tree (world.gd is 15,507 lines / 373 functions; home lane = _process at 5854 + recenter 11733 + _make_chunk_node 11409; face lane = _face_stream 14996, FACE_STREAM_BUDGET_MS 14995, _ensure_face_chunk 15335, _star_faces 14570). The merge must NOT be attempted before an arm exists that can prove or refute it, which is the same order that worked for the keys (AC-0465 before AC-0459) and the placement (measure, then flip).
Scope: add ONE arm that puts the player on a chart, walks/flies it across at least one boundary into a neighbouring chart, and reports at every boundary: the anchor face before/after, whether the chunk under the player has a built mesh and collision body, the seam census fields, and the frame/latency at the crossing. Extend to all twelve charts or a documented subset if a full lap is too slow — state which and why.
Do NOT merge lanes here. Do NOT change placement, keys, streaming policy or light. The arm is the deliverable.
Verify: the arm is green on the current (pre-merge) tree, which is the baseline every later slice is measured against; genhash 25/25 unchanged (no world change); g0 clean; and the arm must FAIL if a boundary chunk has no mesh or no collision, proved by a deliberate negative control (e.g. skip the wait) so it cannot be a green test of nothing.
