AC-0482 — the aim box is wrong on every face but the home face

Coordinator contract. Medium builder applies it with tasks/templates/implement.md. Measure on at least two non-home faces, fix at the source.

1. What AC-0479 fixed, and what it left

AC-0479 found the aim box was placed position-only with an identity basis on the rigid per-column facet frame while the block mesh and collision both consume the per-vertex spherical warp. It corrected the home face's path — the box now sits at the cell's exact warped centre and is oriented by the warp Jacobian — and measured the improvement: corner tangential 37.1 → 10.9 mm at spawn, 296.1 → 15.5 mm far out, box-up 2.316° → 0.162°.

It did not exercise `face > 1`, where a CELL/FLAT UNIT MISMATCH remains. The flat frame and the per-face cell frame are different units, and the non-home path mixes them.

2. The seam is visible in the code already — start here

  • player.gd aim_hit() (:2786) carries a comment that is essentially the bug in words: "the ray runs in the local flat frame" and then "CELL frame (the in-plane axes per cell width) against the face block". It genuinely distinguishes a cell frame from the flat frame; the question is whether every downstream consumer does.
  • world.gd:16087 world_pos_of_flat(x,y,z) is a single generic SphereMath.flat_to_world(x, y, z, R) — it takes no face argument. But world.gd:16064/:16245/:16257 all branch on face == 0 or face == 1, so there is a face-dependent placement convention and the generic conversion cannot be right for all twelve unless the caller pre-maps into the canonical chart. Establish which faces the caller pre-maps for, and where the `face > 1` path diverges.
  • The world has NO HOME REGION by design — the user's standing requirement is that the experience be identical around the entire planet. So a per-face aim defect is precisely the asymmetry the objective forbids, and it hides from home-face testing.

3. What to do

  1. 3.1 MEASURE ON AT LEAST TWO NON-HOME FACES. Move the player to a non-home face and measure the box's misalignment exactly as AC-0479 did: corner tangential error, centre offset, and box-up angle vs block-up. Report the numbers per face. A cell-sized error (~16 m) or a whole-unit error will be obvious in millimetres and metres, so state the magnitude and its units.
  2. 3.2 FIND THE CONVERSION, do not compensate the symptom. Trace the aimed cell from aim_hit() to the placement (AC-0479's new code path) and identify where a cell-frame value is treated as flat-frame (or vice versa) on faces above 1. Fix it at that source. Do not add a per-face fudge offset at the call site.
  3. 3.3 PROVE ON AT LEAST TWO DIFFERENT FACES — a per-face bug can be right on one face and wrong on another, so one face is not proof.
  4. 3.4 REGRESSION-CHECK THE HOME FACE still measures as AC-0479 left it (corner ~10.9 mm at spawn, ~15.5 mm far out). Do not trade one face for another.

6. Acceptance

The mismatch measured as numbers on two or more non-home faces, the mixing conversion named and fixed at its source, both faces re-measured and materially reduced, and the home face's AC-0479 numbers intact.

7. Risks

(1) Testing one non-home face and declaring it fixed — per-face bugs can be face-specific. (2) A per-face fudge offset instead of fixing the conversion, which would break the next face someone adds. (3) Regressing the home face, which is the path AC-0479 just fixed and is the one most likely to be re-tested.
