4. What would fix it — and why nothing landed

The tested fix already exists: lod_avg_seam.gdshader (unshaded; mirrors the near tier's L on the band's own a-channel data), behind the Options switch "Distance terrain: match the near-tier light at the seam" (menu.gd:643-663, Settings "far_seam"), default OFF. AC-0446 measured it: AFTER boundary profile −3…−15 (the band falls inside the near tier's own texture range) — "no dark step; the boundary is now a texture/depth transition, not a shading step". The residual after parity is the 8×8 colour sampling + jitter — the representation limit, which the spec permits.
