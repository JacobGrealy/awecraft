01 Goal — one sentence + success gates

The world is contained to spherical planets that look spherical from the surface (curved horizon, planet limb, dropping terrain) but play byte-identical to flat Minecraft — walk/mine/build on a tangent plane — then fly seamlessly into space, hop between planets in-system, and later jump between solar systems/galaxies via a Mass-Relay network.

Must play flat on the ground

  • Minecraft controls/capsule/raycast unchanged — movement projected onto tangent plane so mining/step feels flat.
  • Eye +1.62, capsule r0.3/h1.8, jump 8.4, gravity 26 — all per-chunk unchanged.
  • But looks spherical: horizon visibly dips, distant chunks drop with curvature, towers/hills show limb.

Must feel like a solar system from space

  • Ground → air → orbit is continuous (no loading screen).
  • Multiple planets in one save; walk around any of them and you loop (true sphere).
  • Inter-planet flight inside a system; inter-system/galaxy = relay jump (instant load + cinematic).
Hard gate: surface play keeps genhash 25/25 parity + battery EXACT (player,interact,light,fluids,craft). Curvature is a placement + camera truth, not a block-size change — looks round, plays flat.

03 How we keep blocks uniform on a sphere — no shell doubling

Blocky Planet's shells (1 block → 4 above at each boundary) solve "thin near core, fat at surface" but violate your hard requirement. We drop them. Instead: the planet is a uniform-thickness spherical shell — only the outer H blocks are stored/playable. The interior is not subdivided finer.

Geometry

planet center C
radius R  ≈ 6 000–12 000 blocks
thickness H = 80 (now) → 384 (AC-0091)
surface = sphere radius R
bedrock floor = sphere radius R-H
playable = radial shell [R-H, R]
core inside R-H = empty / inaccessible
        (or single filler, not voxelized at 1 m)

Over 384 blocks of thickness, radial divergence from center is H/R ≈ 3–6% — negligible. Blocks can stay cubes.

Mapping that keeps 1 m cubes

  • Cube-sphere with pre-warp, no doubling. Take a cube at the origin, give each face an N×N grid where N = 2πR / 16. Pre-warp the flat grid to compensate normalization, then normalize() each vertex onto the sphere. With large R the area distortion after pre-warp is ~2–5% (vs 30–40% raw gnomonic) — invisible in 1 m voxels.
  • Each face's grid is rectangular → your chunk infra survives: a chunk is one 16×16 patch of that grid extruded radially by H. No 1→4 vertical splits, no sector-edge resolution mismatch.
  • Inside a chunk, blocks remain axis-aligned to the chunk's tangent plane. The chunk itself is rotated so its +Y = outward radial. Neighbor across a cube edge is a rotation (u↔v swap + flip) but same scale.

Why this is "plays flat, looks spherical"

Plays flat (physics guarantee)

  • At R=8000, tilt between adjacent chunks = atan(16/R) ≈ 0.11°. Inside one chunk gravity deviation <0.12° — capsule rotation imperceptible, step/slope/place feel flat.
  • Greedy meshing, box collision, and light BFS stay per-chunk at uniform scale — same thresholds, same shapes.
  • Controls are projected onto the tangent plane at the player's foot — no "walking uphill toward the pole."

Looks spherical (visual — now intentional, not hidden)

  • Over 50 chunks (≈800 m) horizon dip ≈ 5.7° — not fogged out. We show it: horizon line drops, distant terrain sinks, sky gradient wraps with planet curvature.
  • From a hill/tower at ~80–384 m height you see the limb/terminator and curvature — key "you are on a planet" moment without any physics cost.
  • We keep fog for far-chunk pop hiding but tune DayNight.fog_near/far so it fades with curvature falloff, not before it. Previously: fog_far = (r+1)*16*0.95 hid it; now: extend far to limb.

Uniform-grid also kills the "12 pentagonal defects" worry of icosahedral hex: cube-sphere defects are just the 8 cube corners where 3 faces meet — there we emit 3 quads meeting at a point (no 1→4 split, just a corner seam). Culling hides interior faces as today.

High-level intuition — blankets on a beach ball

Forget code — picture 6 square blankets draped over a beach ball (front/back/left/right/top/bottom). Each blanket is a normal flat Minecraft grid (1 m squares, same 16×16 chunks you have today) gently bent to the ball. That's the planet.

  • Middle of a blanket: flat MC — you can't tell you're on a ball.
  • Where 2 blankets meet (edge): two sheets taped edge-to-edge — like a normal chunk border, just rotated. You walk and build right across it.
  • Where 3 blankets meet (corner): 3 corners touch one point. On a bathroom floor 4 tiles meet (+); here 3 meet (Y). The tops are flush so your feet feel flat.
  • The gap: under those 3 tops, their side walls leave a tiny triangular gap — a few centimeters at R=8000, completely below the ground you walk on. We drop an invisible foundation stone in it (same as bedrock today) so the game never thinks there's a hole or light leak. You never see it, never mine it.
  • Result: plays flat (your blanket is flat where you stand), looks round (distant blankets visibly curve and the horizon dips ~5.7° over 50 chunks).

Building up — why towers diverge and how we keep them uniform

Side view — columns spread with height

  • R=4000 avg (per-planet R∈[2000,8000]): ground gap = 0, at +384 m gap ≈ 384/R ≈ 9.6% → 9.6 cm / block at 4000 (≈4.8 cm at 8000, ≈19 cm at 2000 clamp).
  • Up to 100 m it's ~2.4% at 4000 — still sub-pixel in grout.
  • Columns lean 0.23° / 16 m at 4000 — still flat under feet, just `2×` the hairline.

Digging down — the mirror (overlap, not gap)

  • Mirror: at -384 m columns would need to be 9.6% narrower at R=4000 — we keep 1 m, so they overlap ~9.6 cm / block (vs 4.8 cm at 8000).
  • Horizon (realistic culling, locked): ground horizon from h=1.62 m at R=4000 is d≈√(2·R·h) ≈ 114 m (~7 chunks). A 30-block tower's top horizon is √(2·R·30)≈490 m → visible out to 114+490≈604 m (~37 chunks). Taller = farther, like ships at sea. Hill 50 m → 632 m, tower 384 m → 1.7 km.
  • Overlap is between side faces underground — interior faces are culled, you never see Z-fighting.
  • Collision stays 1 m cubes — overlap makes deep tunnels slightly tighter by centimeters, not blocks.
Symmetric fix: up = gap (filled with hidden grout), down = overlap (hidden by culling). Both keep cubes uniform and are invisible to play — same filler idea as the corner Y.

How we keep it uniform (no wider blocks) — up

  • Keep cubes perfect — collision stays 1×1×1 cubes, greedy thresholds unchanged.
  • The divergence is a hairline *between* columns, not inside a block. At ground you see no crack; at 384 m it's a `5 cm` line hidden in side-face grout.
  • For a tall wall the top course has that `0.8 m` spread across 16 columns — we seal it with the same hidden filler as the corner `Y` (never exposed, never minable).
  • Alternative (rejected): taper blocks `1.00 → 1.05 m` would close it perfectly but breaks your uniform 1 m gate and complicates Data.
What you feel building up: place a tower 384 high — it stays straight under your feet, no widening. Look down from the top and the horizon still dips `~5.7°` (plays flat, looks spherical holds at any height).

04 Spherical options re-scored after your Picks

B1 — Lat-lon / equirectangular — rejected

Single rectangle draped on sphere. Polar singularities → many 1 m blocks squeezed into wedges at poles, chunk topology degenerate. Cannot keep uniform grid without extreme waste at poles. Keep only as a skybox panorama projection, not as playable geometry.

B2 — Cube-sphere / quad-sphere — uniform shell — chosen for planets

Why it wins now

  • Only approach that keeps rectangular 16×16 chunks + uniform 1 m cubes + modest distortion (pre-warped).
  • Proven for voxels (Blocky Planet, Outerra, NMS far-field). Without shell doubling it is exactly your constraint.
  • Each face is a quadtree — streaming/recenter/band-buckets map directly (just 6 faces).

Remaining cost (no doubling)

  • Sector-edge neighbor = rotation+flip (u↔v). Not free, but constant cost and testable headless.
  • 3D noise or per-face 2D heightmap with edge stitch (choose 2D for P1 to keep genhash cheap, 3D later for caves that wrap poles seam-free).
  • Physics bodies are rotated per chunk — Godot StaticBody3D transform handles this; no per-block warp.

B3 — Icosahedral / geodesic / hex — deferred

Most uniform area, but faces are triangles/hexes — breaks your square chunks, square textures, and greedy quads. Would require rewriting chunk.gd meshing + atlas. Not justified when cube-sphere already hits <5% distortion at R≥6000 with your uniform-shell trick.

B4 — Visual sphere + flat playable (NMS hybrid) — now a visual amplifier, not a hide

With "plays flat, looks spherical" this becomes a visual LOD helper: at orbit distance you see a textured sphere mesh that already shows curvature; on approach you stream the real uniform-shell chunks that also show curvature. The trick is no longer hiding flatness — it's keeping distant chunks as an impostor so you see the whole planet silhouette without streaming 3000 equatorial chunks. Playable layer is still B2.

LOD swap — impostor ↔ chunks hidden in atmosphere (locked)

  • >2000 m: impostor sphere only — 6×512² texture from same fbm as chunks, no voxel streaming. Limb is impostor.
  • 2000→500 m — the hide: both render — impostor `1–2 m` below real terrain (no Z-fight). Real chunks cover the nadir patch (`~400`); limb stays impostor. Atmosphere haze (blue shift + desat) already covers the mid-distance, so color mismatch is masked. Impostor alpha smoothstep(500,2000, alt) with dither dissolves, not pops.
  • <500 m: impostor alpha 0 — chunks only. Horizon beyond `~114 m` is sphere-culling, not fog.
  • Impostor baked once per planet seed — same palette as chunks, so no flash. Scale-invariant: cost `~1.5M` samples whether R=2000 or 8000.

06 Solar system → galaxy via Mass Relay — your transit fantasy, scoped

What we ship when

  • P1 — One planet + space: single sphere radius R, space around it is just skybox + starfield (AC-0035) + free flight. Validates uniform-shell math + gravity + origin rebase.
  • P2 — Solar system: 3–6 planets + sun, each planet is its own uniform-shell world with its own origin. Inter-planetary flight is manual (sublight). Distances are compressed (e.g. 50k–200k blocks between planets) so 5-minute flight is enough — or we keep AU-scale and require warp.
  • P3 — Galaxy via relay: thousands of systems exist as data (seed + orbit), but you cannot fly there. To go inter-system / inter-galaxy you must build/find a Mass Relay — an activatable structure that instant-jumps the ship (scene/origin swap + cinematic).

Mass Relay — gameplay sketch (Mass Effect–style)

  • Build / discover: relay is a craftable megastructure (costly — late game) or a found derelict you repair (ore + crafted parts). Tied to Data.items + new structures table.
  • Pairing: relays are paired or networked (primary relay → any secondary in range). UI picks destination system/galaxy from discovered relay network.
  • Activation: fuel/charge + alignment check, then ship → relay beam → jump (screen warp, unload old planet chunks, load new system). No sublight void — we skip the empty light-years.
  • Why this helps tech: galaxy is data-only until you jump; only current system streams chunks. Float-precision stays per-system; save is system-local.

07 Gravity & player — "Y is not up"

On any planet surface

  • up = normalize(player_pos - planet_center), gravity = -up * 26.
  • Move: project WASD onto tangent plane (basis = tangent/bitangent), then move_and_slide with rotated capsule — capsule tilt <0.12°/chunk, so no uphill feel.
  • Mine/place ray: DDA in world space — no +Y assumption (already true for core/math.gd).
  • Visual: horizon dip is now a feature — once chunks sit on sphere, distant chunks naturally drop (≈5.7° over 50 chunks). We extend fog/sky to the limb instead of hiding it (see §3 "Looks spherical").

Air → space → other planet

  • Altitude a = |pos-C| - R. Below ~400 m: surface gravity + step. Above: blend to free 6-DOF (no ground snap, thrust).
  • Atmosphere entry on another planet: re-orient capsule to new planet's up before re-enabling step.
  • HUD/markers: per-planet tangent space (the GDC "marker over building on another planet" problem) — affects later quest/compass, not P1.
sphere up:       up = normalize(pos - C)
tangent basis:   t = normalize(cross(up, north)), b = cross(up, t)
altitude:        alt = length(pos - C) - R
surface check:   on_ground = alt <= H_ground_epsilon  (~0.3)

08 World-gen — keep Minecraft biomes without shell tax

P1 — per-face 2D heightmap (cheapest, genhash-friendly)

  • Keep today's generator.gd fbm3/biome thresholds verbatim. For each cube face, sample the same 2D fbm at face-local (u,v) with seamless edge blending (lerp across 4-texel border).
  • Height = radial displacement R + h(u,v). Caves = 3D noise in local space extruded radially.
  • Genhash stays deterministic per seed; each face reuses one noise tiling → cheap, no polar seam.
  • Limit: caves that cross a cube edge need cross-face stitch — acceptable for P1 to have slightly sparser caves at the 12 edges.

Later — 3D noise on sphere (seam-free)

  • Sample FBM at p = normalize(cube_pos) * scale for any radius — no seams, no per-face blending.
  • Better for true spherical caves/ores, but ~2–3× cost. Pair with AC-0125 GDExt hotloops if walk p95 regresses.
  • Transition is data-compatible: same block ids, just different noise domain. Keep uniform shell thickness H so no 1→4 change needed regardless.
AC-0091 ordering: Y=-64→319 (384 tall) lands before any spherical work. Shell thickness H is that height. Planet R is independent — pick 8000–12000 for v1 (curvature visible at distance but still <0.12°/chunk locally, so it reads as a planet without breaking step physics).

09 Floating origin & precision — per-planet, not per-galaxy

float32 loses ~1 m past 10k and ~1 km past 1M. A planet at R=8000 already pushes the origin if you store absolute solar-system coords.

Scheme — per-planet local space

SpaceOriginWhat's there
Planet-localPlanet center C = (0,0,0) while landedAll chunks + player in [R-H,R] shell — fits in ~12k extent → float32 safe.
System spaceStar at (0,0,0) while in flightPlanet impostors + ship; planet chunks inactive.
Relay jumpSwap system (unload old, load new)Galaxy = relay graph, not continuous space.

Rebase when |player| > 20k inside planet-local, or on every relay jump. Translate all chunk nodes by -player_pos (one world.transform.origin shift) — chunk keys unchanged (they're face+cx+cz).

Implementation hook

core/origin_shift.gd
  func world_to_render(p: Vector3) -> Vector3
  func rebase_if_needed(player_pos)
  signal rebased(offset: Vector3)  # world.gd re-keys physics
  • Chunks stored by logical key (face,cx,cz), rendered at render_pos = logical_pos - origin_offset.
  • Save file stores logical key + seed + relay graph — never the render offset.
  • Galaxy-scale needs no 64-bit Vector3: each system stores planet orbits as doubles on disk, but live simulation is always planet-local or system-local float.

11 Rings — deferred, but how they'd return

When you want a Halo ring later, it reuses almost all of P1a: a ring is a torus with major radius R_major ≫ R_minor where R_minor ≈ R_planet. Gravity = toward ring axis, blocks still uniform.
