AC-0413 plan — the cloud deck flattens to a tinted ring at distance

Goal

Re-confirm the D-26-2 finding (deck = rim ring + tint, no internal depth, at 1600–2600 m) on the current tree first — the frames that recorded it came from a tree whose satellite body had inverted mesh winding, so the planet beneath the clouds was a flat air-coloured wash (that was AC-0411, fixed in commit 2196943). If the finding no longer reproduces, report that the ticket shrinks or disappears — do not manufacture a fix. If it does reproduce, test the proposed cause (fixed 24 view steps through a chord that grows with distance) and, only then, change the sampling with a measured cost. The near field (120 m / inside the annulus) must not regress: that volume read is user-verified by eye.

Re-confirmation design (phase 1, before any change)

  • Frames (current tree, standing user config R24/F83, T=0.5 frozen, CLOUD_T0=411200, seed 44, 1280×720 Forward+/lavapipe, planet preset, 1200-frame drain + 600 mid-drain step): b-2600.on, b-2600.off (AWECRAFT_CLOUDS=0), b-1600.on, b-1600.off, b-200.on (inside the annulus, the near-field reference frame).
  • Instrument: the AC-0384 analyze2.py band statistics plus the DECK MASK (pixels where ON-minus-OFF max-channel diff ≥ 8 = the layer’s own pixels; the ON luminance std inside that mask = the layer’s brightness spread, the same concept as the 700 m lower_mid lum_std that ran 16.9 / 32.4 / 68.9 / 44.9). The disc-centre ON-vs-OFF delta separately tests the “flat film over the disc” mechanism.
  • Cause test: (a) the r19/r21 finding frames vs the AC-0411 after frames attribute the disc wash to the body (r19: wash byte-identical clouds on/off, gone with SAT_BODY=0) — so the recorded “tinted ring” reading is re-measured on the fixed tree; (b) the step-density hypothesis is checked against the measured deck-mask spread at the shipped 24 steps — if the ring/masses already resolve at altitude with a spread comparable to the 700 m instrument values, step count is not the cause and no sampling change is owed.

Sampling change cost (stated up front, the ticket’s constraint)

Standing cost: the volume render walls run ~823–976 s against the 1200 s budget (AC-0407). The view march is ~430 noise taps worst-case per pixel (2 seg × 24 × (3 view + 5 light + 1 edge) octaves); the altitude frames (1600/2600) pay the long limb chords, the near frames pay full-sky chords. If a change is needed it trades step count at altitude only (the near field keeps 24 steps exactly — that is the user-verified volume), estimated +5–10 % wall on the altitude frames (more view steps, same taps/step), which stays inside the 1200 s budget with ≥ 15 % headroom; if the budget is breached the trade is the light-march tap again (u_vol_lsteps 5 → 4, the reach is already > 2× the layer), not the view-step structure.
