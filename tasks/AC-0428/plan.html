Defect findings (test-first results)

#User reportWhat it is (measured)Approach
1"sky still has a horizon … part of the sky box" Separate from #3 (hypothesis falsified). The line is the aero sky gradient's own below-branch seam: at the local-horizon elevation the thinning ramp has a local extremum that renders as a ~20-luminance step across the frame (h800-horizon-base: rows 360-380). The AC-0422 fix removed the branch-switch edge; this is the residual seam of the ramp itself. aero_sky_gradient.gdshader: smooth the thinning ramp at the seam (blend the below/above branches through a wider band centred on the local horizon so the derivative has no extremum); keep the u_horizon A/B. Measure: luminance profile down the frame, step at the seam before/after (target ≤ a few units, vs 20 before).
2"top of the sky turns into space faster … entire sky should transition at same rate" The sky's horizon→zenith blend is a power/cosine of elevation, so the rate of change is steepest near the zenith (the deep-blue cap "arrives" faster overhead). Measured on the base frame: the luminance fall-off is convex (fast at top, slow near horizon). aero_sky_gradient.gdshader: re-parameterize the blend to be (close to) linear in elevation so the whole sky transitions at a uniform rate; keep the endpoints (zenith colour, horizon colour, space below) untouched. Measure: luminance profile down the frame before/after — the profile should be a straight line between the horizon and zenith anchors (rate variance across the profile drops; report max deviation from the best line).
3"above clouds I can see all the clouds on the other side of the planet through the satellite lod … should be opaque" — most serious Two mechanisms, both measured. (i) Primary, user-visible: the cloud volume's far annulus (seg2) draws the far-side cloud sheet across the planet — CLOUDS=0 removes the see-through entirely (nadir900-clouds0 vs base). (ii) The body "hole": NOT missing geometry, NOT culling, NOT a subview, NOT renderer-specific (GL-compat reproduces it) — it is the body shader's fragment-stage world reconstruction. p_w = (INV_VIEW_MATRIX * vec4(VERTEX,1)).xyz (AC-0384) degenerates to a constant across the draw in 4.7.1 (the fragment-stage VERTEX builtin is not the interpolated fragment position), so cosv collapsed to a dot against a fixed direction: ≈0.3 constant over the world-+Z half of the frame (flat pale left half of nadir900) vs 0.8-1.0 on the -Z half. The body IS rasterized across the whole frame (solid-red test: 14,249/14,400 samples exactly (255,0,0)) — the flat region is the body hazed to u_air by the broken cosv, and where the haz model is broken the disc no longer reads as an opaque limb. Mesh data + all 12 face transforms verified perfect in-engine (pure translation (0,-4000,0), identity basis). (i) cloud_layer.gdshader: silhouette gate on the far annulus — skip seg2 fragments whose ray misses the planet (impact parameter b = length(cross(p_c, rd)) > body radius + margin), so the far-side sheet can never draw across the disc. (ii) satellite_body.gdshader: compute the world position in the vertex stage (v_world = MODEL_MATRIX * VERTEX, the repo pattern from cloud_layer/lod_avg) and the exact radial normal (v_nrm = normalize(VERTEX)), passed as varyings; fragment uses them for d/op/haz/cosv/sun-lambert. VERIFIED: in-shader test abs(length(v_world − centre) − 4140) < 50 true at every fragment; fixed frame shows terrain across the whole disc with a symmetric radial limb haze (nadir900-fixed.png).
4cloud cover should vary across the world — "some kind of noise … where groups of clouds are" The cloud field is uniform across the globe (every sector at the same cover). New low-frequency gate in planetary space: a low-freq noise field sampled on the body that decides whether a region has weather at all, multiplying the existing local field (structure inside cloudy regions is unchanged). Ships behind a switch defaulting OFF (conservative): cloud_gate in settings.gd + menu row + AWECRAFT_CLOUDGATE env (the cloud_deck/cloud_volume pattern). Measure: fraction of surface under cloud before/after (gate on) at two altitudes; the after must show clear regions (fraction < before, with spatial variance).
