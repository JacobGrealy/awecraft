AC-0429 plan — remove the lower boundary as a concept (the skybox has no bottom edge)

Goal

Below the local horizon the sky is space — the same black, the same stars. The only thing that ever appears across a frame is the planet and its air. Not a smoother version of the old look: the anchor, the band, the blend at the bottom of the sky are removed. AC-0428 measured the step at the local horizon to 0.00 by making the two sides of the box the same colour (u_air) — the user is right that the box is still there: a flat pale expanse from the local horizon down to the planet's limb, with a structure computed and smeared at its top. The fix must make the below-horizon region space exactly (at the user's altitude) with nothing else of the sky model touching that region.

What draws or implies a lower boundary (the survey)

All five live in one place — sky_on() in godot/core/aero_sky_gradient.gdshader (the default u_sky_alt >= 0.5 path). One root cause: the model treats "below the local horizon (hl < 0) or under the R+SEA disc" as a region with a DIFFERENT STATE — an air floor — instead of letting the sky be the space background everywhere and the planet (with its air) be the only thing in it.

#ThingWhere (sky_on, line refs pre-change)What it does
1The below-branch air floor (the main body) belowc = mix(mix(space_color, u_air, 1.0 - w), u_air, u_horizon) Below hl = 0 the sky paints the single-source AIR colour — a flat pale band (u_air ≈ [208,241,251] at noon). That flat expanse terminated at the local-horizon plane IS the skybox's bottom face the user sees. At w = 1 (full space) it is STILL pale (AC-0422's after-1300 frame: row 690 = 250.32 "air below").
2The gradient's horizon anchor horizon_eff = mix(horizon_color, u_air, w * u_horizon) (AC-0428 item 1) The upper gradient's lower edge is anchored to the air colour as the A window opens — the top half of the same box, meeting the floor at the local horizon. AC-0428's "step 0.21 → 0.00" made the two halves the same colour; the boundary as a structure remained.
3The branch-switch band below_soft = max(under_disc, smoothstep(0.0, 0.03, -hl)) (AC-0422) A ~2° blend band straddling the local horizon — the band where the two halves of the box meet.
4The horizon haze band hz = exp(-abs(hl)*9.0) * haze_amount * (1.0 - w) A bright band centred ON the local horizon, from both sides — a fake-horizon line that keeps 20% of its weight at the user's w = 0.8.
5The thinning weight's horizon exemption thin = mix(mix(w*w, w, u), w*u, u_horizon) (AC-0422 ·u / AC-0428) The local horizon is where the sky darkens LAST (weight 0 at hl = 0, w at the zenith) — the lower edge of the space transition, the "atmosphere seen edge-on" band.

Checked and NOT lower-boundary things (untouched): the limb term (the normalized air column through the A shell — the planet's OWN air, additive, exactly 0 above the horizon from outside: it becomes the ONLY boundary, which is what the user wants); the star field (full sphere, no bottom face, occluded only by the planet disc at R+SEA); the AeroWash quad (uniform screen tint ×(1−S), no edge); the satellite body + cloud shells (the planet and its air); the env fog / fog wall (terrain fading to u_air — the planet's surface, not the sky's bottom); the sky_off path (the sky's OFF state — must stay byte-identical, untouched); and the under_disc case (camera inside the R+SEA disc): there w ≡ 0 (alt < 126 m < the 300 m window floor), so the change below is provably a no-op.

Why this is "no line, no band" and not another blend

  • At the user's frame (w = 0.8): ws = 1 → the below-branch is space_color EXACTLY (flat black + stars) and the anchor is space_color exactly, so above the horizon the sky starts AT space and the local horizon carries no colour change at all (seam = 0, not ≤ 0.03-degree-smeared).
  • At intermediate w (the 300–630 m climb): the below-branch and the anchor move from u_air/horizon_color to space with the SAME ws, so the two halves stay matched at the boundary (|seam| ≤ 15/255·(1−ws), the same order as the pre-existing ground-level seam, 0 once ws = 1).
  • At the ground (w = 0): ws = 0 → every expression reduces to the pre-ticket value (bit-identical daytime sky + the AC-0384 r8 fog seam).
  • What remains in the lower frame: the planet's disc, its u_air rim haze and the sky's limb air-column glow — "the planet and its air". From above the planet (limb aim, w = 1): the whole dome is space + stars; the limb is the only boundary.
