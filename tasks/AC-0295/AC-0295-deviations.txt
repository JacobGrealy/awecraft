   values; R16 → R4 exercises the same mechanism (596 orphans at R16 is a
   large-enough population to make the standing-vs-travelling diagnosis

2. **Orphan definition.** Orphan = a resident chunk (present in
   `world.chunks`) that the world's OWN eviction test would free right
   now: the `_resident_sweep` home pass's `two_out` against the CURRENT
   window (not in the circle ring AND taxi·16 m > the evict window, read
   live from the same helpers the recenter reads), plus the face lane's
   `resident_of` negative set. This is NOT "outside the render circle":
   post-migration, home residency is windowed by the SIM band (the
   (b1_eff+2)·16 m evict window), so chunks between the new render circle
   and that window are kept BY DESIGN (the sim band + the standing keep
   policy). The ticket's "outside the new smaller radius" reads as
   "beyond what the world would still keep" under the current residency
   model — the ticket text itself is flagged pre-migration.
