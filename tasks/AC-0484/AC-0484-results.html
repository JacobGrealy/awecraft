Disk writes are periodic (the save lane), bounded by ≈2 KB/chunk.

Reading the separation: at the per-chunk scale the whole pipeline is ≈ 5.3 ms generation + 3.3 ms meshing (incl. its light pull) + 2.4 ms light seed + (save-encode + data-lane pull only when that lane runs) + ≤1 ms queue wait. Generation is the LARGEST single bucket and its top-3 stages (field 36% / scan 31% / fill 22% = 88% of generation) are the honest targets for the series — but meshing + the data-lane pull are the same order of magnitude, and the queue wait is a non-issue at r4 with 2 workers.
