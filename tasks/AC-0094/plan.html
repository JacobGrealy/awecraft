AC-0094 — do drops pop, fall, land and spin in place today?

Coordinator contract. Medium builder applies it with tasks/templates/implement.md. Verify first, fix only what is measured. The ticket was filed before two things landed that may already have delivered it, so re-measuring is cheaper than re-building.

1. Why this is a verification job first

  • The ticket (filed 2026-09-01) says drops "spawn/hover/spin-orbit" and asks for: spawn at the broken block, slight sideways velocity, gravity, then in-place spin on landing.
  • AC-0093 already fixed the orbit: entities/drop.gd:25-35 explains the pivot was the bug (the mesh origin was the box's corner, so a local Y-spin orbited the geometry at r≈0.212) and reparents the mesh under a centre pivot so the spin is truly in place.
  • The pop and gravity appear to already be there: drop.gd:16 sets _vel = Vector3((randf()-0.5)*3.0, 3.0 + randf()*2.0, (randf()-0.5)*3.0) — lateral ±1.5 plus an upward 3–5 — and drop.gd:102-110 integrates position += _vel * dt and snaps to the ground top on contact before settling.
  • The world has changed enormously since either was written (1 m cubic grid, the warp, the sphere-aware _ground_frame path at drop.gd:47-73), so "the code looks right" is not evidence that it behaves right in game.

2. What to establish

  • 2.1 Does it actually happen? Break a wood block in a running game and observe: does the drop spawn at the block, move sideways while falling, stop lateral motion at ground contact, and then spin without orbiting? A render sequence is the evidence — a still cannot show motion, so use several frames or an arm that samples positions over time.
  • 2.2 If it is correct already, close this ticket on that evidence and say plainly that AC-0093 plus the existing velocity code delivered it. That is a legitimate and useful outcome — do not invent a fix to look busy.
  • 2.3 If it is NOT correct, say precisely which of the four behaviours fails (spawn position, lateral pop, gravity/land, in-place spin) and fix only that.
