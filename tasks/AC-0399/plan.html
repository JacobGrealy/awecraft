AC-0399 plan — streaming-fill-follows-the-view

Goal

Make the streaming build order follow the camera's view while the player is in the flight band, so the arc being looked at fills first on takeoff — without changing the ground fill order, the streaming architecture, or generation (genhash must stay 25/25).

Current fill ordering (verified in code, not assumed)

  • ONE scheduler: the _drain_build_queue steady pass (world.gd, AC-0335 unification). The pick is argmin _grid_score(e) = taxi*10000 + layer: taxi = |dx|+|dz| chunks from the live recenter anchor (the player's FLAT column — a pure function of x/z, altitude-independent; last_pcx/last_pcz), layer = Y-distance rank from the player's slab. The score is already a float; ties break by scan order.
  • No view dependence at all: AC-0250 removed the old look bias ("the look no longer affects the order at all" — the _pool_key comment, world.gd:533). The frontier therefore grows as a SYMMETRIC taxi diamond, at the slower of the TG data-landing rate and the dispatch pace (3.5 ms/unit wall + 8/frame cap; ~3 frontier columns/s measured in the 5 FPS render path).
  • The data (gen) pass uses the same score (_pick_data_cached), so gen and build share the order.
  • Why it fails at altitude: the planet preset at 2600 m looks at the tangent limb; only the ~75–110° azimuth wedge around the view direction of the annulus (flat 128–384 m) is in frame. ~3/4 of the annulus fill work goes to sectors BEHIND the camera → round 4's frontier 256 m @180 s / ~365 m @360 s, while at 120 m the full annulus completes by 180 s (the annulus is the near part of the frame there).

Change (bounded — no architecture rewrite)

  • godot/player/player.gd: cache the EXISTING AC-0145 band (smoothstep(500, 2000, radial altitude), already computed per physics step) in last_flight_band + public getter flight_band(). One home for the number: the player owns it, the world reads it.
  • godot/world/world.gd:
    • _view_sample() once per drain frame: the camera's view direction in the FLAT xz plane (the player's aim_dir() projected through the recenter-anchor column's sphere basis — the same transform the player's _flat_ray uses; the basis is cached per anchor column, so the per-frame cost is one basis multiply) × band × vbias_w (default 0.7) → _view_lead; quantized (8-sector compass + band level) for the pool key. On the ground (band 0) the lead is exactly ZERO.
    • _grid_score: when the lead is active and the column is a DRAW tier (tier ≤ 3), effective taxi = taxi × (1 - max(0, cos(column dir, view dir)) × |lead|) — a column directly in the view loses up to 70% of its effective distance; a column 90°+ behind is untouched. Tier 4 (data-only, past the render edge — invisible) is NOT biased, so the outer world keeps its current order. Lead inactive → the EXACT pre-AC-0399 score (int-identical floats).
    • _pool_key: the two quantized view fields join the key (constant 0/0 on the ground — ground cache behaviour unchanged). A rotation crossing a compass sector or a band level is the only look change that triggers a rescan (the AC-0250 removal was the CONTINUOUS look rescore storm; quantization keeps the debounce).
    • Env knobs (harness preload, the AWECRAFT_DRAIN_MS pattern): AWECRAFT_VBIAS_W (0 disables = the pre-AC-0399 order — the "before" runs), AWECRAFT_VBIAS_FLIP=1 (the lead points 180° away — the negative test).
  • godot/scenes/harness.gd (the planet preset): ADDITIVE, env-gated AWECRAFT_SNAP_STEPS=300,900,1800 — snapshot at each cumulative drain frame (the 60/180/360 s of the acceptance at the ~5 FPS render path), one process instead of three. Default (env unset) is the current single-snapshot behaviour, byte-identical. Plus two stand-in-observer fixes the bias needs from the preset (both additive to the preset only): the frozen player's physics-step band update never runs (set_physics_process(false)) so the preset calls the new player.sync_flight_band() after its out-of-band teleport; and the fresh player's identity basis would aim down world -Z ~90° off the tangent limb, so the preset orients the stand-in where the preset camera looks (player.look_at — in the real game the camera rides the player's basis, so aim IS the view).
  • godot/ARCHITECTURE.md: the §6 build-order bullet updates (AC-0313 "(taxi, layer)" → AC-0399: view-weighted effective taxi in the flight band; the ground order and the architecture are otherwise unchanged).

Expected (estimates — the measurement is the acceptance)

2600 m: the in-frame wedge of the annulus (316 of 1056 columns) builds right after the inner footing (~145 cols) → visible arc 100% at ~130–170 s (before: ~376 s). 120 m: band = 0 → byte-identical order → the full-annulus-by-180 s ground behaviour is preserved by construction (measured as well). 1600 m: band 0.75 → large; 700 m: band 0.05 → small; 300 m: band 0 → equal. If the measured gain at 2600 m is small, the honest answer to report is the RATE number (~3 cols/s at the frontier on the render path; ~16/s pace-limited on a 60 FPS box) — the scope fence says the budget question may be the real limit.
