Goal

Set the night ambient floor in the rendered light to 0.20 of full sunlight (3.0/15 on the 0–15 scale) so a sealed, sourceless cave is clearly moonlit at midnight instead of pitch black. The floor is the constant term of the L formula in the four chunk_lit_*.gdshader (the AC-0128 structure): L = 0.20 + 0.80 * max(u_day * sky, blk). The constant and gain MUST sum to 1.0 so full sunlight (max = 1) stays EXACTLY 1.0 — noon full-sky cells are untouched, and the eff-0 floor is 0.20 day AND night (the floor is a constant in the existing structure; no day/night split is added).
