AC-0402 plan — planet-grey-at-some-times-and-sky-not-altitude-aware

Goal

Make the satellite disc show saturated terrain at every time of day (D5) and make the sky thinning-to-space respond to altitude over a long climb (D4), each measured across the required sweeps with renders, each look change behind a default-ON switch whose OFF state short-circuits before the work.

Diagnosis (from code reading + the existing captures; renders confirm)

D5. The body's day/night re-weight is albedo × (0.18 + 0.82·day(t)·sun_l) (satellite_body.gd:599-601 + the shader line 154). At T0.5 the disc-centre factor is exactly 1.0 (sun_l clamps to 1 at the nadir) — the body is the FULL baked terrain at noon, so the grey is NOT the body's material or day-night treatment. It is not cause #1 (the uncompiled shader drew a 255-white Lambert ball; the disc centre is 184/221 grey, and that defect is fixed) and not cause #2 (the fog term repainted the disc with the fog colour (177,240,255)-class cyan, also fixed). The grey is a third, independent cause: the cloud deck's overcast phase — cloud_amount = 0.30 + 0.45·day (aero.gd:193) makes the deck 2.5× denser by day, and the pre-AC-0401 deck (u_boost 2.2, u_under 0.70) then covers the disc with an opaque neutral veil (measured: disc centre (221,221,221), sat 0, in the r9-character frame ac0401-deckoff-2600.png at the same T0.5 weather state, vs (144,209,221) sat 77 with the AC-0401 deck default ON). The before sweep (T0 0.0/0.25/0.5/0.75) + the T0.5 clouds-OFF body-only render confirm which side owns the grey on the current tree.

D4. The sky's space blend uses the physics flight-band S (smoothstep(500, 2000, alt_rad), main.gd:1325), which completes by 1600 m (S=0.92; the 141 m spawn offset puts the 700/1600 m frames at alt_rad 841/1741) — "space effectively reached by 1600 m", no mid-state on the ladder, and the gradient shape reads state-independent. The fix gives the background blend its own, stretched window (start = BAND_WALK_MAX, end = BAND_FLY_MIN×1.6 = 3200 m alt_rad, read off the live player — no duplicated constants): the blue horizon thins gradually over the whole climb (S_sky ≈ 0.04/0.44/0.92 at the 700/1600/2600 m frames), while the limb-glow gate, the star opacity and the sun-glow fade keep riding the physics-band S untouched (the AC-0386/AC-0401 contracts).

Risks

  • Render cost: ~13 R24 snapshot runs × ~4-7 min (the user-config ladder streams a 24-chunk world at ~5 FPS). Mitigation: sequential resumable scripts, 1400 s cap per run, memory cap 12 GB, one godot at a time.
  • D5 may already pass (AC-0401's deck re-characterisation is default ON): then D5 ships as a finding + measurements, no deck change — the ticket says that is the valuable outcome.
  • The stretched window vs AC-0386's "one number, one home": the physics band stays the home for the gate/stars/glow; the background blend gets a documented sky-specific derivation from the same live constants. Recorded as a deliberate deviation in the results.
  • Parallel session WIP (uncommitted main.gd/game.gd/drop.gd/debug.gd hunks + the AC-0401 leftovers): the fence files are not touched; the measured tree is HEAD + those uncommitted hunks (stated in the results).
