Deviations / deliberate non-actions

The residual dense-phase nadir grey is a weather state (the body underneath is proven saturated) — the named follow-up is a “keep the nadir open” weather rule or a lower CLOUD_AMOUNT_DAY, for a future ticket.
  • The sky's stretched window vs AC-0386's “one number, one home”: the physics band stays the home for the limb gate, the star opacity and the sun-glow fade (all unchanged); the BACKGROUND blend alone gets a documented sky-specific derivation (same live constants, BAND_WALK_MAX .. BAND_FLY_MIN×1.6). The flight band's purpose is the walk/fly split, not the sky — recorded in ARCHITECTURE.md.
  • The in-frame gradient direction (bright toward the top of the tangent frame, deep toward the disc): the sky gradient is keyed to the CAMERA horizon (h = dir.y) with the single-source air colour at h = 0 (the AC-0386/AC-0400 contract) — pre-existing, present in the before frames identically, and the ground-level sky the user has is that shape. The ticket's measured D4 requirement is the ALTITUDE RESPONSE, which the ladder now shows; flipping the base gradient would re-shape the whole ground-level sky and is NOT done here.
  • The 120/300 m frames: 120 m is inside the outermost deck shell (4275 > camera radial 4261) — no sky region at all, before or after (the weather fills the frame — correct for a low approach). The 300 m sky is bit-identical to the old sky (window = 0 below 500 m alt_rad).

Findings (for the coordinator / future tickets)

  • The pre-existing 300 m era difference: the AC-0384-era final-descent 300 m sky differs from the AC-0401-era (pre-AC-0402) 300 m sky by mean ~30 sRGB (R-biased, top-of-frame (139,239,255)→(96,217,255)) with NO AC-0402 involvement (my ON frame is pixel-identical to the AC-0401-era frame). Something between the AC-0384 final run and the AC-0401 run moved the 300 m sky — probably an uncommitted-hunk state difference; cheap to bisect if it ever matters.
  • The deck drift phase is not strictly deterministic across runs (the per-frame _cloud_time increment is minf(delta, 0.05), so fast frames accumulate less drift) — two runs with the same CLOUD_T0 pin can land on different mass positions over the nadir. This is why the D5 “grey at T0.5” is phase-dependent; any future nadir-weather rule should pin the deck clock too.
