AC-0423 — plan: the see-through gaps between chunks

1. The defect (established by the previous run)

A frame at ground level shows what is behind the terrain along the chunk seams: pale-cyan slits (RGB ≈ 183,235,247 — skybox colour) at every seam crossing the view. BEFORE frame: captures/ac0423-before-ground-north.png. The fog wall (332 m) cannot reach 16–30 m seams — refuted. Measured live (probe r4/r4b): a 552–558 mm across-seam gap at terrain-top height (terrain tops 139–146) at the x=16 seam; node transforms verified against a Python replication to the mm.

2. Root cause (analytic + measured)

The per-column tangent-sheet placement (SphereMath.column_transform / C++ sm_column_transform, SEAM_FILL 1e-4 included) is gapless at the sheet base — the folded-net edge lines coincide to the 1.6 mm AC-0362 grout — but the sheets' edge lines diverge with height: the neighbour's radial leans 16/R away from the own sheet's radial, so a V of width ≈ h·(16/R) opens at surface height h above the fold (fold ≈ 0.4 m, near the base). Terrain tops sit h ≈ 140 m up → ~0.55 m see-through slit at every seam at ground level (0.5584 m at t = 140, i = 8, R = 4000 — matches the 552–558 mm measurement). A line of sight aimed at the seam (depression band ≈ 5.56°–5.72°) threads the V channel — its walls are the culled seam faces (no geometry) — to the skybox. Cliff cases self-close (the cliff face catches the ray); the open case is roughly-flat terrain across the seam (the common case). AC-0307's close note documents the divergence as a "known physical consequence" covered by "AC-0042's hidden filler" — the filler is the flat-world one and never existed in the sphere placement, so nothing ever closed the V; the AC-0362 grout closed the sheet-plane footprint residual (vertical rays) only.

3. Fix design

Extend the top-face edge rows (opaque merged + single-quad + fluid top) past the nominal 0..16 local footprint by the V width at the corner's own height, so the two sheets' top faces overlap across the seam; the sheet planes then meet in the fold crease and no line of sight threads the seam at surface level:

E(t, i) = K0[k][i] + K1[k]·t + SEAM_V_MARGIN (0.05 m), clamped [0, SEAM_V_MAX (2.5 m)]

with k ∈ {0=W, 1=E, 2=S, 3=N} (parse_nbs order), i = the along-edge cell (0..15), t = the corner's local height. K0/K1 come from the two columns' placed transforms (awesm::sm_column_transform) and the neighbour's edge-column tops read per cell from the neighbour ring already handed to build_accs (slab·256 + y_in·16 + t; an all-air edge cell has no seam surface there → no V to fill). The per-cell top is the exact geometric value — the V closes against the neighbour's own edge surface (no over-extension into its solid). Side faces stay on the exact seam plane (top faces only — the merged path is guarded on fi == 2, the single-quad paths on fcv == FCV[2]).

UVs: stretch (the fold crease hides the strip).
