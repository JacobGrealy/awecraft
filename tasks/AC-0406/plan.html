AC-0406 — plan: the cloud volume gets sky light

Goal

The user: “why are all the clouds dark?” The raymarched volume (AC-0405) is lit by self-shadowing alone against a peak-normalised HG phase — any sample not directly sunlit and forward-scattering scatters ~0, which from underneath (where the player looks at clouds) is every sample (measured before-underside ALBEDO ≈ 0.007, i.e. black). Fix the lighting — NOT the density, the radii, or the march — so undersides and interiors read as soft grey with the project's air tint while keeping AC-0405's gains: the 68.9 brightness spread (vs the reference's 32.4), the inter-layer parallax, and the saturated tint (181,229,190 at disc centre).

Acceptance (the user's view, measured): luminance of a cloud's underside (120 m ground frame) and interior (300 m in-annulus frame) against its sunlit top (700 m deck band), before (AC-0405's r120-on / r300-on / r700-on) and after (same cameras, same frozen weather T=0.5, CLOUD_T0=411200, R24/F83 user config). A ratio near 1 = flat (the old deck's failure); the target is readable form without black shadow. Spread + tint re-measured after, against 68.9 and 181,229,190. Added frame cost stated (r700 wall vs AC-0405's 781 s).
