What this gate does and does not establish. It proves the seam machinery reaches the pixels: the per-primitive arm repaints 0.0447 of the frame and the blend-window arm 0.0021, both far above the ~0.0002 antialiasing floor. It does not rank the two tiers by how good they look — at any framing where the whole creature is visible a limb joint is a few hundred pixels, and the toon ramp's band quantisation collapses much of the remaining difference. Reading that as "smooth is better" would be reading a threshold, not a measurement.

One control is wired but visually inert, and is recorded as such. toon: false swaps the quantised wrapped lambertian for the unquantised one; on most of the surface the two differ by about one step of 255, so the toggle changes a documented 0.0002 of pixels — the antialiasing floor. It is proven to apply (the state reads back) and is excluded from the pixel requirement rather than being tuned until it looked like a signal.
