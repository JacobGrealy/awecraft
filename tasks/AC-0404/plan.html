Goal

  1. Item 1 (live) — the tiers leave with one rule. Every near tier (high = band A chunks, medium/low = bands B/C annulus) must give way to the satellite body as altitude rises, on one consistent rule (the body's own fog wall — d ≥ u_fog_far per fragment, the body's op = smoothstep(u_fog_far, u_render_edge, d) start — not three thresholds). The acceptance is the ladder (2600/1600/700/300/120 m, user config R24/F83, fog wall 332 m), walked as descent and takeoff: at each rung state which tiers and shells are drawn and which are not, measured from the frames (A/B renders: body-off, world-off), and show that above the handover the only thing visible is the planet and its atmosphere. If a rung measurement shows a near tier drawing past the wall, chase the violating draw path and fix it. If no tier draws past the wall, the single-rule contract holds by construction and the ladder evidence says so — report that, with the measured tier table, rather than forcing a second metaphor.
  2. Item 2 (live) — the limb is the body's material alone. The atmospheric limb treatment must not exist in any near-tier shader path in any state — not gated off, absent. Code review found exactly one near-tier material carrying a limb term: core/lod_avg.gdshader's haze bridge (haz = mix(1.0, fog_f, w) — the outer 48 m of the annulus forced to 100% air colour, the body's rim mirrored onto the ground). The chunk_lit_* four and the fluid_anim_* two are fog-only (engine depth fog + partition discard). Fix: strip the bridge — the annulus fades with the engine-fog model (fog_f) only, up to the wall (where fog_f = 1.0 = exactly u_air, so AC-0400's single-source seam with the body's haz_d rim is preserved by construction; the annulus fog factor then equals band A's engine fog at the same view distance — continuous everywhere, not just at the inner edge). No gate can bring a term back that is not in the path. The body keeps haz_a/haz_d; the sky pass keeps its altitude-gated background glow (the orbit instrument) — both are the satellite-lod / atmosphere side of the contract. The missed case: measure the rim on the near tiers at an altitude where the gate is open (rung G600, S≈0.013, plus the well-open ladder rungs) — before and after — plus the user-state probe (below), because the previous acceptance measured the glow from orbit (open) and the near field at low altitude (shut), never the near tiers at open-gate altitude.
  3. Item 3 (SUPERSEDED) — confirm, don't redo. The clouds were reworked as a raymarched volumetric layer (AC-0405, closed) with the lighting model (AC-0406, closed). Do not touch them; confirm they still hold after the changes (the ladder frames run with the volume default-ON; the 120 m frame shows the volume state; a shell A/B at 1600 proves the deck draws).
