    a) NO candidate is exact on every sample. The plan's gate anticipated this branch;
       the statement is: the correct inverse of the mesh's LSQ placement divides by
       |e|² AND inverts the full 3x3. norm_basis (o·e/|e|², the plan's candidate 3) is
       the WINNER but only 0.4422607421875 exact, because at the chart-CORNER chunks the
       LSQ basis is strongly NON-ORTHOgonal — scalediag-r1.log: chunk 5:0:0 has
       |bx|=5.7503, |bz|=2.8924, bx.bz=8.1625 (cos ≈ 0.49) — so the orthogonal-projection
       approximation misses up to a full chunk width (worst 16). The missing fifth
       candidate is B.affine_inverse() (full 3x3 inverse) — a design decision for the fix
       ticket, deliberately not invented here.

       (candidates 2/4) is the WORST of the three distinct formulas (mean 38.468994140625
       cells, worst 91): dividing the pre-divided anchor basis by sc leaves |e|²/sc as the
       effective divisor, and the non-orthogonal cross term l_z·(e_z·e_x)/sc contributes
       ≈ 21 cells at the corner (15.8 × 8.1625 / 6.1359, all three in scalediag-r1.log /
       scaleprobe-r2.log).
