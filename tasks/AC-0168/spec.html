Registry notes (verbatim, quoted):

1) User Story: As a player walking near the edge of render distance, I want chunks at r+1 to stay on screen (fog hides them) so stepping back doesn't make the edge flicker or pop.

2) Technical Details: User decision after discussion: r+1 hysteresis should retain mesh AND keep it visible (not visible=false). Currently AC-0080 hysteresis: r+1 candidate keep data+mesh, >=r+2 free after 2 frames. Keep mesh_built true at r+1, fog_far=(R+1)*16*0.95 hides it, true free only at r+2. Follows AC-0167 same-mesh band fix. Verify walking edge shows no unload/reload flicker at distance line. Extra ~300-400 cols at R=50 (7845 circle).

Goal

R+1 hysteresis retains the mesh and keeps it visible (hide-not-kill): a candidate one render-ring past the radius keeps mesh_built=true with its instances unhidden (the fog at fog_far=(R+1)*16*0.95 already covers the band), so stepping back re-enters it instantly with no unload/reload flicker or pop; true free stays at r+2 after the existing 2-recenter-event hysteresis.
