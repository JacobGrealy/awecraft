The planet now creates its own horizon. The sky must contain no floating horizontal line, no lower sky floor, no artificial horizon band competing with the planet silhouette. Keep the day/night sky, sun, moon, stars, altitude transition and planet atmosphere.

3. The four removal sites (locate each by symbol)

  1. The artificial local-horizontal division. Filing: hl = dot(dir,upc)/r feeding below_old/below_soft/below, splitting the background at hl=0 rather than at the planet silhouette. Remove that split from the corrected background path, including the under_disc forced-fill decision. Keep geometric variables that planet-relative atmosphere genuinely needs.
  2. The separate below-horizon air/space floor. Filing: belowc construction and col = mix(above, belowc, below). Explicitly: do NOT merely widen smoothstep(0, 0.03, -hl) — widening is softening, which the ticket forbids.
  3. The artificial horizon anchor/floor compensation. Filing: a clamped elevation u, horizon_eff/anchor, and thinning tied to the synthetic boundary must no longer impose a lower edge or horizon-centred stripe. A smooth directional sky gradient may remain, but must continue through local horizontal without a floor, kink or anchored stripe. The filing flags a specific code-supported residual band: u_no_floor=1 selects horizon_color at ws=0, bypassing horizon_eff, while belowc selects u_air — two different colours meeting at the horizon. That is the mechanism to remove.
  4. The explicit local-horizontal haze stripe. Filing: hz = exp(-abs(hl)*9.0) … col = mix(col, haze_color, hz), which paints a horizon independent of the planet. Do not confuse it with the planet-relative air-column term (preserve that).

4. The two audits

  • sky() routing: confirm which path is taken by default and that the default path is the corrected one.
