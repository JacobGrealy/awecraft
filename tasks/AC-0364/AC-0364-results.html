3. The window — duration and what bounds it

3.1 Measured (hard): ≥ 10.13 s, and the window is still open at 10.13 s

At the walk's end — 10.13 s after the gate-lift proxy — the state is: in-radius HIGH set 72/145 (50%), low coverage ~97% (1076/1105), queue 1316 deep and flat, storm alive (109 storm frames in the walk, p99 180, max 354). The in-radius set is NOT substantially built and the storm has NOT stopped by the end of the only thing we can measure directly — so 10.13 s is the hard lower bound, and the rest is the model below (labeled as such).

3.2 Modeled (the extrapolation, with its error bars): total window ≈ 20 ± 4 s

After the walk (player still, production → 0), the queue drains at the steady-state rate of the §5 model: rate(T) = (T/c) / (T + K) per ms, with the measured c = 4.5 ms, K = 20 ms → 133 units/s at the current budget. Queue 1316 → ~9.9 s → storm ends at ≈ 20 s from gate-lift. The in-radius HIGH set completes when the drain + TM/handoff finish the remaining 73 columns: the high columns are the INNERMOST of the (taxi, layer) queue order, but their landing is paced at the measured ~5.8–8/s (TM inflight 3 + light-settle defers + handoff) → 10.13 + 73/5.8–8 ≈ 19–22.7 s. The two converge: storm end ≈ high-set completion ≈ 20 s. Error bars: the walk-phase sustained rate was ~75/s (recenter churn + COLLIDE spikes eat budget — measured net −36/10.13 s), the still-phase ~133/s is a model point, and c carries ± 1 ms from the 20-frame sample → the total window is 20 ± 4 s; the high-set "substantially built" point is 19–23 s. (The arm's settle loop ran to its 1200-frame cap mid-window — consistent, not a contradiction: 1200 steps at ~20/s ≈ 60 s > the remaining ~10 s of window.)

3.3 The bound — the census answer

  • NOT generation throughput. TG is STARVED: tg inflight 1–2 in all 20 worst frames (Little's law: 1.5 ÷ 5.5 ms ≈ a 270/s data-landing capacity against a ~100/s demand). Raising threadgen_max buys nothing — the workers are waiting on dispatch, not the other way round. (This is the census line the ticket asked for: AC-0352's "pool pinned at its cap" reads as saturation, but the current ring shows TG idle, TM at 1–8 slab tasks against a 64-slab / 3-chunk gate — the pools are under-full, not pinned.)
  • NOT the 3.5 ms pace constant. The unit bank saturates (its 28 ms cap ≈ two fast steps' accumulation) and the drain runs to its 30 ms TIME cap every storm frame — halving the pace constant alone changes nothing (§5, the no-op finding).
  • IT IS the main-thread DRAIN stage: the 30 ms/frame time cap × the ~4.5 ms per-unit dispatch cost → ~6–8 units/frame → ~75–130 units/s sustained — against a queue of 1316 that the walk keeps refilling at ~71/s. Window ≈ queue ÷ rate.
  • Coadjutor: the COLLIDE lane — ~25 ms/frame average (0–44 ms spiky, 841 staged drops over the walk) sits in K and keeps frames > 30 ms; it scales with landing rate, so it dies with the queue but extends the tail.
  • Separate bound for the measured "built" metric: the in-radius HIGH fill (5.8/s) is paced by TM chunk-inflight 3 + the light-settle defers + the handoff — the drain defers the high columns and ships the far lows instead. That is why the arm's in_radius_built creeps at ~6/s while the tree fills at ~85/s.

3.4 The load gate next to the window (the spec's STEP-2 pair of numbers)

The gate (SIM TAXI DIAMOND, 41 columns at sim 4) holds ≈ 2.1 s at R24 (AC-0313 standing value; this run's boot-to-walk phase, derived from the ring's t_ms span + walk_s, is 2.7–5.9 s — the harness's 3×3-core proxy is looser than the game's gate). Gate ≈ 2.1 s vs window ≈ 20 s: the gate is ~10% of the story. Moving the gate earlier buys at most ~2 s; the decision belongs on the window, not the gate.

5. The cost curve — window vs per-frame fill budget (the model; no constant was changed)

Setup. "Per-frame fill budget" must mean the unit cap AND the time cap together: with c = 4.5 ms/unit, the 30 ms time cap already allows only ~6.7 units < the 8-unit cap, so raising LOW_WAVE_FRAME_CAP (or halving the 3.5 ms pace) alone is a NO-OP — the bank is saturated and the time cap binds first (this is the measurement contradicting the inherited "the drain is paced at 3.5 ms/unit" framing: that paces the bank, the frame is capped by time + cost). Points: budget ×1/×2/×4/×8 = (B, T) = (8,30) / (16,60) / (32,120) / (64,240). Model: units/step u = T/c (cap B never binds), step wall fw = T + K, steps/s = 1000/fw, wall rate = u/fw; window = walk (10.13 s, kinematic — production keeps the queue refilled at ~71/s, so all points keep the measured 10.13 s walk; the queue at walk end ≈ 1316 at every point) + 1316 ÷ rate. Frame cost: DRAIN ms ≈ T per storm frame; p99/max scaled linearly on the DRAIN share (20 ms non-DRAIN held fixed) — upper-bound extrapolation, stated as such.

The curve is flat — the ticket's most valuable branch, and it is the measurement's answer. 8× the fill budget buys −17% window (20 → 16.5 s) while tripling the storm's worst-frame cost (p99 180 → ~940 ms, fps 20 → 4). The reason is arithmetic, not tuning: the wall rate is (T/c)/(T+K) and saturates at 1/c ≈ 222 units/s — the main-thread per-unit dispatch cost. More budget just moves the same ~4.5 ms of main-thread work into fewer, longer frames (the code's own "frame-rate bound" arithmetic, world.gd ~8000: 8×30 fps ≈ 4–5×60 fps ≈ the same wall rate). The K = 20 ms non-DRAIN overhead (COLLIDE's ~25 ms average included) taxes every step and is why even the asymptote (16.0 s) leaves the 10.13 s kinematic walk untouched. A main-thread budget cannot move this window by more than ~20%; the fix that moves it is cutting c (halving 4.5 → 2.25 ms doubles the ceiling to 445/s AND halves every DRAIN frame — the storm would end with the queue at half depth, i.e. window ≈ 13–15 s at unchanged budget).

6. Verdict — the single cheapest change

drain_budget_ms 30 → 60 (world.gd 126/3113), paired with LOW_WAVE_FRAME_CAP 8 → 16 (line 970) so the bank can feed it. Two one-line constant edits, no new code path, fps-independent by construction (the wall-clock bank is unchanged), reversible. It IS the first point of the measured curve: window −10% (20 → 18 s), the storm's DRAIN 30 → 60 ms (p99 ≈ 180 → ~287, max ≈ 354 → ~567, storm fps 20 → 12.5). It is the cheapest change that the census identifies as on the binding constraint — TG is starved (its cap is not), the handoff cap (17 in-harness / 5 walking) has headroom, and the pace constant alone is a no-op (§5). Two honest caveats for the follow-up ticket: (i) beyond 2× the curve flattens (§5) — the budget is the cheap FIRST step, not the fix; (ii) the in-radius "built" metric is separately TM/settle-gate-paced (5.8/s) — threadmesh_max 3 → 6 is the paired one-constant move that halves the high-set completion (window 20 → ~17 s) at the cost of ~1–2 ms/frame extra handoff+collision. And the real fix — cutting the ~4.5 ms per-unit main-thread dispatch cost (slab copy + nbs stamps + the pick/scan + dedup) — is the only lever on BOTH axes of the curve; per the fence it is its own ticket with its own gates.

The per-chunk GENERATION cost (5,502 µs) enters none of the window arithmetic — TG is starved (1–2 inflight), so the AC-0363 "1.84× cost → 1.84× window" stretch does not show in THIS window: the window is queue ÷ drain-rate, and the drain-rate is main-thread-work bounded, not generation-cost bounded.
