Registry notes (verbatim, quoted):

1) User Story

Walking into the world is smooth once it is built, but for the first minutes after loading I am walking through a world that is not there yet, and that is when the long frames land. I want to know how long that window actually lasts, and what it would cost to shorten it, so the decision to spend frames on filling faster can be made from numbers instead of taste.

2) Technical Details

PREREQUISITE - READ AC-0363's RESULTS FIRST. That ticket attributes the post-cave-series regression: per-chunk generation went 2,884 -> 5,319 us/chunk across the cave series (carver, aquifers, P4 contents), against an AC-0352 measurement taken on a 2026-09-23 tree at roughly half today's cost. Do not set any budget here before knowing whether the current cost is 5,319 us/chunk or materially above it. The two tickets are deliberately adjacent in the queue for this reason.

THE STATE THIS TICKET EXPLAINS (AC-0352, R24, 2026-09-23):
- At the end of a 10-chunk walk: `in_radius_built_final` **22-34 of `target_in_radius` 2,401 (~1%)**, `queue_size` **1,306-1,352**, `resident_final` 1,453, TM pool **pinned at its cap (tm/tg 8/2)** in every one of the 20 worst frames.
- The storm is not a spike: it IS the post-load fill state. The load gate only waits for the SIM TAXI DIAMOND (41 columns at sim 4, `simband_ms` ~2.1 s at R24 - AC-0313 clause 4), so the player activates and walks while the resident set and far field are still being built.
- The drain is paced BY TIME per unit and by a per-frame unit cap (the constants named in the tree; AC-0363 records their current values - read them there rather than trusting a name from this note). Consequence to CONFIRM, not assume: when the cost of one unit rises, units discharged per frame falls, so the under-built WINDOW lengthens even if no single frame gets worse. That is a different failure shape from a per-stage slowdown and the measurement must distinguish them.

STEP 0 - measure the window, not the frame:
- Extend the walk arm to emit a TIME SERIES of `in_radius_built` vs `target_in_radius` against wall clock, plus queue depth, resident count and TM/TG in-flight, sampled every N frames.
- Deliverables: **columns/second** sustained; **time-to-built** (seconds until `in_radius_built` reaches `target_in_radius`, or the asymptote if it never does inside the walk); the queue depth TREND (rising = the drain is behind production; flat = balanced); and the wall time the player spends above a stated "under-built" threshold (e.g. built < 50% of target).
- Report the same figures at r4 (cheap reference) and R24, and state the seed, the knobs, and the tree SHA - AC-0352's numbers are a dated reference and cannot be compared without one.

STEP 1 - the budget curve (the actual decision input):
- Sweep the drain's unit pace / per-frame cap constants over a small set of values (e.g. current, 2x, 4x) and for each report BOTH sides: (a) columns/second and time-to-built, (b) the frame-time cost - `storm_walk_p99_ms` and `max_ms` against the AC-0352 standing gate (`storm_walk_p99_ms <= 200`).
- The output is a TRADE CURVE (fill speed vs. worst-frame cost), not a recommended constant. Choosing a point on that curve is a product decision (how much hitch for how much earlier world); this ticket supplies the curve and the coordinator picks.
- If the curve is flat - i.e. raising the budget does not shorten the window because the bottleneck is upstream (generation throughput on the workers, disk, or the TM pool cap rather than the main-thread pace) - SAY SO. That is the most valuable possible finding here: it would mean the fill window is bounded by something a main-thread budget cannot move, and the fix belongs elsewhere (worker pool sizing, far-field generation cost, or the load gate).

STEP 2 - the load gate, measured not guessed:
- Report how long the load screen holds at R24 and what it waits for (`simband_ms`, the 41-column sim diamond), next to how long the world then takes to become usable. If the fill window is minutes, the gate/streaming split is a legitimate design question - but it is answered by these two numbers, not by moving the gate first.

FENCES: measurement only - do NOT retune any constant, do not change the load gate, do not touch generation. Whatever the curve shows becomes its own ticket with its own gates. Hold the standing values (boundary r4 band, storm_walk_p99_ms, the R24 crossing row) and state them per sweep point so a regression is visible while sweeping.
