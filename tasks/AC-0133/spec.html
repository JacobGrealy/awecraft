Goal

Restore the web-parity star behavior the user sees in the frozen web: the star field is a WORLD-SPACE object (a child of the world root, NOT parented to the camera) that is recentered on the camera position every frame — web :3248 window.__stars.position.copy(camera.position) — with its ROTATION never set. Consequence (the web's actual behavior): the field does not parallax with camera movement (it follows the eye) and appears fixed to the sky — when you turn your head, the stars rotate in your view (you see different parts of the fixed field), but when you walk they stay put in the sky. The user's report — "stars do not move with sky, look like dead pixels" — is the signature of a screen-fixed field: the node is parented to the camera (or its transform is camera-composed), so it inherits the camera's rotation and the stars are locked to the screen. Fix the transform/parentage so the stars are fixed to the sky, not the screen.
