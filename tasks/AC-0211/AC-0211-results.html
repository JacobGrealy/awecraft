ALL GATES GREEN. The remaining mesh GDScript hot spots around build_accs moved to C++: (1) the neighbor SNAP ring (dispatch used to _slabs_deepcopy ALL 24 slabs × 4 neighbors; the worker only ever reads ONE 16×16 boundary slice per slab — the new AweMesh.snap_rings snapshots exactly that, 256 B/slab, and parse_nbs consumes the compact ring natively), (2) the own-column value-copy (AweMesh.slab_copy replaces ChunkIO._slabs_deepcopy on the C++ lane), (3) the flat-view expansion (the sync build_mesh lane materialized 5 × 98k flat cells in GDScript before the C++ call — the new AweMesh.sync_snap decodes the paletted slabs in C++, same lane as build_accs), and (4) the scoped handoff stale-check (AweMesh.rows_eq — 256-B row equality without the per-row 4096 flat materialization). LOSSLESS: extended meshprobe = 100% exact — 32/32 chunks, 1770/1770 accs, plus the four NEW AC-0211 arms all 32/32 (compact-ring nbs → byte-identical build_accs result vs the legacy full-slab nbs; slab_copy parity; rows_eq verdicts vs the GDScript row loop; sync_snap vs the legacy GDScript _build_snap). MINFO 88/89 exact + 1 border chunk in the pre-existing jitter band (proven pre-AC-0211 on the AWECRAFT_MESHCPP=0 lane). G0 0 script errors; SMOKE battery ok:true ×4 (C++ on AND AWECRAFT_MESHCPP=0 fallback); chunkiocpp md5-identical; genhash 25/25 == AC-0188; light/stripsprobe/pullprobe preserved. nbs+copy snapshot per chunk: 213 µs GDScript → 132 µs C++ (1.6×); worker build_accs unchanged band (2.88 → 3.09 ms).
