AC-0439 plan — the sky must be consistent with where the player actually is

Goal

The user's rule, stated three times: the sky must be consistent with where the player actually is. In space there is no horizon and the planet is the boundary (correct today — keep it). Inside an atmosphere, the sky below the local horizon is the same air as the sky above it, and only the planet's surface ends it. A boundary drawn by the sky model itself must not exist in either case. The decision keys on INSIDE vs OUTSIDE the atmosphere — the distinction AC-0429 erased when it made below-the-horizon space at every altitude.

Where the defect is (current build, read before touching anything)

sky_on() in godot/core/aero_sky_gradient.gdshader: the weight ws = smoothstep(0.0, 0.3, w) (where w = u_space_sky = Aero.sky_space(alt) = smoothstep(0.25A, A, alt)) drives all three of AC-0429's changes — the below-horizon floor belowc = mix(u_air, space, ws), the gradient's horizon anchor anchor = mix(horizon_color, space, ws) (with u_no_floor = 1), and the death of the horizon haze band (hz *= (1-ws)). ws reaches 1 at w ≥ 0.3 ≈ 630 m radial — deep inside the atmosphere (A = 1200 m). At the user's frame (radial 941 m, w = 0.799): the floor is space (black below the horizon), the anchor is space (the sky darkens to black at the horizon), and blue survives only above u ≈ 0.3 plus the sun glow. That is exactly the reported frame: blue above, black below, split by a line the sky model drew. The inside/outside decision is made in the shader from the A-window blend, and it fires 570 m too early. The measurement that proved AC-0429 (flat zero below the horizon) was taken at 800 m — i.e. inside — and was the defect reported as the fix.

Where the inside/outside transition happens, and why

At A = 1200 m radial (Aero.atmosphere_height(), env-overridable via AWECRAFT_ATMO): below A the player is INSIDE — the floor is air, the anchor is the air-blended horizon, the haze band is alive; at/above A the sky above the horizon is ALREADY fully space (w = 1 from A), so the floor must be space too. To keep no line in any single frame, the floor fades air→space over [A, A+0.25A] = [1200, 1500] m — the same tail the limb's air column draws, so the last air seen below the horizon is the grazing column the limb is already painting. Why A and not the current w ≥ 0.3: A is the sky's own space boundary — the one home for "how far up is space" that u_space_sky, the star field, the sun glow and the body all already key on; the decision must be the player's radial position relative to that shell, not a re-derivation from the sky blend.
