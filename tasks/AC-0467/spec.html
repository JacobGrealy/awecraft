Registry notes (verbatim, quoted):

1) User Story
Two visible defects have the same root and the same cure. Blocks sitting on a chunk seam still render smeared over each other - the band is 602-730 mm wide even though the crack it hides is only 506-634 mm - and the world is still built by two different systems depending on which part of the planet you stand on. Putting the HOME pair onto the same sphere placement the outer faces already use makes neighbouring columns agree at every height by construction, which removes the overlap band (and its texture smear) instead of narrowing it, and it is the remaining half of the one-world migration.

2) Technical Details
WHY THIS IS THE CURE FOR AC-0434, measured in its stage 1 (round 28): the seam band exists because each side extends its edge rows past its nominal footprint by E = K0[k][i] + K1[k]*t + SEAM_V_MARGIN, clamped to [0,2.5] m (gdext/src/mesh.cpp:426-454), to close a lean-induced crack. Per-corner overlap is 602-730 mm (mean 656) over 144 cells, closing a crack of 506-634 mm. A half-rule only halves that band (~330 mm, still visible). If each column is placed on its own radial, neighbouring surfaces COINCIDE at every height, so nothing needs extending - the K tables, the ring reads, sphere_r, the margins and the clamp all delete, and with them the UV stretch the user photographed.
WHY IT IS ALSO THE MERGE'S OTHER HALF: the warp placement already does exactly this on the face lane (AC-0458 flag, AC-0460 default on, occluder parity 335/335, cost 0.423 ms/chunk in native code). The HOME pair still rides the per-column LSQ transforms (SphereMath.column_transform, world.gd _col_sphere_transform 11412). This ticket extends the same canonical placement to the home lane.
SCOPE: the home pair's per-column transform path, its 1 m flat grid and the consumers that assume it. Reuse the validated machinery rather than inventing: SphereMath.uv_to_world / world_to_face as the chart, the native warp (gdext warp_face_verts) for the vertex pass, and the AC-0462 exact-inverse lesson for world->cell conversion (a non-orthogonal basis needs the full 3x3 inverse, never per-axis dots - measured: per-axis reads 44% exact at best, affine_inverse 100%).
VERIFY: genhash 25/25 (generation must not move); the light set; the seam band re-measured with AC-0434's seam_band_probe.gd - the target is that the band COLLAPSES rather than narrows, and the per-cell table must show it; planetwalk (AC-0466) still green across its two boundaries; faceedit still green on both chart classes; and the render the AC-0434 ticket demands (surface-flush block on the seam, standing on the line, camera above, before/after) plus a seam gate asserting BOTH directions per cell - gap <= grout AND overlap <= grout - so closing the overlap cannot reopen the crack.
HARD CASE, from AC-0434's notes: at a step across a seam the crease of the two planes is not where the two staircases meet. The new placement must state what happens there and measure it, not leave it as an unexamined edge case.
