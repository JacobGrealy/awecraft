Fog geometry (why 0.875 hides the pop-in)

Godot depth fog (FOG_MODE_DEPTH): fog factor 0 at fog_depth_begin, 1 (invisible) at/behind fog_depth_end. The far-edge ring pops in as the player crosses a chunk boundary: its near face sits at R*16 (crossing instant, worst case) to (R+1)*16−8 (chunk center) from the player. Hidden ⟺ fog_far ≤ pop-in face. With 0.875: fog_far = 14(R+1) ≤ 16R for all R ≥ 7 → the pop-in is 100% fogged at every playable radius. The old 0.95 (fog_far = 15.2(R+1) ≤ 16R only for R ≥ 19) left the R16 crossing-instant pop at 98.7% fog — a faint flash.
