AC-0064 — Health hearts render as real heart graphics

User 2026-08-21: "the hearts for health have an issue, they do not show as heart graphics." Evidence: tasks/AC-0033/p2_paused.png (row of filled squares above the hotbar).

Root cause (verified by coordinator)

The 10 hearts in godot/ui/inventory.gd (~:249 declared, :340-347 built, :784-798 positioned) are Labels using a Unicode heart character. Godot's default font has no glyph for it → every heart renders as a tofu/filled square. The Faithful-32x pack does NOT ship the vanilla assets/minecraft/textures/gui/icons.png HUD atlas (only forge/neoforge variants — check whether those contain a usable 16px heart; if yes you may cut it from the pack, but don't depend on it). So the hearts are a code-drawn pixel glyph.

Requirement

  • Replace the 10 heart Labels with MC-style heart icons drawn in the HUD's _draw pass (or 10 small ImageTexture slots rebuilt on hp change) — a crisp 9×9 pixel-art heart (MC's shape: two rounded lobes on top, pointed bottom), rendered with nearest filtering, size matching the current row (~14-18px), with the classic dark outline so it reads on any background.
  • Three states, driven by p.hp (float, 0..20, 2 hp per heart) exactly like the current label logic (inventory.gd:793-798: v = hp − i*2): full (v≥1), half (0<v<1 — MC style: left half bright red, right half dark/empty), empty (v≤0 — dark gray heart silhouette, still visible as a heart).
  • Full heart = MC red (≈ Color("e52521")-ish / your pick, close to vanilla), outline dark. Keep position/centering of the row (already computed at 798) and the damage-flash hook (_on_damaged) behavior.
  • Do NOT touch the FOOD bar / hunger display (that is AC-0065's scope — even though it's visually adjacent). Don't move the hotbar. Don't change hp semantics anywhere.
