Registry notes (verbatim, quoted):

1) User Story

Our caves are close to vanilla's but the way they are evaluated is ours, not Minecraft's, and that difference is what costs us 1,630 us per chunk in the carve scan. I want the carving done the way Minecraft actually does it - vanilla's tunnel noise inside the density function, not a separate per-block rule on top - so the cave network matches vanilla's topology and the per-block carve apparatus can go away.

2) Technical Details

WHAT WE ALREADY HAVE (do not re-port these - they are vanilla already):
- The density ROUTER: AC-0347 P2 landed Java 1.21.4 `noise_settings/overworld.json` `final_density`'s `range_choice` as-is - the SHALLOW/DEEP split on `k = H - y` with `K_CUT = 16`, `d = min(S_ramp, 5*entrances)` shallow and `d = min(entrances, 4*layer_c^2 + clamp(-1,1)(0.27 + cheese_c) + clamp(0,0.5)(1.5 - 0.64*k/K_CUT))` deep. The constants (0.27, 0.64, 1.5, 5, 4, 0.37) are vanilla's and the unit convention is vanilla's (`2*(vn3-0.5)`).
- The CARVERS: AC-0290 is vanilla's carver caves (gallery/flat-floor, bubble-interrupted trunk, canyon) - positional + per-chunk RNG, already cheap per block.
- The AQUIFERS: AC-0291 is Bedrock's Voronoi `NoiseBasedAquifer` as-is.
- The PILLARS/VEINS/BIOME: AC-0292 is vanilla 1.21.4 `caves/pillars` (threshold 0.03 in the deep branch), big ore veins, and the +64-shifted `surface_rule` abs-y windows.
- The LAYER and ENTRANCE noises: `cave_layer` / `cave_entrance` AS-IS (rides 4 samples per 32-block period).
So the ONE piece that is ours rather than vanilla's is the TUNNEL apparatus.

THE DIVERGENCE TO REMOVE (the whole ticket):
- Ours: THREE `fbm3` fields (`f_spag`, `f_nood`, `f_gate`) on the cave lattice, combined as `|f_spag - 0.5| < SPAG_TH * w or |f_nood - 0.5| < NOOD_TH * w` with `w = clamp01((f_gate - GATE_LO)/(GATE_HI - GATE_LO))`, where `f_gate` is explicitly "the AweCraft stand-in for Bedrock's spaghetti_3d_rarity, which picks the spaghetti scale per region".
- Vanilla: `spaghetti_2d` + `spaghetti_3d` (with its own rarity selector) + `noodle`, all evaluated INSIDE the density function and combined with the cheese/layer/pillar terms by the router's min/max structure.
- THE STRUCTURAL CONSEQUENCE, and the reason this is a real change rather than a rename: our tunnel rule sits OUTSIDE `dens_at` BY DESIGN ("ours: the AC-0289 tunnel rule, which stays OUTSIDE dens_at - the tunnels keep piercing the caps and connecting the levels"). That placement is what forces the separate per-block pass over the carve band, and the per-block pass is the cost: the scan is 1,630 us/chunk = 29% of the 5,711 us total, and the tunnel predicate is evaluated per cell through `tunnel_air_c`'s trilinear lookups.
- The stated purpose of staying outside is to preserve vanilla's TOPOLOGY (cap-piercing, level-connecting). The claim to test is that putting vanilla's own tunnel noises inside the density function preserves that topology by construction, because vanilla's topology IS that density function - which makes this convergence rather than a regression.

WHAT THE TICKET CHANGES:
1. Replace the three-field `f_spag`/`f_nood`/`f_gate` apparatus with vanilla's `spaghetti_2d` + `spaghetti_3d` + `noodle` noise instances evaluated inside `dens_at`, using vanilla's constants and unit convention.
2. Delete `tunnel_air_c` and the per-block tunnel part of the carve scan. The scan keeps its one remaining job (deriving the solid flags + the effective surface from the density field, which the fill and the fluid decision need) - confirm whether that remaining scan is still needed per block or can be reduced, and report the finding either way.
3. Delete the three tunnel fields from the full-path field build (three fewer `fbm3` builds at 2 octaves each).
4. Decide and record the fate of `f_gate`: vanilla's `spaghetti_3d_rarity` at vanilla's threshold, or vanilla's region-based scale selection. Do not keep an AweCraft stand-in in a ticket whose purpose is convergence.

EXPECTED PERFORMANCE EFFECT (measure, do not assume): the three field builds leave the 1,502 us field stage, the per-block tunnel predicate leaves the 1,630 us scan, and the density function gains vanilla's tunnel terms - which DO evaluate per block, because vanilla also evaluates its density function per block. So the win is "three field builds + a whole per-block predicate" against "a few more terms in an already-per-block function". Report before/after per-stage numbers (`g_t_*`) and state whether the net is a win or a wash. A wash is an acceptable result if the topology improves; do not dress it up.

CONTRACTS AND GATES (this is the high-risk part - it changes generated terrain):
- **H must NOT move.** H is `surface_h` of the 3 SURFACE fields, and the skip/far paths never read a cave field, so it stays bit-exact BY CONSTRUCTION. Gate it: thash byte-identical, `farab` 1080/1080 with `h_mismatch` 0, and the promotion contract (far -> real full regen) re-proven.
- **genhash WILL move and must be REBASED deliberately**, with the new reference recorded and the reason stated (an intentional generation change, not a regression - the AC-0292/AC-0360 precedent). `genprobe` grows lockstep blocks for the new spaghetti/noodle instances and must be exact (the AC-0291 pattern: 10,300/10,300 f64-exact).
- **AC-0342's land-dry water contract** must hold (land open water 0, ocean surface 1,081/1,081) - the tunnel rule is applied before the solid flag, so its removal can change the water-logging decision.
- **The cave-contamination discipline is mandatory.** AC-0292's closeout recorded a contamination scare, and AC-0290/AC-0291 each needed a cell-exact before/after. Build the before/after comparison instrument FIRST (the AC-0360 `voidstat` pattern: a two-generation pre/post-carve plus a production-predicate re-run per cell with an unex invariant of 0), so the cave change is measurable rather than eyeballed. Bind the "deep bands byte-stable" style invariant where it applies.
- G0 zero SCRIPT ERROR; `ladder` band_a_ok 20/20; `meshprobe` 1.0; `halo`; `lightstate`; `r16` all sub-flags with `far_holes` 0 both bands; `boundary` r4 in band; the battery.
- Shots: record renders BUT note HARNESS section 2 - the proxy renderer cannot see underground, so cave screenshots on this box are NON-evidence (AC-0292 recorded exactly this). Use censuses as proof; if shots are taken, say plainly what they can and cannot show.
- ARCHITECTURE.md section 4/6 cave bullets updated in the same task (invariant 2), including the fact that the tunnel apparatus is gone and the scan's remaining job.

DECOMPOSITION: this is large enough to land in pieces - (P1) the vanilla tunnel noises inside `dens_at` + the census instrument, (P2) the apparatus removal (`tunnel_air_c`, the three fields, the scan reduction), (P3) the genhash rebase and the full gate set.
