   force; the mean moved 0.55 -> ~0.25, not 0.19, and the disc's AVERAGE

   "chunks load in blue" edge — is 3x weaker; the nadir is now 0.11 instead
   of 0. The total haze budget followed the real air column. Reported, not
   massaged.
