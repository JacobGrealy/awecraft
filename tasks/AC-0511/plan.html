2. Establish the mechanism before changing anything

  1. 2a WHICH TERM MAKES THE BLUE. Candidates: the body's haz_d air-column term; the sky gradient behind the disc (core/aero_sky_gradient.gdshader — NOTE: AC-0483 removed its synthetic horizon this session, so re-read it rather than trusting older notes); or a combination. Isolate ONE TERM AT A TIME — bypass it, re-render, compare the same pixels. That is the AC-0402 method and it is expected here. Report the pixel values for each isolated state.
  2. 2b IS WHAT REMAINS DEFENSIBLE? A planet seen from 941 m does have real aerial perspective, so "there is some haze" is not automatically a defect. Measure the magnitude and hue of the tint against the near-field ground colour and state whether it is physically plausible or over-strong/mis-hued. AC-0510 removed the fog and the aerial perspective got STRONGER at 300 m (contrast 7.75 → 17.97), so at 941 m the current risk may be TOO LITTLE atmosphere rather than too much — check BOTH directions and do not assume the user wants it all removed. The user's words were that the LODs look blue while the satellite does not — that is a MISMATCH complaint, not necessarily a "no atmosphere" request.
  3. 2c DO THE TWO TIERS STILL DIFFER? The original complaint is an asymmetry: near LODs blue, satellite not. Measure both in the same frame and report whether they are now treated identically. If they differ, THAT is the defect regardless of magnitude — and unifying them is the fix, not desaturating one of them.

3. The requirement

From the user's standing position: no atmospheric treatment should make the world look like a different place depending on how far away it is. The tiers must read as ONE world. So the target is consistency, and only secondarily a specific haze strength.

3a. ⚠ REPAIR (round 115) — THE MEASUREMENT IS DONE; ONLY THE FIX NEEDS AUTHORISING

A medium run measured everything and correctly returned PLAN-INSUFFICIENT because the fix's file was outside this plan's fence. Its findings, which are the substance of this ticket:

  • THE TERM IS haz_a IN core/satellite_body.gdshader (:213), not in lod_avg.gdshader — lod_avg has been HAZE-FREE since AC-0404. My anchor was stale; the repair records the real site so nobody looks in the wrong file again.
  • ISOLATION at the 941 m nadir, 477,777 px (91% of the disc): S0 (144.5, 210.2, 172.2) → bypass (70.5, 160.1, 45.9); the haze contributes (+74.1, +50.1, +126.4)/255, and (+122, +85, +183) at the rim. At 941 m haz_d is ≡ 0, so it is haz_a ALONE. The sky is a separate background blue that tints no terrain (0 terrain px changed when it was isolated), so it is not part of the fix.
  • THE TIERS DO DIFFER, measured in one frame at 300 m: near (71.3, 119.7, 49.8) against satellite solid (214.5, 248.2, 248.2) — Δluma 136.7. That is the user's mismatch complaint in numbers.
  • THE HUE IS CORRECT AND THE MAGNITUDE IS NOT. u_air (208,241,251) is the sky's own single-source horizon colour — the right Rayleigh hue. But the haze is ~3x too strong at 941 m (haz 0.55 against the game's own sky-model air column of ~0.19) and ~22x at the 300 m wall (100% against 0.046 — haz_a saturates at 324 m). Both directions were checked: the risk is TOO MUCH atmosphere, not too little, because the sky is already ~80% space and the near field has been crisp since AC-0510.

THEREFORE THE FIX IS AUTHORISED, AND IT IS A WEAKENING:

  1. Make haz_a physical: haz_a = clamp(d / u_air_cm, 0, 1) instead of its current saturating curve, so the air column grows with actual depth through the atmosphere rather than reaching 100% at 324 m.
  2. Push u_air_cm = 7273.5 m from Aero.atmosphere_height() — use the game's OWN existing atmospheric constant rather than a new magic number. Add the push site and say where it went.
  3. Decide the haz_d WALL BRIDGE explicitly. AC-0399's haz_d rim existed partly to bridge the seam with the ENGINE env fog — and AC-0510 removed that fog this session, so the premise is GONE. So state what haz_d is still for: if it now bridges nothing, say so and remove it or say why it stays. Do not leave a term whose justification was deleted by a later ticket.
  4. Expected outcome to verify, not assume: wall haze 100% → ~5%, and the tier gap Δluma 136.7 → ~41.4, where the residue is real terrain rather than atmosphere. If the measured result differs from that, report the actual numbers.
