7. Gate (c) — is the remaining haze defensible aerial perspective? (argued, not asserted)

Magnitude: no — it runs ~3x too strong at 941 m and ~20x at the 300 m wall. The arbiter is the game's own single-source atmospheric model — the sky pass's limb term (air/cm, the "same grazing-ray physics" the body's own header claims, AC-0384 r4). The sky's normalization cm = 7273.5 m (2*sqrt(rout^2 - (R+SEA)^2), rout = R + A + 0.25A, walljump-regime.log). Against it: the body's measured mean haz at 941 m is ~0.55 vs the air-column fraction 0.129 (nadir) to 0.399 (terrain tangent), mean ~0.19 — 2.9x. At 300 m, haz_a saturates (cosv -> 0) at t = 324.4 m from the camera, so the entire body region beyond the 332 m fog wall is painted 100% air, vs an air-column fraction of 332/7273.5 = 0.046 at the wall — 22x. (Derivation: (1-cosv)^2 is the orbital grazing proxy; it is only valid for a camera above the shell. Inside the A = 1200 m shell — 300 m and 941 m are inside — the cosv -> 0 boundary arrives 324 m (alt 300) and 1246 m (alt 941) from the camera, far short of the line-of-sight air the sky model counts.)

Hue: defensible. The haze target u_air (208, 241, 251) is the sky's own horizon output (single-source Aero.fog_display, AC-0384 r8): pale blue, B/R = 1.21, the correct Rayleigh hue, and consistent with the sky by construction. The hue is not the defect; the magnitude/falloff is. Direction check (both directions, per the plan): the risk is too MUCH atmosphere, not too little — the sky at 941 m is already 79.9% space-blended per the game's own window (u_space_sky, AC-0417: "fully established" lands at A), the near field is crisp after AC-0510 (contrast 7.75 -> 17.97), and weakening the disc haze to the column value still leaves real aerial perspective: ~13-40% toward u_air plus a fully hazed rim (the tangent = 1.0 in both models), so the signature limb stays and the planet still reads as a planet.

Asymmetry attribution: with the body haze bypassed (S1), the same-frame near-vs-body gap falls from delta-luma 136.7 to 41.4 ((71.3,119.7,49.8) vs body-raw (87.9,164.6,130.7) on the changed pixels, triad-n300.json) — ~70% of the mismatch is the atmospheric term on the body; the remainder is genuine terrain (the outer disc is a paler biome at deep mips) plus the baked-vs-unshaded lighting difference — not a second atmosphere.

Mapping to the user's words (s0n941.png vs s1n941.png): at 941 m the only terrain tier in the frame is the body; the "chunks that load in" are the streaming low-mip body blocks around the player — hazed pale blue in S0, saturated green in S1 — "the planet LOD [centre, raw] does not [look blue]" is the nadir region where haz_a = 0. The body's own header says haz_a "keeps the disc's edge continuous" — it is an orbital-rim term that saturates far too early inside the atmosphere.

8. Gate (d) — fix direction, and what was (not) changed

Direction: WEAKEN — bring the body's haz driver to the single-source air-column model the sky pass already implements (the body's own header's claim). Unify follows as a consequence: at the wall the body's haze drops 100% -> ~5%, so the raw near tier and the body read as one world (the residual delta-luma 41 is terrain, not atmosphere). Strengthen is rejected (direction check above). No pixels were changed by this run: the fix's file (godot/core/satellite_body.gdshader) is outside the plan's §4 table (the anchor is stale — deviation 1), and the weaken curve (the normalization + the A push) is a design decision the plan does not make. PLAN-INSUFFICIENT on the fix step; the measurement (gates a/b/c) is complete and stands alone. Gate (d) before/after numbers are therefore N/A; the temporary bypasses are restored (git diff empty, verified at gate time).

Proposed mechanism for the coordinator's design pass (the one-line change + its uniforms): replace satellite_body.gdshader:213 float haz_a = pow(1.0 - cosv, 2.0); with the normalized air-column fraction along the camera->fragment ray: the fragment shader already computes d = length(p_w - cam_w) — the path, and below alt 1200 m the camera is inside the shell so the whole path is in-shell air — giving haz_a = clamp(d / u_air_cm, 0.0, 1.0) with u_air_cm = 2*sqrt(rout^2 - (R+SEA)^2) = 7273.5 m at A = 1200 (pushed from satellite_body.gd via Aero.atmosphere_height(), the single source; the same rout = R + A + 0.25A the sky limb uses). Keep haz = clamp(haz_a + haz_d, 0, 1) and the u_air target unchanged (single source). Expected effect (derived, walljump-regime.log): 941 m disc mean haze 0.55 -> ~0.19, rim stays 1.0; 300 m wall 1.0 -> 0.046. The coordinator must also decide the haz_d wall-bridge semantics, whose AC-0399 premise (the env fog behind the wall) no longer exists since AC-0510.
