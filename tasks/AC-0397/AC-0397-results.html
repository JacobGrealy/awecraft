2. What changed

Drive the fill from the resident far payload — do not re-derive. The payload H is the carved top (AC-0387: the far lane runs the full path's per-column sequence; farab-gated, negative-tested), so driving from it is driving from the carved top, at zero added generation cost. Rejected alternative: re-running carved_top_pass inside the skip path — the AC-0387 price (~3,258 µs/chunk) on every materialization, re-deriving bytes the lane already holds (the project's "never re-derive a value another lane computes").
