AC-0463 slice 1 — one node-creation path (pure refactor)

Coordinator contract. Medium builder applies it with tasks/templates/implement.md. Pure refactor: behaviour must not change. The lane merge is too large to attempt whole (inventory on the ticket); this is its first, safest slice — the duplication that makes every later slice harder.

1. Goal

The skeleton that creates and registers a chunk node exists once, used by both lanes, so a later merge has one place to reason about identity, residency and pooling.

2. What is actually duplicated today (re-measured after the canonical placement landed)

_ensure_face_chunk (world.gd:15339) and the home path's chunk creation (via _make_chunk_node and the column handoff) both do the same NODE-LEVEL steps:

  • _column_checkout() (1384) — the AC-0247 pooled column or a fresh node;
  • col_gen += 1 — the AC-0247 logical identity bump (instance_id is fixed per object);
  • set face, cx, cz;
  • init_slabs();
  • add_child().

Everything AFTER that differs genuinely and must not be unified here: the home lane hands generation to the worker and the face lane calls WorldGen.generate_face synchronously. Generation, scheduling and the drain stay exactly as they are.
