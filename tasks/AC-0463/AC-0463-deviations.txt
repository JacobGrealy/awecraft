      (the home r+2 free is deferred by the two-stage r+1/r+2 hysteresis,

      sampling cadence (every walk step / every 30-frame steady sample).
   b. POOLED-COLUMN ACCOUNTING = the col_gen census. Every checkout bumps
      exactly one node's col_gen (world.gd:11463) and _column_checkout bumps
      perf_pool_col_grows_n ONLY when it creates a node on demand
      (world.gd:1413 — the prewarm, 1342, is excluded), so: created = the
      col_grows delta; checkouts = the sum-of-col_gen delta across resident
      AND pooled nodes; reused = checkouts - created. No game counter added.
   c. RESIDENT = presence in world.chunks (the home pair face <= 1, the chart
      faces > 1 — the world's own split, world.gd:11934). mesh-built is NOT
      required: the drain fills data behind the resident set, and the resident
      dict is the quantity the slice-2b policy merge inherits.
   d. STEADY-STATE VERDICT — the one decision this slice makes on its own,
      and the one that cost a re-run: the first run measured the face lane
      still FILLING its window after the settle (58 -> 94 over ~90 frames,
      then flat 3/3). That is a bounded fill (the 12 ms budget paces the
      idempotent _ensure_face_chunk fill), NOT the plan's "grow without bound"
      leak — but a first<last rule would have reported it GROWING/LEAK and
      "outranked the merge" on a false positive. The instrument now does the
      plan's dichotomy honestly: a BOUNDED plateau wait first (flat over two
      consecutive 30-frame samples, cap 600 frames; the fill series recorded),
      then six samples at 30-frame spacing; GROWING = a count still rising at
      the END of the window (a real leak, reported via leak/leak_note),
      STABLE = flat (keys_same reported as evidence).
   e. HOME TRAVERSE LENGTH: 16 x 8 m in +x (128 m) from the spawn. The chart
      edge sits at hw = 3141.6 m (R default), so the traverse never comes near
      a boundary and the whole arm stays far inside the 900 s gate budget
      (measured ~2.5 min incl. boot — no shortening needed, stated per §7).
   f. window_cells reports the x-face edge-band depth rows (the +x/-x seam

3. MEASURED FACTS THAT SURPRISED THE PLANNER (baseline input to slice 2b)
   - HOME EVICTION IS HYSTERESIS-DEFERRED: a teleport recenter does NOT free
     the old window at once (r+1 = candidate, r+2+ for 2 recenter events =
     free). The resident count transiently holds the NEW window plus the
     hidden old set — r1 peak 125 vs ~93 (home traverse) / 115-118 (crossing
     steady) resident. Any unified model must reproduce this two-stage
     release, or it will measure phantom churn.
   - THE FACE WINDOW FILL RUNS AHEAD OF THE SETTLE: at the 12 ms budget the
     seam-side window (4 faces, 12/22 rows deep) takes ~1-2 s to fill after a
     recenter; it lands exactly at the window capacity and then holds
     (idempotent dict lookups) — so the "face steady state" is a well-defined
     number per position, once the fill is allowed to finish.
   - FACE EVICTION IS TRAVEL-GATED: evicted only after >16 m of travel, in one
     pass (2 face evictions across the whole run, both at the z_seam stand
     where the x_seam faces left the win+64 m band) — a stationary player
     NEVER sheds face chunks.
   - THE POOL IS COLD AT THE END: col_pool/mi_pool final 0, peaks 33/437 —
     every checkin is immediately consumed again on this tree (gate run: 448
     checkouts vs 68 created columns over the whole run; the direct r1 run
     measured 449/69 - stable to one checkout of frame-timing jitter). The
     pool amortises, it does not sit.
