2. Deviations

Stage L7 is a design note, not a code change: it establishes what the per-face AweStarlight engines still do after L3 (one drain entry) and L6 (the feed consumes the payload), with the full file:line record in AC-0463-deviations.txt (section "AC-0463 stage L7").

WHAT THE ENGINES STILL DO (2.1): five things nothing else does — the face relaxation step (world.gd:15026-15031); the settled oracle + last_eff publish + compare-and-erase re-bake, which IS the face remesh trigger (15039-15079); the payload source for all four face build sites (15004-15011 — deleting the engines silently reverts L6's feed); the edit entry point (6532-6536); the eviction lifecycle (15652-15668). The face light_settled flag has zero product readers (arms only).

FINDING (2.2c): L1's "wasted, never wrong" claim on the home drain's face-key scan does not survive code contact. _star_settled_drain (6219-6245) has no face-key guard and queries the HOME engine with face-LOCAL coordinates (6230); face-local coords are 0..392 (0-based) vs home -196..196, so in the numeric overlap [0,196]^2 a settled home column at the same numeric pair lets the home drain overwrite the face chunk's last_eff (6239) and unconditionally erase the face key from star_owed (6236-6245) — the face column never settles and the mesh keeps pre-settle light forever (margin glow stuck on the transient; glow-free: silently wrong last_eff record). No current arm measures that band in a discriminating way (crossface samples it at sky-15 cells only; facefeed samples carry the edge coordinate 392).

2.3: exactly one of L1's three per-face items is engine state — the (face,cx,cz) key space (the per-instance sec_key namespace, starlight.cpp:143-146/1370). Mirror-class orientation and the AC-0297 pull-landing rule are chunk/mesh state and survive without the per-face engine.

2.4 COSTED: (a) DELETE — needs first a C++ key migration (sec_key + 18 of 25 bindings + ~47 GDScript call sites + .so rebuild), a face-key routing decision in the home drain (else the double-settle L3 flagged), and a step-budget merge (re-baseline the face arms); risk highest, in the gate blind spot (a wrong-but-lit face bake is invisible to the current set); buys tidiness only — no policy is unified. (b) KEEP as settle-trackers — L1's residue after L3+L6 is: the engine itself (kept), the dispatch (live policy), the home drain's face-key scan (the one true residue — a behaviour-change guard needing measurement, per the finding), the whole-column face reseed (L4 froze it), the fixed per-face step, one line of defensive erase; code touched at most 1-3 lines; risk lowest. (c) MIDDLE — (b) + the contained face edit (L1's never-landed step 3): one small C++ on_edit taking local coords + the lane-1 swap; retires the per-edit whole-column reseed; moderate.

RECOMMENDATION: (b). The engines are the feed's source, their cost is measured bounded, (a)'s risk sits where the gates cannot see, and (a) unifies no policy — a deletion on a third guess about this subsystem is the failure mode the plan names.

2.5: STEP 5 IS EMPTY under (b) — every item it names is live settle-tracker function. The honest closure: step 5 empty; follow-up 1 = the measured face-key guard (its own small stage, needs a glow-sampled band arm or a declared re-baseline); follow-up 2 = optional contained face edit per 2.4(c). The ticket closes on "kept by design + one measured guard" footing, not "split removed" footing.
