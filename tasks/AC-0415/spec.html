Registry notes (verbatim, quoted):

1) User Story: As a player I can turn fog off and expect the world to look unfogged. Today the middle-distance terrain keeps fading to the fog colour anyway: with fog disabled the low/medium LOD tiles render as flat pale-blue blocky plateaus while the near tiles are correctly coloured, so one frame shows two different looks at once (user report 2026-10-09, live Windows build 222b1d6, "I have fog disabled"). With fog ON everything reads correctly, which is why this has gone unnoticed. The switch should govern the whole render ladder, not the near tiers only.

2) Technical Details:

SYMPTOM: with `fog_enabled` off (Settings, main.gd:92-97) or `AWECRAFT_NO_FOG=1` (main.gd:1482-1485), the med/low average-colour tiers render as flat `u_air` (the pale sky-horizon blue of AeroLib.fog_display) beyond the fog window, while the high tiers (world/chunk_lit_*.gdshader, which have no manual haze) show clear terrain.

CAUSE: godot/core/lod_avg.gdshader is `render_mode fog_disabled` and implements the engine's depth fog itself - `uniform vec3 u_air`, `uniform float u_fog_near`, `u_fog_far`; `if (d >= u_fog_far) { discard; }` (the AC-0401 partition); `float fog_f = clamp((d - u_fog_near) / max(u_fog_far - u_fog_near, 1.0), 0.0, 1.0);` `ALBEDO = mix(ALBEDO, u_air, fog_f);`. Those uniforms are pushed every frame in main.gd:1474-1479 from `env.fog_depth_begin` / `env.fog_depth_end` / `env.fog_light_color` - and `_update_sky` STILL assigns `env.fog_depth_begin`/`env.fog_depth_end` (main.gd:1487-1493) in the same function that just set `env.fog_enabled = false` (main.gd:1482-1485). Nothing tells this material that the switch moved, so the engine fog stops and the hand-rolled copy keeps going.

WHY THE MATERIAL IS OFF THE ENGINE FOG AT ALL (history, so nobody re-derives it as intentional): AC-0384 r8 (commit ecc5285) took it off DELIBERATELY, because it then had to fade in two directions - inward matching the factor band A receives at the same view distance (band A/annulus continuity by construction), outward matching the satellite body's own haz at the rim - and the engine fog gives exactly one factor and one mix colour per pixel. AC-0399 (71115ad) and AC-0400 (63e298c) retargeted the outer end; AC-0404 (bce6053) DELETED the bridge - "the limb belongs to the satellite lod's material alone" - which left `haz = fog_f`, i.e. the engine's own linear depth ramp reimplemented, plus a `fog_disabled` flag that now buys nothing.

FOSSIL EVIDENCE (in the file today): `u_render_edge` and `u_center` are declared and pushed every frame (main.gd:1478-1479) and are NEVER READ - one occurrence each in the file, the declaration itself. They are the deleted bridge's (and the view-weighted fill's) leftovers.

THE FIX SHAPE, DECIDED HERE - DELETE THE DUPLICATE, DO NOT FLAG IT: re-enable the engine fog on this material (drop `render_mode fog_disabled`), delete the manual haze (the `u_air` mix, `u_fog_near`, and their per-frame pushes), delete the two dead uniform pushes, and KEEP the AC-0401 `discard` - it is the partition, not fog, and it still needs `u_fog_far` (keep that one uniform, or read the value from the push that already exists for the partition). A `u_fog_on` flag was considered and rejected: it would preserve a second fog model to serve one setting, when the whole reason the second model exists is gone.

WHY THE CONTINUITY SURVIVES (this is what AC-0384 r8 hand-rolled, re-checked against the engine path):
- band A <-> annulus: both take the SAME engine factor at the same view distance - the r8 argument now works FOR the engine path instead of against it;
- annulus <-> body rim at the wall: at `d == u_fog_far` the engine factor is exactly 1.0, which is `u_air`, which is the body's `haz_d` value at the same wall - the AC-0400 single-source seam holds;
- direct precedent on the SAME instances: the water surfaces inside the same ring batches are drawn with the fluid materials (core/fluid_anim{,_bf}.gdshader), which carry no `fog_disabled` and already take the engine fog. Today one MeshInstance3D carries two fog models, one of which is correct.

WHAT MUST NOT CHANGE: the AC-0404 contract (no limb term on any near tier), the AC-0401 partition (the near tiers stop at the wall and the body's rim is the seam), the ring-batch geometry and draw state (AC-0338), and the geometry itself - this is a material/uniform change only, so genhash 25/25 must stay byte-identical and meshprobe/ladder/halo/lightstate are untouched.

EVIDENCE OWED (fog-OFF is the subject, fog-ON is the control):
- fog-OFF frames before/after: the pale-blue plateaus get their vertex colours back at the same tile layout, with no new seam where the haze used to be;
- fog-ON frames before/after: the manual ramp was authored to MATCH the engine's, so these should be near-identical - any visible difference IS the drift between the two models and must be reported as a finding, measured at the band A/annulus boundary and at the annulus/body rim;
- one frame from altitude for the annulus/body rim seam (the AC-0400 target), and the standing set (G0 zero SCRIPT/SHADER ERROR, genhash 25/25, meshprobe, ladder, halo, lightstate).

FENCE NOTE: `godot/scenes/main.gd` is DIRTY with another session's uncommitted work at the time of filing (the AC-0411 note fences main.gd/game.gd/drop.gd/debug.gd), and settings.gd/menu.gd/player.gd/harness.gd/ARCHITECTURE.md/HARNESS.md are dirty too. The push deletions live in main.gd `_update_sky` - coordinate with that session or land the shader-side deletion plus the push deletions in ONE commit once the fence lifts; do not edit main.gd while its owner is mid-task.

SWITCH: no new player-facing switch - this ticket makes an EXISTING setting (`fog_enabled`, DEFAULT ON) govern the whole ladder instead of the near tiers only. The conservative default is unchanged; only the OFF state changes, and it changes to what the player already asked for.

OUT OF SCOPE: re-characterising the fog model itself (depth curve, light energy, aerial perspective, the `fog_start_pct` value), the cloud deck's own fog behaviour, and the satellite body's fog/haze contract.
