AC-0469 stage B — fix the face-lane leak (the window is measured in cells, not chunks)

Coordinator contract. Medium builder applies it with tasks/templates/implement.md. Bounded fix: the face stream's creation window is computed in CELLS where it must be in CHUNKS, so at 1 m cells it creates ~6× more chunks than eviction removes.

1. The diagnosis (measured, round 41-42 — this is the whole bug)

In _face_stream (world.gd:15108), the per-face row count is:

var rows: int = mini(SphereMath.CHUNKS_PER_FACE, int(ceili(win / S)) + 1) if is_x_face else mini(SphereMath.CHUNKS_PER_FACE, int(ceili(win / (S * 0.5))) + 1)

win is a distance in FLAT METRES (64 at sim_dist 4). S is the CELL size (face_cell_size). Dividing metres by cell size yields CELLS — but rows indexes CHUNKS, and a chunk is 16 cells. At the old 6.136 m cells the two were within a factor of ~10 of each other by coincidence (64/6.136 = 11 → 12 rows); at 1 m cells it becomes 64 → 65 rows, i.e. ~6× the chunk count, while eviction still sweeps a 128 m radius only after 16 m of travel. Creation outruns eviction and face residency climbs without bound: measured 79 → 499 with a stationary player, leak: true.

3. Edit shape

  • 3.1 One conversion, stated once. Express the chunk count as ceil(win_m / (16.0 * S)) + 1 (and the z-face half-width variant as win_m / (16.0 * S * 0.5) — keep whatever halving rule is there now, only fix the units). Prefer introducing ONE small helper (e.g. _chunks_across(metres, cell_m)) used by every site that needs it, so a future cell-size change cannot desynchronise them again — that is the durable fix, not the two edits.
  • 3.2 Audit the siblings. Any other place in _face_stream that turns metres into indices must use the same helper. List them in the deviations. Do not change a value that is genuinely in cells (e.g. ccx * 16 + 1 .. + 14, the & 15 local slot) — say which ones you checked and why they are correct.
