AC-0469 stage C — the twelve-chart lap

Coordinator contract. Medium builder applies it with tasks/templates/implement.md. Measurement only: extend the traversal arm, change no game behaviour. AC-0466 covered 2 of 12 boundaries and waived the rest; the user's requirement — "the experience has to be the same around the entire planet", now at a 1 m grid — cannot be evidenced on two seams.

1. Goal

Every one of the twelve chart faces is visited and every crossing between neighbours is measured for mesh presence, collision body, and crossing latency at the 1 m grid.

It covers the x_seam 4→5 and z_seam 10→11 boundaries and records the other ten as a waiver.

  • 4.1 Derive adjacency, do not assume it. For each face 0–11, find at least one neighbour by probing the cube map (walk along +u/−u/+v/−v and see which face world_to_face reports). Record the derived adjacency table in the deviations — it is a deliverable, and it also cross-checks the seam pairs the earlier tickets assumed.
  • 4.2 Cross every boundary the derived table gives (up to 24 directed pairs; deduplicate to undirected). If the 600 s budget cannot hold a full lap, cover as many as fit, report exactly which were covered and which were not, and state the wall time — a documented subset is acceptable, a silent one is not.

  • 4.4 The negative control must still fire — the arm's teeth survive the extension, or the extension is worthless.
  • 4.5 Also assert all 12 faces are REACHABLE: a short report of which faces were actually stood on. If a face cannot be reached within the step budget that is a finding, not a silent gap.
