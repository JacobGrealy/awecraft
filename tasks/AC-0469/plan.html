AC-0469 stage G3 — one creation pass, with the geometric rules as sub-rules

Coordinator contract. High-effort builder applies it with tasks/templates/implement.md. Step 3 of four. This is the step where stage F's named failure becomes live, so its gates are written around that failure rather than around tidiness.

1. Goal

Chunk creation is driven by one pass that consults the same resident_of membership and the same window as the sweep, with the three genuinely geometric face rules preserved as sub-rules — and no edge-crossing frame stall.

2. The failure to avoid (stage F, named in advance)

Losing the 12 ms budget stalls the edge-crossing frame: the home crossing sweep is one-shot, synchronous, flat-keyed and unbudgeted. Its correctness twin is a crossing-row void if the synchronous fallback is ever dropped. So this stage must keep work PACED where it was paced and keep the sync fallback untouched. A p95 crossing regression here is a failed stage, not variance.

3. The three rules that must survive (stage F classified them (b) — genuinely geometric)

  1. The edge-normal gate (~15296-15302): a face farther than win from its edge is skipped entirely.
  2. The per-face edge band rows (~15257): the in-band axis is full-width on x faces and half-width on z faces, and the row count comes from the ONE metres→chunks helper.
  3. The along-edge half-window with its mirror chart (~15270-15285, 15324-15333): each face covers only HALF the edge, the + half of a pair scales from 0 and the mirror half from -hw (the AC-0470 fix), and the z faces index the halved axis' 3,141-cell chart.
