AC-0469 stage F — what would it take for banded residency to own faces 0–11?

Coordinator contract. Medium builder applies it with tasks/templates/implement.md. Diagnosis and design only — no behaviour change. Everything else in this ticket is unified: one grid, one placement, one key shape, one node-creation path, one landing, one generation queue and drain, one travel gate and window. What is left is the last structural difference — the home lane owns banded residency for faces 0/1, and _face_stream owns a bespoke budgeted window for faces 4–11. This stage states precisely what merging them requires, so the implementation stage is not a guess.

1. Goal

A written, evidence-backed answer to: can the banded residency model run faces 0–11 in one place, what coordinates would it need, what does _face_stream do that the bands do not, and what would break first.

2. What to establish (each with file:line evidence)

  • 2.1 The coordinate question. Banded residency is computed around _rec_player_wx/wz in FLAT coordinates (home patch). A player on a chart face has flat coordinates only through the extended-flat chart (face_cell_band / the _face_player_flat clamp). Can one coordinate space express "the player's surroundings" for all 12 faces without special cases? Say what the space would be, or say why it cannot be one.
  • 2.2 The inventory of differences. List what _face_stream does that the band model does not: the edge-normal gate, the per-face edge band, the along-edge half-window, the budgeted pass, the separate _face_order/_face_evict_last bookkeeping. For each, say whether it is (a) subsumed by bands, (b) genuinely face-specific and must survive as a rule inside the unified lane, or (c) only needed because the current split exists.
  • 2.3 What the bands actually guarantee. Read stream_set / in_stream_set / the band radii and state what guarantees "the chunk under and around the player exists" today, and whether that guarantee is expressible per-face.
  • 2.4 The failure that would come first. Name the single most likely breakage of a naive merge, and the arm that would catch it. (Candidates to consider: the 16 m travel gate interacting with a band that is 98 m wide; the face budget disappearing so a wide band stalls a frame; the edge band near the home boundary.)
  • 2.5 A staged implementation order that keeps the world playable after each step, with the gate that proves each step.

5. Acceptance

6. Risks

The honest answer may be "these cannot be one model without a change to the band model itself" — that is a finding to report, not a failure. A design note that concludes "merge them" without naming the coordinate space and the first failure would be worse than no note.
