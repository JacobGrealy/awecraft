AC-0469 stage G1 — one resident_of(key) predicate (zero behaviour change)

Coordinator contract. Medium builder applies it with tasks/templates/implement.md. Pure refactor. Step 1 of the four-step residency merge designed at stage F, and the one step that does not depend on the open A/B choice (extended-flat vs angular bands).

1. Goal

The question "should this chunk be resident right now?" is answered in ONE place, used by both lanes, with every existing value and cadence preserved exactly.

2. Why this step first (from stage F's design)

Stage F established that the two lanes cannot simply be merged — they do not share a coordinate space, and three face-lane behaviours are genuinely geometric (edge-normal gate, per-face edge bands, along-edge half-window with its mirror chart) while only two are split artefacts (the 12 ms budgeted pass and the separate _face_order/_face_evict_last bookkeeping). Step 1 is deliberately the neutral one: it unifies the PREDICATE without touching the metric, so it is provably behaviour-preserving and does not pre-commit the A/B decision.

4. Edit shape

  • 4.1 One predicate, two callers. A function taking whatever a caller can supply — for the home lane a flat chunk (cx,cz) plus the player's flat position; for the face lane a face plus (ccx,ccz) plus the player's flat position — returning whether that chunk belongs in the resident set under that lane's CURRENT rule. If the two rules genuinely differ in metric (stage F says they do), the predicate may dispatch on which space the chunk is in, but it must not change either answer.
  • 4.2 Identical answers. For a set of sampled chunks, the new predicate must return exactly what the old inline test returned. State how you verified this (an arm, a temporary assertion, or a diff of the two expressions) in the deviations.
  • 4.3 No value moves. Same radii, same margins, same cadence, same travel gate.
