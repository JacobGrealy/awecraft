4. The multi-fire finding (the ticket's hypothesis #1 — CONFIRMED)

ac365_tickn (20 Hz ticks fired INSIDE one _process frame) over the 20 worst frames: {1: 3 frames, 2: 15 frames, 3: 2 frames} — 17 of 20 fired more than once. The tick is driven by accumulated wall time with a catch-up of up to 4 (TICK_MAX_CATCHUP), so a stalled frame that accumulates > 100 ms runs 2–3 full ticks back-to-back. Per-tick cost from the data: tickn=1 frames 3.7–5.0 ms; tickn=2 frames 5.1–7.8 ms (≈ 2.6–3.9 ms each); tickn=3 frames 7.5–9.3 ms (≈ 2.5–3.1 ms each) — ≈ 2.5–4 ms per tick, so the consumer scales WITH the stall rather than merely contributing to it: every 50 ms of backlog the frame pays another ~3 ms of main-thread tick work.

5. The engine-side gap (STEP 2 — a number, unchanged, not fixed here)

All six brackets sit inside World._process, so the arm-side frame wall (which includes physics over the collision bodies, render-server bookkeeping, and scene-tree work) still exceeds the _process total by the ~16–17 ms AC-0352 measured; these brackets change that reading in no way (they measure strictly inside the partition, and the MISC reconciliation stayed exact on all 20 frames). It remains a different instrument problem — FrameTime-style profiling or an engine-side physics-step bracket — deliberately left to its own ticket. Nothing in this run touches it.

6. What remains unattributable, and the instrument gap (GC)

  1. Display-rounding floor: the derived remainder is 2.5 ms over 20 frames (0.125 ms/frame), and the split's 12 fields are reported at 0.1 ms precision — the true unbracketed _process work (loading/banana ticks, the idle predicate, deferred frees, tex refresh) is each well under 0.1 ms on this population. There is no hidden consumer in MISC on the current tree.
  2. GC-class pauses: NOT measurable in-process — an instrument gap, stated as the ticket allows. Godot's script GC runs on the main thread but exposes no pause-duration counter to GDScript; a bracket cannot wrap an engine-internal collection, and a crude proxy (object-count deltas, Engine.get_total_object_count()) sees the allocation, not the pause. The instrument that would be needed: the Godot profiler's script-GC category (profiling build / the editor profiler export) or an engine-side timer. Note the shape evidence: with the tick bracketed, MISC is a steady 3.8–9.4 ms that tracks tickn almost exactly — the "bursty unattributed tail" GC hypothesis is not supported on this tree; whatever GC pauses exist, they do not own the residual.
