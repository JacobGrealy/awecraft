AC-0384 — plan, round 6 (2026-10-06): the sustained descent passage

Goal. A descent at the user's configuration (R24, fog full at 332 m) must pass through structured cloud over a sustained altitude band — the deck census (ON−OFF pixel diff, % of frame) must show present, structured cloud at every ladder altitude (2600/1600/700/300/120 m) at the reference weather, and dense at some altitude at every observed weather — by making the three proven shells (4275/4330/4400 about (0,−4000,0); radii/centre/transforms untouched) read as three distinct layers, each carrying its own structured coverage.

Diagnosis (measured, round 5 = results §23)

  • The r8b deck census at CLOUD_T0=411200: 2600 m 10.58% (thick belt core — the NMS orbit read), 1600 m 0.000%, 700 m 0.000%, 300 m 4.36% (thin veil), 120 m 0.22% (limb strip). The descent crosses one wisp and then falls through empty sky.
  • Geometry read of the preset views (camera radius 4141+alt; the deck is the shell wall the view rays cross): 2600 m sees the lower 46% of the frame as deck, 1600 m the lower 63%, 700 m the lower 81%, 300 m the lower 75%, 120 m the top horizon strip (e ∈ [−16°, +21°] below horizontal — the camera is 14 m inside the inner shell). The 1600/700 m zeros are not a cull or view problem: the base field is below the 0.46 coverage threshold in those deck regions at this weather, and the round-5 drift scan (6 clock values) found the belt limb in a clear phase at every phase — one weather draw cannot be relied on.
  • All three shells currently sample the same fbm structure at their own feature scales (u_scale3 = 2π(R+h)/feature, 256/160/110 blocks) — the decks are correlated views of one field, so a clear phase in the field is a clear phase in all three decks at once: one thin band.

The fix — per-shell pattern offset (the vertical phase), shader-side; window only if the mirror demands it

  • godot/core/cloud_layer.gdshader: new uniform vec3 u_phase (default 0 = today's pattern); the sampling coordinate becomes q = dr * u_scale3 + u_phase. Each shell then carries an independent structured pattern (the offsets are incommensurate lattice shifts, so the three decks decorrelate) while the latitude banding (shared dr.y term) keeps the decks' belts aligned in latitude — the layers read as a stack of banded decks, and the union of three independent coverages is dense where one field's phase is clear. The r5 distance-LOD clamp, the r6 contrast chain, the boost, and every shell geometric parameter are untouched. If the calibrated mirror shows the reference-weather 1600/700 m regions sit more than ≈0.02 below the window's lower bound, the coverage window widens one notch (0.46/0.54 → 0.45/0.55 or 0.44/0.56) — decided by measurement, reported either way.
  • godot/scenes/main.gd: one hunk, in _place_clouds — push u_phase per shell (constants beside the existing u_scale3/u_drift pushes). The top shell keeps phase 0 so the orbit view (and its grain baseline) is the measured r8b one; the 330 m and 275 m shells take the new phases (the decks the descent actually crosses). Listed verbatim in the results for hunk staging; no other main.gd hunk.
