AC-0384 — plan, round 8 (2026-10-05)

Goal. Kill the fog-wall / dome discontinuity and the near-empty 120 m cloud view: the near field must resolve into the SAME colour the far dome and the limb haze present (one source for sky/fog/haze colour), and a descent at the user's configuration (radius 24, fog full at 332 m) must pass through clearly visible cloud — with the deck's measured radii/centre/clearance (4275/4330/4400 about (0,−4000,0)) and the r2 distance-LOD grain (4.71/1.90 @ 2600 m) untouched.

Diagnosis (measured, this round)

  • JOB 1 — the seam is a WEIGHT failure, not a colour failure. The annulus (far tier, 128–384 m) is 100% engine fog above ~138 m: a flat fog-colour ring. The satellite body just outside it renders haz = haz_a + (1−op) ≈ 0 there (op → 1 beyond the render edge; haz_a is limb-only) — crisp terrain against the flat wall. Re-probed the round-4 frames (m384-on-d*.png, exact preset pose): the annulus projects at d_min ≈ [143, 860] @ 2600 m, [649, 1949] @ 700 m, [1120, 2833] @ 300 m, [2033, 3305] @ 120 m (law of sines); the colour step across each seam is the measured discontinuity (reported before/after). Making the fog colour equal the haze colour would NOT fix it — the body's haze WEIGHT is ~0 at the seam. The consistent fix: the far tier itself bridges, by a crossfade across the annulus, from the engine-fog model (inner edge — identical to band A at the same view distance) to the satellite body's own haz model (outer edge — identical to the dome). Then the chain is one fade: band A (engine fog) → annulus (crossfade) → dome (body haz) → limb haze → sky rim, all toward the same air colour.
  • JOB 1b — the colour source. The fog colour (DayNight.sky_display, a day/night/dusk lerp) and the sky's horizon (AeroLib horizon + haze + space) are TWO models that happen to be close (measured: fog (177,240,255) vs sky-near-horizon (197,238,246) @ 700 m). Per the brief, one source wins: AeroLib.fog_display(t, S) = the sky pass's exact h = 0 output (horizon mixed with the haze by haze_amount, then to SPACE_SKY by S²) feeds env.fog_light_color AND the body's u_air.
  • JOB 2 — the 120 m frame is sky-only (the planet preset looks at the tangent limb; the frame spans 52.5°–127.5° from the zenith = the mid-latitude belt of the shell) and the pattern there is 0.19%: the 11-cycle latitude band sits mostly out of phase in-frame (its dense cap is the out-of-frame zenith cap) and the coverage window (0.50/0.58) passes little of the ≈0.485-mean fbm field. Fix in cloud_layer.gdshader only: band cycles 11→5, band floor 0.5→0.75, coverage window 0.50/0.58→0.46/0.54. All low-frequency / threshold changes — no new high-frequency term (grain-safe); the r5 distance-LOD clamp (2.5 px octaves) is untouched; shell geometry untouched (the satellite arm re-asserts radii/centre).
