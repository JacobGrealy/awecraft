AC-0384 — plan (round 2, 2026-10-04)

1. Goal

Make the satellite body's surface coincide with the terrain surface it replaces: the body is currently a sphere at R + SEA (sea level) while the terrain the player stands on is at R + H with H > SEA (spawn top 141 at seed 44), so the body renders as a shell H − SEA metres below the ground — a small pale sphere detached from the play area with a sky gap at the horizon (the user: "a much smaller sphere than the high LOD … its surface is nowhere near the actual play area"). The fix displaces the 12-face body mesh onto the baked terrain height field (16 m grid) and carries the height channel in the texture alpha; the acceptance is a ground-level Forward+ capture at the player's eye showing the body surface meeting the terrain at the horizon.

Hypothesis status (tested, not assumed): code reading already shows both radii derive from Game.planet_R (body: world.gd:4768 → satellite_body.gd:265/897 with R + SEA; terrain: sphere_math.gd:362/369 — a flat point at height y sits at world radius exactly R + y), and the user's own satdiag (seed 5432) shows the body node at pos=(0,-4000,0), scale=(1,1,1), R=4000. So the node transform is NOT the bug and the R sources agree — the "different sources" are the height sources: the body uses the constant SEA, the terrain uses the actual column height H. The arm now measures both world-space radii at the player's position (the measurement section 16 skipped) and gates |body_nadir_radius − terrain_top_radius| < 4 m; pre-fix that is ≈ −16 m (red), post-fix ≈ −1 m (green).

Risks / edge cases

  • Bake wall on this box (196,196 × generate_far ≈ 18–20 min per the AC-0363 per-chunk measure): the ship run and the seed-5432 run get 1800 s timeouts; the render uses seed 44 (res path, no bake) so its 1200 s budget holds even at ~2–5 FPS with the 1.7 M-vertex body.
  • Main-thread geometry build on the load path (1.7 M verts at R=4000): the per-face grid (≈100 ms) is built during the existing one-face-per-frame LOAD loop (12 frames), not all in the final frame; the ArrayMesh array copy stays in the final frame (~100 ms). A one-time entry hitch, reported in the results if any arm wait tightens.
  • Re-ship changes the shipped bytes: the deterministic bake must keep the RGB channels byte-identical (the _pixel colour path is untouched); verified by an RGB pixel diff of old vs new before the files are replaced; rebuild_vs_shipped re-baselines to the new set (0 diff by construction).
  • Seam precision: the body's 16 m grid carries the per-texel H (1 m home / 6.135 m face lattice, bilinear onto 16 m) — the residual vs the terrain's 4 m far-tier pooling at the seam is ≈ ±2–4 m typical (0.14–0.29° at 400 m) vs the pre-fix 16–24 m (1.4–3.4°); the worst case is a 16 m-scale cliff at the horizon (rare, visible as a subtle step — noted, not chased in this ticket).
  • Legacy caches/res: the _h cache token forces a one-time re-bake for old caches (loud in SATDIAG); a legacy RGB res image falls back to the constant sphere with a loud line — graceful, never a silent white ball.
  • Lavapipe frame cost of the 1.7 M-vertex body during the drain: if SNAPDRAIN not fully drained appears, the shot still fires at the cap (the near terrain settles inside-out first) — the marker + frame count are reported honestly.
