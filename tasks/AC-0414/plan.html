AC-0414 — satellite bake before load, multithreaded — plan

The ticket notes (TASKS.yaml) are the specification; this plan follows them. Deviations, if any, are recorded in the results page and continuity.

Goal

  • Clause 1 — a world whose seed is not the shipped one bakes its planet texture before the player is handed the world, on all cores, with the loading screen saying what it is doing. The release gate (the sim taxi diamond, main.gd _await_sim_band — fenced) is gated on the body's phase from the world side: while a bake is in flight inside the loading window, the diamond's columns are never dispatched (the loading-window drain phase 1 only ever dispatches real-band columns, so this holds exactly the release set). Bounded: a 42 s hold budget (the 3000-frame release cap is 50 s wall) releases the diamond with a LOUD line; the bake tail then runs at a 4-worker cap so streaming keeps its share. The player is never released into a silent late planet.
  • Clause 2 — while the body is not LOADED the sky must not fake a planet: the sky's limb term (the clamped impact-parameter term is a filled disc — the "pale ball" class) is multiplied by the body's LOADED state, pushed from the _ac0401_push seam like the other limb uniforms. The correction's open question (what paints the disc over a rendered body) is identified with the single-launch A/B renders (LIMB=0 / GRADE_GLOW=0 / SAT_BODY=0) — a finding, reported; a fix for it would be a third clause.
  • Switch — one player setting sat_preload (Options > Settings, persisted, default ON — the user asked), env override AWECRAFT_SATPRELOAD=0|1 (the AWECRAFT_RAMPS pattern). OFF is the pre-change path literally: the old single-task low-priority _bake_worker runs unchanged, no hold, the shader gate uniforms at their pre-change defaults (mix(1.0, x, 0.0) == 1.0).

Load-time budget targeted, and how it is measured

Baseline (this box, 6 cores): the release gate (sim taxi diamond) is 2089 ms at the user config (AC-0313 re-measure; 959 ms harness default). The old bake: ~794 s worker wall (AC-0384 r2, same box, the exact code the OFF switch keeps). After: the release wall = min(bake wall, 42 s budget) + the diamond build (≈ 1–2 s at the load caps); the readiness wall (boot→body-LOADED) ≈ shards (~4–6 s) + 12 faces at 6 workers (~110–120 s) on this box, the tail at 4 workers after a late release. Target: readiness a multiple of ~3 min or less here (vs ~13 min), and the release wall never more than ~44 s (bounded + loud). Measured by the temporary satboot probe: t_loaded (first frame phase==LOADED) and t_band (first frame the diamond is meshed — the exact _await_sim_band condition) from boot, both switch states, cold seed 5432; OFF runs to completion for the fresh before-walls. The bake's own bake_stats (wall_ms, per-stage ms, the new mt field) is reported from every run.
