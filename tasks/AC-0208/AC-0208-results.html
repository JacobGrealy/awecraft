  • Keep-list honored: the AWECRAFT_MERGE=0 check in build_mesh is a harness A/B (kept); _gen_unit/<_materialize_chunk_data> sync gen is the documented spawn contract (now a pure C++ generate_flat call); all AWECRAFT_FRUSTUM code untouched.
