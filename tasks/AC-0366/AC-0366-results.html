2. What prices what — instrument notes

  • Per-stage prices are measured — the permanent C++ atomics (g_t_*_us in the shipped generate_resl, gen.cpp) divided by 25, as AC-0292/AC-0360/AC-0363 did. No python/GDScript transcription of the generation was written or believed (the AC-0362 trap, applied).
  • Per-point / per-octave / per-field prices are static allocations of the measured field-stage total over a counted number of noise evaluations. That is legitimate because every field on both lattices is built from the same primitive (vnoise3, gen.cpp 365; fbm3 = oct × vnoise3, 396; vn3 = n nonzero-amplitude × vnoise3, 417) — the field stage is pure primitive work, so one uniform price per vnoise3 is exact by construction, not an assumption about fields. The per-field split of the 1,503 µs is not measured by the existing instruments (the field stage has one accumulator); the measurement that would price it is the ticket's own STEP 0: one wall accumulator per cave-family build call site beside g_t_aquifer_us (additive, no world change). Recorded as a finding, not a failure.
  • Two unit counts are bounded, not measured (no counter exists): the number of scan blocks per chunk (bounded via the thash H-band, §4.4) and the number of aquifer searches per chunk (bounded via AC-0291's census, §4.5). The counters that would price them exactly are named in §4.4/4.5 — one line each.

8. Which candidate to attack first — handed over, not decided

(d) — the per-block aquifer Voronoi search. The number: 1,600 µs/chunk — 29.1% of the total 5,502, 67.7% of the 1.84× regression, ~14,057 searches × 113.8 ns, the single biggest named term in this codebase's history, and 29.1% of today's total by itself. It is also the only candidate that (i) grew during the window, (ii) is not frozen by a prior decision (the lattice resolution, octave counts and field count are AC-0344/AC-0359 "must not move"), and (iii) has a live fast path already (the 84% sky-block skip) whose boundary (max_fluid) is the natural first lever. A fix that moves fluid placement is a separate ticket with the full genhash/genprobe/farab/ladder/thash gate set.

Step-2 record — costs the numbers say are avoidable without changing output (recorded, not implemented):

  • The margin recomputation — priced 676 µs/chunk, capped at ~737 µs: 1,176 of the 2,401 cave points (48.9%) are the 1-cell xz margin, rebuilt per chunk for all 9 fields (1,176 × 25 oct × 23.0 ns = 676 µs); each of them is a CORE point of a neighbor chunk, so the same 25-octave value is computed up to 4× per region (the coarse lattice has the same 48.9% margin, 216 of 441 points). A cross-chunk lattice cache — a chunk computes its own 5×49×5 core (1,225 pts) and reads its margin from neighbors — caps the saving at ~49% of the field stage: (30,625 + 2,700) evals × 23.0 ns = ~767 µs vs 1,503, i.e. ~737 µs/chunk. It is a structural change to the per-chunk build, not a hoist: separate ticket, full gate set (the values must stay byte-exact; genhash 25/25 is the proof).
  • The AC-0360 precedent is the template: a gate in the right place (tunnel deep-only) already bought −392 µs by removing 3 shallow-band lattice reads per scanned block. The remaining scan deep-band reads (layer + tunnel×3 + pillar per deep y — the deep band k ≥ 16 is ~80–85% of the scanned blocks, H ∈ [118, 164]) are the same shape of lever — but every read there buys a feature (the pillar max, the tunnel pierce), so each removal is a fidelity decision, not a free win.
  • The aquifer search's 12-candidate window (2×3×2 in the 3×35×3 table) is the per-search cost's shape (113.8 ns); narrowing it changes fluid placement → separate ticket.
