Scope of this piece

  • Make the surface-walk model and the 6-DOF flight model one model: a single blend factor driven by altitude, interpolated between <~500 m surface walk + step and >~2000 m 6-DOF thrust/roll (the ticket's spec).
  • Compute the altitude bands on radial altitude |pos − C| − R (the same number on every face) — not flat-Y, which breaks the moment the player flies over a fold.
  • Reconcile the EXISTING flight knobs (the flight already exists — double-tap / fly-action toggle, altitude-keyed speed, its own settings). Do not assume a blank slate.
  • Line the 6-DOF band up with the atmosphere (the sky/fog's visible edge), not an arbitrary number.
  • Verify: takeoff, a high-altitude 6-DOF excursion, and a landing on a DIFFERENT face, with the band numbers reported — extending the spherewalk arm.

The one model (bands, in radial terms)

Radial altitude — the altitude of record for the bands:

C      = (0, −R, 0)            # the planet centre in the global frame (the frame shifted by (0,−R,0))
alt    = |pos − C| − R = (pos + (0, R, 0)).length() − R   # metres above the surface, SAME on every face
band   = smoothstep(500.0, 2000.0, alt)                   # 0 = surface, 1 = 6-DOF, smooth between

The bands, in radial terms:

BandRadial windowWhat it contains
Surface walk (band = 0)alt ≤ 500 mFull radial up-alignment (local Y slewed to the exact radial), full gravity −up·26, tangent-plane WASD + auto-step (≤0.5 m home / 1.0 m face) + crouch edge guard, jump. Byte-identical to piece 1 (at alt ≤ 500 the blend is 0, so nothing moves).
Blend (0 < band < 1)500 < alt < 2000 mThe up-alignment slew rate and the gravity both scale by (1 − band) — the transition is the rate-limited slerp (SPHERE_SLEW 12 rad/s), never a snap, so takeoff and landing are seamless on ANY face.
6-DOF (band = 1)alt ≥ 2000 mNo up-alignment, no auto-level — the basis is FREE (the player banks/points without the frame fighting them). No gravity (thrust, not weight). Body-frame 3-axis thrust: WASD in the tangent plane + A/SHIFT radial. Flight speed = WALK·6 (cruising, since 2000 > 275).

Why 500 / 2000, and why radial:

  • Radial, not flat-Y: on a planet the altitude the bands key on is |pos−C|−R — the distance above the surface, which is the same number on every face. A flat-Y altitude is only right near the home patch; a few hundred metres past a fold the ground has curved down and the flat-Y no longer equals the height above the surface, so a Y-based band would mis-blend (a player over a fold would read the wrong altitude). The band is computed radial, stated here as the ticket requires.
  • 2000 m (the 6-DOF edge) lines up with the atmosphere, not an arbitrary number: the atmosphere's visible edge today is the depth-fog boundary (full fog 714 m at the R50 render edge 800 m) — the sky/fog is distance-driven, not altitude-driven (checked above). 2000 m sits well ABOVE that fog boundary, and at R = 4000 it is the altitude where the planet's horizon curvature reads clearly as a curve in space (horizon distance d = √(2Rh + h²) ≈ 4.47 km, far past the 800 m render edge). It is also the design-of-record window (docs/planet-epic.html, story 2 §09 T6; the same 500/2000 m window the cancelled AC-0146 impostor-LOD used). So "above the atmosphere" in the 6-DOF model coincides with being visually above the depth-fog atmosphere and seeing the horizon bend.
  • 500 m (the walk edge) is the top of the band where terrain detail, per-slab collision, auto-step and the crouch edge guard still matter — the "in the atmosphere, near the ground" region. Step assistance is intrinsically a surface-band feature (it is already gated on is_on_floor(), which is only true at the surface).

What 6-DOF means here, precisely: the existing flight already provides body-frame 3-axis thrust (WASD in the tangent plane + A/SHIFT radial) — that is kept. The band's job is to REMOVE the up-alignment above 2000 m (the basis is free, no slew to the radial, no auto-level) and REMOVE gravity (thrust, not weight). The rotational freedom ("roll") is the free basis — the player's view (pitch + yaw) is unconstrained and the frame no longer levels. There is no dedicated bank key in the existing input model and adding one is out of scope; the free basis is the 6-DOF rotation the ticket calls for.

The run 1 fly-heading bug (arm-side, found + fixed): crossed_midline:false + land_x:231.3 — the flyer flew +x 207 m instead of crossing the fold. Root cause: the FREEZE PROBE sets p.basis DIRECTLY (to misalign it 15°, then to restore it), which BYPASSES the player's yaw tracking (_applied_yaw — the yaw already baked into the basis). After the probe, _applied_yaw is STALE, so the arm's p.look(PI/2) (the fold-crossing heading) computed the yaw DELTA PI/2 − _applied_yaw against the wrong baseline and rotated the basis to a +x heading. Fix (arm-side, no product change): after the descent shortcut, set the basis to a clean frame (radial up, heading −x) AND re-sync p._yaw = p._applied_yaw = PI/2, so the crossing flies deterministically. look() itself is unchanged (it is a no-op once the yaw is re-synced).

The walk skips:12 (bound < 12) is piece-1's walk HEALTH bound (the walker's blind-reflex avoidance skipped 12 waypoints this run) — the walk physics is band 0 and byte-identical to piece 1 (which measured 11 across three consecutive runs); it is a run-to-run variation, not a piece-2 regression.
