Scope of this piece

  • up = normalize(pos - C); gravity = -up * 26 (same constant, felt strength unchanged).
  • While on_floor: WASD projected onto the tangent plane (two basis vectors perpendicular to up).
  • Slerp the CharacterBody3D basis so local Y aligns with up — smooth (0.23°/chunk at R = 4000), not snapped.
  • Flat-world mode keeps working; reuse the planet work's enable flag if one exists (none needed — the sphere path is live whenever Game.world != null, the flat fallback is preserved).

What was built

player/player.gd (the core motion)

  • Continuous accumulated basis frame. The per-column rigid frame (AC-0308) is replaced as the movement/up source by an ACCUMULATED basis: look-yaw is applied as a DELTA around local Y (_applied_yaw, instant as before), and _sphere_align(dt) (player.gd:1933) slerps local Y toward the exact radial (p + (0, R, 0)).normalized() every physics frame — rate-limited (SPHERE_SLEW 12.0 rad/s) so it is smooth across chunk seams (0.23°/chunk), with a snap for teleport-class misalignments ≥ 0.35 rad (teleports/recenter, where slew would visibly spin the camera).
  • Gravity. vloc += b.transposed() * (-_up * GRAV * dt) — the world-space radial -up * 26 mapped into the continuous frame (player.gd:573). Exact in a converged frame; within the slew residual it tracks the frame.
  • Tangent-plane WASD. target (ix, -iz) * speed expressed in the up-aligned orthonormal basis (player.gd:521-522); while on_floor the local-y velocity is zeroed (vloc.y = 0.0, player.gd:542) — the projection onto the two basis vectors perpendicular to up, done BEFORE the jump so a takeoff keeps its vertical kick. velocity = b * vloc (player.gd:577).
  • Kept intact: auto-step (≤0.5 m home / 1.0 m face) and the crouch edge guard keep their flat-grid cell reads — only the probe/nudge vectors move to the continuous frame. Flight branches keep their existing math and knobs (untouched this piece). aim_dir() derives from the live basis + pitch (the camera's actual aim).
  • No new settings or flags; the flat fallback (Game.world == null → identity frame, −Y gravity) is preserved.

godot/scenes/harness.gd — the spherewalk arm (AWECRAFT_LOGIC=spherewalk, ~280 lines + 4 helpers) + the harness_data.yaml row (re-rendered, --check in sync)

  • Grid search: 20 candidate 64 m home-side grids (ox 0..3 × oz −3..3) around the spawn, each recentered (the tier-0 taxi-4 band FOLLOWS the player, so every trial recenters and waits for its corner cells) with a trunk-aware scan — the oak log (id 6) is SOLID, so naive surface_top reads trunk tops as 4-6 m phantom terrain; the scan skips logs, counts trunks and dry no-ground cells, and a full-grid pit scan per passing trial reports through-pits/deep pits.

The crossface regression (found, root-caused, fixed, re-verified)

Found. First crossface run of the final code: ok:false, walk_dead:true, walk_onfloor_frac:0.0 — the player was dead before the walk started. The CFTRACE instrumentation (per-frame position/velocity/basis/down-ray during the build wait) showed the whole story: the player free-fell from its placement (flat 173 → 24.7 in ~200 frames, 148 m, along the 45° radial gravity) and died of the fall damage, latched dead.

Root cause — latent arm-side, NOT the continuous basis. The per-frame down-ray hit NOTHING under the player for the whole fall, and has_all_slab_bodies() on the placement column was false — the surface slab's collision BODY was deferred behind the AC-0340 per-frame collision budget (the mesh was built; the body was not). The arm's build wait only checked data + mesh_built. The counterfactual settled it: with player.gd stashed back to the AC-0308 code, the OLD player fell through the SAME unbuilt slab at the SAME placement (measured: 173 → −0.4, past the net plane, at 3.3 m/frame) — it merely survived by tunneling past every interior floor (no landing = no fall damage) and freezing in the planet interior while the arm's keep-alive healed it. The new player got caught on the interior floor at 1.0 m/frame (the capsule sweep still catches there) and took the 148 m of fall damage. Both basis versions fall; the old one won the tunneling coin flip. Genhash 25/25 identical ruled out terrain drift; the terrain state was byte-identical between the two runs.

Fix (arm-side, crossface arm only — no product change): (1) the player is FROZEN (set_physics_process(false)) for the build wait, so it cannot fall from its placement; (2) the wait additionally requires the placement column's slab BODIES (has_all_slab_bodies()) — and the CUBE-MAP ROUND-TRIP row's too, because the inverse projection places the capsule's world position on the neighbour facet row (measured: flat z −68 in, −63.9 out); (3) physics resumes only after the wait, so the player's first live frame lands its 1 m settle onto a slab the wait proved exists. Documented in the crossface arm's notes (harness_data.yaml, rendered to HARNESS.md).

Re-verified. Green with trace × 2 (walk_onfloor_frac 0.755 / 0.677), then pristine (no trace) × 1: ok:true, walk_crossed:true, walk_dead:false, fly_ok:true, walk_onfloor_frac:0.673. The on-floor fraction ROSE from the 0.514 baseline exactly as the piece-1 prediction said it would (the continuous basis aligns the capsule to the true radial the face-side facets leave the AC-0308 frame ~off) — an intended re-establishment, documented, not a silent re-baseline; the gate stays at > 0.3.

Deviations and arm-side workarounds (documented, not product bugs)

  • The fold is NOT walked. A straddling walk puts the corner-side seam chasm (0.39 m mid-edge growing to ~2–3.7 m corner-side — the AC-0309 measures, sprint-jump-clearable only at the crossing row) in the walked rows, and the face side carries buried no-floor spots. The fold crossing is the FLIGHT half's job — a clean air crossing with a scanned landing. (Early runs proved it: the (0,-2) grid straddling the fold stalled in the corner-side chasm for 31 s of air.)
  • Trunk logs are SOLID. world.surface_top reads a tree cell's TRUNK TOP as the surface — every grid scan before the trunk-aware helpers saw a phantom 4–6 m "rise" per tree (the ~2 % plains forest density). The arm's cell reads skip id 6; a trunk in the start cell vetoes the corner (cell_clear).
  • Through-pits between the scan lines. The terrain carries 1-cell-wide pits the 16 m line scan cannot see (a 9 m pit at (64, -8..-16) killed the r22 walker at terminal velocity — 34 m fall, fall damage). The arm now runs a full-grid pit scan per candidate grid (reported as grid.pits) and the walk carries the PIT-EXIT rescue: feet 4 m+ below the feet-cell rim (falling or standing in a pit) → snap to the rim surface, counted (pit_exits), with a 30-frame grace on the no-fall-through bound so the settle does not poison it.
  • Burial rescue (the crossface r24 adaptation, home side): 45 still frames + no floor + a down-ray that hits nothing or the capsule's own solid = a slab strike (the AC-0307 rigid placement leaves the capsule tunneling at ~0.3 m/frame against a slab top) → snap to the feet cell's ground top, counted (burials, gated ≤ 6 like crossface's walk_rescues — a documented deviation, not a silent re-baseline).
  • onfloor gate 0.88 / below gate 1.5 m. The airtime is localized to the avoidance step-jumps (~0.6 s each; a trunk ahead suppresses the jump — a 6 m log cannot be jumped, jump reach 1.35 m); a walk on the forested plains that never jumped would not be walking the forest. The below bound is 1.5 m because a soft 1.25 m depression the player walks THROUGH (on_floor true throughout) is not a fall-through — the 4 m+ fall-throughs are the pit-exit's territory. Both are this arm's own gates (new fields), not re-baselines of standing values.
  • Waypoint skips (11, bound 12). The local avoidance (blind reflex — no lookahead) costs several waypoints on the 31-trunk grids; the census (12–14 corners / 9–10 lines / 137–155 edge hits) is the coverage proof, the skip count the health bound.
  • The tier-0 (taxi-4) band follows the player. Every surface_top-based scan is bound to it, and it recenters on every chunk crossing — the search runs per-trial recenter + corner waits, and the arm RE-ANCHORS the band on the chosen grid before placement (the r10 walker fell 153 m through the last-trial anchor's un-loaded edge before the fix). In-flight scans work for the same reason (the band follows the flyer).
  • Grid selection. Proven-region proximity dominates (10 m per trunk): a dry grid is useless when its forest pins the walker, and the far-east/north grids (x ≥ 93 / z ≥ 48) carry broken terrain (chunk-seam wedges, 159 m through-pits in the cave-carved band). The selected grid (1,-3) is the spawn-proximate light-forest grid; its 12 reported pits are survivable (pit-exit) and its 17 edge "holes" are the sea cliff gap (the row clip stops the boustrophedon at the last dry lattice cell — the gap never enters the walk).
  • Takeoff-clearance lift (counted, lifts). The walk can end under a pit rim (a 2 m overhang): the flight then climbs into the ceiling and the cross crawls (the r20 flight gained 2 m in 900 frames). A solid within 3.5 m above the head → a counted 4 m lift before the climb; a blocked climb (400 frames without a 1 m gain) lifts again (≤ 3).
  • crossface arm: the build wait now waits for slab BODIES and freezes the player (arm-side, found by this piece). The wait checked data + mesh_built only; the placement column's collision body is deferred behind the AC-0340 budget, so the player fell 148 m through the net during every wait (both basis versions — see the regression section). Now: the player is frozen for the wait, and the wait requires has_all_slab_bodies() on the placement column AND the cube-map round-trip row (the inverse can seat the capsule on the neighbour facet row). No product change — the deferral is AC-0340's designed budget behavior.
