AC-0434 (stage 1) — what IS the per-cell seam band, to the millimetre?

Coordinator contract. Medium builder applies it with tasks/templates/implement.md. Measurement ticket: no game code changes. The ticket's own notes say MEASURE FIRST, and that two prior measurements disagree — 654–1290 mm (AC-0423) against 634–678 mm (AC-0427) — so the per-cell figure is unknown and the choice of fix depends on it.

1. Goal

For every seam cell of a measured seam: the extension E, the crack width the cell would have had without it, and the resulting overlap — per cell, so the range and its per-cell wobble are on the record.

2. What the ticket already establishes (do not re-derive)

  • The two chunks OVERLAP by design: top-face edge rows extend past the nominal footprint by E = K0[k][i] + K1[k]*t + SEAM_V_MARGIN, clamped to [0, 2.5] m (gdext/src/mesh.cpp:426-454, margins at :435/:436).
  • seam_ext_corner rewrites only px/pz (mesh.cpp:459-476) and the UVs pass through unchanged (:498-518, 533-562) — that is the smeared texture the user photographed.
  • The band is the SUM of both sides' extensions; AC-0423 measured 654–1290 mm across 9 pairs where the crack had been 552–558 mm; the AC-0427 round re-measured 12 seams at −634…−678 mm.
  • Armed only where the build ctx carries sphere_r (world.gd:3860/5607/6092, chunk.gd:1626) and only toward a neighbour with any surface on that edge (build_seam_ext, mesh.cpp:982-1055). The face-chunk lane sets no sphere_r and has no extension at all.

4. The measurement

  • 4.1 Per-cell E. For the seam pairs ac0423probe already measures (its 9 pairs), extract the per-cell ext_edge values it reports and tabulate them. That arm already exposes them — use it rather than recomputing.
  • 4.2 Per-cell crack width. For the same cells, the geometric gap the extension is closing: the horizontal distance between the two chunks' nominal edge positions at that cell's own height. Derive it from the same face_chunk_transform/placement the mesh uses (the LSQ chunk transform is what places these meshes), and state which transform defines it.
  • 4.3 The comparison the ticket asks for, per cell: E, crack, and overlap = E_left + E_right − crack. Report worst, mean and the per-cell spread. Say explicitly whether the 654–1290 mm and 634–678 mm readings are both explainable as different CELLS of the same distribution, or whether they genuinely disagree.
  • 4.4 The unbounded case. Also measure or state what happens to a cell whose neighbour has NO surface on that edge (§2's gating) — the extension is 0 there, so the crack is untouched. Report how many of the sampled cells fall in that class, because those cells cannot be fixed by tuning E.
  • RESULT keys: ok, pairs, cells [{pair, k, E_left_mm, E_right_mm, crack_mm, overlap_mm}], worst_overlap_mm, mean_overlap_mm, no_surface_cells, transform_used, fails.

6. Acceptance

A per-cell table that settles the per-cell band figure, explains or refutes the disagreement between the two prior measurements, and says how many seam cells the extension cannot reach at all. No fix is chosen in this stage; the ticket's three candidate fixes are decided by these numbers.

7. Risks

The two chunks' surfaces sit within millimetres on flat ground, so the WINNER of the overlap flips at the crease — that is the visual doubling. If the probe shows the overlaps are already small per cell while the render clearly shows a wide band, the band is NOT the sum of extensions and the ticket's premise needs restating: report that instead of fitting the numbers to the story.
