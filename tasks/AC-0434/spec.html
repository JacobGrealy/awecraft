Registry notes (verbatim, quoted):

1) User Story: Standing on a chunk line, a block and the block across the seam render stretched over
each other. In the reported case a crafting table sat flush with the sand surface on a seam: its
texture was smeared about half a block into the neighbouring sand, the neighbour's sand was smeared
back over the crafting table, and the seam ran away from the camera as a visible band with two crisp
edges. It reads as blocks bleeding into each other along the whole line, not as a gap or a hole. The
ground should meet itself at a seam, not double up.


2) Technical Details:

WHY IT HAPPENS (read from the code; the amounts are the ones the building tickets measured). AC-0423
closed the see-through seam by making the two chunks OVERLAP on purpose. The top-face edge rows extend
past the nominal 0..16 footprint by the V width at the corner's own height:

  E = K0[k][i] + K1[k]*t + SEAM_V_MARGIN, clamped to [0, SEAM_V_MAX] = [0, 2.5] m
  (gdext/src/mesh.cpp:426-454; SEAM_V_MARGIN 0.05 at :435, SEAM_V_MAX 2.5 at :436)

seam_ext_corner rewrites ONLY px/pz (mesh.cpp:459-476), and qwrite/qwrite_vc copy the UVs through
UNCHANGED (mesh.cpp:498-518, 533-562) - so the edge block's 1 m texture is stretched over up to 1.6-3.5
of geometry. Both sides do it toward each other, so the visible band is the SUM of the two extensions:
AC-0423 measured ext_overlap 654-1290 mm across its 9 seam pairs where the crack it replaced had been
552-558 mm, and the AC-0427 round re-measured 12 seams at gaps -634..-678 mm, i.e. crack + two
margins. On flat ground the two overlapping surfaces sit within millimetres of each other, so which one
wins flips at the crease - that is what makes each block appear to lie over the other in both
directions. AC-0423's own notes accepted this cost as "the UV stretch on the extension strip (hidden by
the fold crease)"; a block sitting flush with the surface ON the seam is precisely the case where the
crease hides nothing. AC-0427 then added the edge column's outward SIDE faces to the same displacement
(mesh.cpp:486-503) - note that qwrite_merged, the normal merged opaque path, still guards on fi == 2
(mesh.cpp:1871), so the side-face extension is live only on the per-cell emitters.

WHERE IT IS ARMED. Only where the build ctx carries sphere_r - the home worker ctx template
(world.gd:3860, 5607, 6092) and the sync build (chunk.gd:1626) - and only toward a neighbour whose edge
column has any surface at all (build_seam_ext, mesh.cpp:982-1055; a neighbour with no surface on that
edge gets no extension). The face-chunk lane sets no sphere_r and so has no extension at all.

THE FIX DIRECTION (decide with the measurement; do not assume which). The overlap is not geometrically
required - each side only has to REACH the crease, and it currently overshoots it by a full crack width
on each side.

  (a) Move each side by HALF the pairwise gap evaluated at the vertex's own height, keeping the
      existing grout: the band becomes ~100 mm instead of ~650 mm. Keep the FULL width toward any
      neighbour that is not armed, so a lane that forgets the key cannot leave a seam half-open - the
      unilateral guarantee is the reason the over-extension exists and it should survive the change.
  (b) Extrude each column along its own radial (filed in the one-world ticket, P2) - this makes the two
      sides agree at every height by construction and DELETES this whole apparatus (K tables, ring
      reads, sphere_r, margins, clamp).
  (c) Scale the strip's UVs so the smear is not visible - AC-0423 rejected this as the more expensive
      option ("the alternative being a new atlas band"), so it needs a new atlas band to be viable.

MEASURE FIRST. Confirm to the millimetre that the band is the height-times-frame-lean term plus
margins: 140 m x 16/4072 = 550 mm on 16 m chunks, with the per-cell K0 wobble coming from the
neighbour's per-cell edge-column tops (mesh.cpp:1008-1041). Record per-cell E against the per-cell
crack width, because the AC-0423 measurement (654-1290 mm) and the AC-0427 one (634-678 mm) disagree
in range and the true per-cell figure decides which fix is enough. The instruments already exist:
ac0423probe's ext_edge/ext_overlap_mm and the standing seamcensus.

DO NOT REOPEN THE CRACK. What this extension was covering is 552-558 mm of skybox visible through the
ground - the user's earlier report. The gate must assert BOTH directions per seam cell: gap <= grout
AND overlap <= grout, not overlap alone, and the render evidence must be the user's own vantage
(surface-flush block on the seam, standing on the line, camera above), before and after.

KNOWN HARD CASE. At a step across a seam the crease of the two PLANES is not where the two staircases
meet, which is where AC-0427's side-face extension does its real work. A half-rule has to state what
happens there rather than leaving it as an unmeasured edge case.
