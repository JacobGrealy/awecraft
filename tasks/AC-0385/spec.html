Do not assume a rewrite is needed - but do not assume a tweak is either.
KEEP THE ALREADY-SETTLED CONTRACTS: the planet's atmospheric look is defined by the depth fog the satellite tier uses (full strength by 714 m against an 800 m render edge at R50) and the whole-body tier opens above it; the satellite's dissolve is opacity-driven by view distance with a fixed 0.25 texel-to-chunk ratio, and clouds must not reintroduce a pop at that transition. Renders are NOT available on this box (llvmpipe is degraded), so evidence this by geometry, density and coverage numbers, and by a data-driven PNG if that helps the coordinator's eye - the coordinator looks at pictures, builders do not.
CONSTRAINTS: genhash 25/25 byte-identical (weather is not generation unless it is, in which case say so and rebase deliberately); the standing set green (G0 zero SCRIPT ERROR, farab h_mismatch 0, ladder 20/20, meshprobe 1.0, halo ok, lightstate 0/0, r16 all sub-flags with far_holes 0/0 both bands, boundary r4 in band, the battery); the satellite arm's ladder and ratio intact; and if the native lane is touched, rebuild + re-stage including the Windows DLL.
