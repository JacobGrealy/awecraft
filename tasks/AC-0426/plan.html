AC-0426 — trees render as black silhouettes in daylight — plan

Goal

The tree ~54 m ahead of spawn (world ≈ (12,−45), the AC-0425 f1 "blob") renders as a pure black silhouette in daylight. Measured mechanism (AC-0425, taken as given): the top two log cells are ring-enclosed by their own crown, so their side faces sample the zero of both direct terms — sky channel s = 0 (the crown occludes the column's sky flood) and block-light g = 0 (no glow source) — and the display formula L = 0.20 + 0.80·max(u_day·s, g) (the four chunk_lit_*.gdshader, AC-0128 structure) collapses to the flat 0.20 floor at every time of day. Multiplied by the dark bark/leaf atlas cells (measured this run: darkest log cell (56,43,24)/255, 38.5 % of the tinted leaf tile has a zero channel) and the Forward+ pow(2.2) pre-encode, the pixels land below 8-bit → exact (0,0,0). Acceptance: the same tree from the same viewpoint in daylight shows bark and leaf texture; trunk luminance profile before/after; the world's overall contrast measured afterwards; nothing that should be dark at night became bright; ships behind a switch, default conservative, off state provably the current behaviour.

Chosen option and why

Ticket option 2 with the time-of-day variant: when both direct terms are zero, the face falls back to a sky-ambient term that tracks the sky's actual day factor at that hour, instead of resting on the constant floor. Implemented as an additive third term, not a re-tuning of the floor:

L = 0.20 + 0.80·M  +  u_amb · u_day · step(M, 0),   M = max(u_day·s, g)   [1.0 iff M is zero]

DEVIATION 1 (recorded in the results page): the first implementation of the mask was 1.0 − step(0, M) — but M is non-negative by construction, so that mask is always 0.0 and the term was dead. The A/B measurement caught it (the ON frame was identical to the BEFORE frame); the second-pass mask was step(M, 0).

DEVIATION 2 (recorded in the results page): the strict-zero mask still produced a byte-identical ON frame — because chunk.gd:995 clamps the face light scalar to [MIN_AMB, 1.0] with MIN_AMB = 0.08 (chunk.gd:5, mesh.cpp:117), an unlit face never stores s = 0, it stores the floor (20/255 = 0.0784 after the 8-bit vertex-colour packing). Measured on the built R4 tree (4,461 vertices, zero at r = 0, r_min = exactly 20/255): AC-0425's "s = 0" was the conceptual model, not the implementation. The shipped mask is step(M, 0.08) — 1.0 iff the face is at the floor ("effectively unlit"); faces with real pull (≥ 21/255) are untouched.

  • Why not option 1 (raise the 0.20 floor): the floor is a user-directed value (AC-0128/AC-0135) and it also sits in the lit path (const+gain = 1.0 at noon) — raising it brightens the whole scene including full sun, the exact quiet-flattening the ticket warns about.
  • Why not option 3 (re-bake / different combination): re-baking the light or the vColor channels changes generation-fed data, costs a mesh re-bake and risks the genhash and farab/halo/ladder standing set for a look change; the defect is in the display fallback, not the bake.
  • Why this shape is the safe one:
    • Off (u_amb = 0.0): 0.0·x = 0.0, x + 0.0 = x in IEEE 754 → bit-identical to the current formula, proven by a pixel-identical A/B frame.
    • Night: the chunk shader's u_day = DayNight.day(t) is exactly 0.0 at night → the ambient term is exactly 0.0 at night → the night look is bit-identical even with the switch ON; a tree at midnight stays black (correct, per the ticket).
    • Any face with even a little direct light (M > 0) is untouched at any hour — open terrain is pixel-identical; only zero-light pockets (under-crown trunks, enclosed caves and hollows) brighten, and only in proportion to the sky (linear in u_day: 0 at night → the measured 0.35 at noon).
    • No bake, no data, no vertex colour touched → genhash 25/25 by construction (verified by run), no mesh re-bake, the standing set's light/bake gates see nothing.

Magnitude u_amb = 0.35 (the "ON" value, a world.gd const): at noon a zero-light face goes L 0.20 → 0.55 (≈ the shade of a face at half sky, matching how a real under-canopy trunk sits in the shade without reading as a hole). Measured atlas consequence: the darkest bark cell (56,43,24) at faceShade 0.8 renders ≈ (9,7,4)/255 — dark brown, readable, no longer 0; the bark midtones ≈ (19,15,9); leaf structure survives. The lower (open-column) trunk stays fully lit as before, so the tree reads bark-then-crown, not a hole-then-crown.
