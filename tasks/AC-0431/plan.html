AC-0431 — plan: black step faces along chunk seams

Goal

Establish the cause of the black faces at terrain steps along chunk seams (flagged by AC-0430, whose UV audit found those faces' texture coordinates correct — the darkness is a lighting artifact). Fix it and prove it with the user's view re-taken, face luminance before/after as numbers, a wider-scene contrast check, and a night check.

Hypothesis (cheap, tested first): the black step faces are unlit faces at the light floor. The light scalar is clamped to MIN_AMB = 0.08 (20/255 after 8-bit truncation) at mesh time, so an unlit face reads exactly the floor — and AC-0426's u_amb sky-ambient term fires exactly there (step(m_direct, 0.08)), lifting the display L from 0.2627 to 0.6127 at full day. That term ships behind ambient_sky, default OFF (AWECRAFT_AMBSKY=0|1 env). Evidence to reuse: AC-0430 measured the black face's vertex sky term ≈ 0.078 (the floor); AC-0426 measured the lift's safety (frame mean +0.15, ground band 0 differing px, night bit-identical because u_day = 0 kills the term).

If it holds: the fix is a change of default (settings.gd "ambient_sky": false → true) — no new code. Acceptance = the user's view with the switch in each state + the final default-boot view.
If not: the probe arm measures the face itself (baked vColor r/g/b + UV + light_at of the adjacent air cell) to discriminate the candidates (disagreeing neighbour light, wrong normal, different emit path, seamc corner-shading interaction).

What the faces are (working model, to be confirmed by the probe)

Modern per-vertex lighting (default ON) stores per corner: r = sl (geometric sky term, clamp(1 − 0.4·occl, 0.08, 1.0) — a corner buried under ≥ 2 neighbour column tops hits the floor), g = block light (0 unless glow evidence), b = oshade (0.5·FSH + 0.5·lam). Display: L = 0.20 + 0.80·max(u_day·r, g); ALBEDO = tex·b·L. A side face at the floor with a dark atlas cell and low oshade rounds to (0,0,0) in 8-bit — the black polygon. The lit neighbour band keeps its own (higher) scalar, which is why it renders brown while its twin renders black.
