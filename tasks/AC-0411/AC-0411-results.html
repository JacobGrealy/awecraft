1. What the wash actually was

THE WASH IS THE PLANET'S UNDERSIDE: the body mesh is wound inside-out, cull_back culls the entire near-side cap, and the far side (the −Y hemisphere) is drawn instead. At the far-side nadir the outward normal points away from the camera, so cosv = 0 → haz_a = (1−cosv)² = 1.0 → ALBEDO = mix(base, u_air, 1.0) = u_air exactly — the flat pale-cyan wash (144,209,221), independent of the texture.

The mechanism. _geom_for_face (godot/world/satellite_body.gd) chose each face's index order with a per-face corner test: outward = (p01−p00).cross(p10−p00).dot(p00) >= 0 — a 3D geometric-normal test. That selects the order whose indexed geometric normal points outward. But under Forward+ cull_back an indexed triangle rasterises as front-facing from outside exactly when its geometric normal (v1−v0)×(v2−v0) points toward the centre (the opposite side). The test was therefore inverted for the engine, on every face:

  • Faces 0/1 (the +Y hemisphere — the whole near-side cap) rasterised back-facing → culled. The cap the camera is looking at is never drawn.
  • Faces 2–11 rasterise their far side → the visible orbital "disc" is the planet's underside, seen where the cap should be.
  • Disc centre (nadir ray, far intersection at (640,575), d≈10215): the far-side nadir's outward normal is (0,−1,0), pointing away from the camera → cosv = clamp(dot(normalize(cam−p), NORMAL), 0, 1) = 0 → haz_a = 1.0 → the full u_air wash, exactly. The perfect flatness (659/665 samples byte-identical) is the flat far-side surface at haz=1 across the whole nadir region. The baked 71%-grass texel the ticket cites is a near-side cap texel — it is never rendered; the disc shows the far side's chart at haz=1.
  • The "green rim" of the real frames: far-side fragments near the silhouette, where dot(normal, view) rises from 0, so haz drops below 1 and far-side terrain shows.

Why the earlier attributions and theories were wrong (or incomplete). The three previous attributions of the "plain white ball" were real, separate, and already fixed (the shader that never compiled — AC-0391; the fog erasure — AC-0384 r3; the cloud mass in front — the body-off A/B). This is the fourth, and it is the one that dominates the daylit orbital view. The "shifted duplicate sphere ~2300 m below" and "12–14° hole" theories from earlier analysis were wrong: the disc is the far side of the true sphere — correctly positioned, correctly drawn-from-inside because of the inverted cull classification. A mid-analysis red herring is recorded for the file: the uniform (155,255,68) disc interior in the DIAG-12 face-id frame was the run's thick cloud deck (cov=0.750, the AC-0385 shell, transparent/no-depth, drawn in front), not a mystery surface — the thin-deck real frames show the far-side haze wash instead. No second sphere, duplicate, gizmo, or near-clip exists anywhere (swept: single SatelliteBody instance at (0,−4000,0) scale 1, op_nadir=1, camera near 0.1, the 10 m walk pad is 2 px at 2600 m).

3. The fix (files, and why this shape)

Primary — godot/world/satellite_body.gd, _geom_for_face (one line): the corner test's comparison is inverted, dot(p00) >= 0.0 → dot(p00) < 0.0. Every face now uses the index order the engine rasterises as front from outside; the near hemisphere (including the whole +Y cap, faces 0/1) draws, the far side is culled — one front patch per view ray, no self-ordering problem. The per-face selection structure is kept (the 12 charts have different 3D handedness, so the per-face test is still required; only the engine-convention comparison was wrong). The change is mesh-index only: vertex positions, UVs, and normals are untouched (normals were already correct outward, nrm.append(p / rr)).

Kept alongside — godot/core/satellite_body.gdshader, u_srgb_pre (fix v1 from the first half of this task): the baked face bytes are sRGB-companded (the bake's _l2s) but the upload is a linear-format texture (ImageTexture.create_from_image, no sRGB flag), so the sampler returns them as-is and the Forward+ output encode re-compands them — a genuine double companding on the daylit terrain term. The shader now pre-decodes the terrain term (texv = mix(texv, pow(texv, vec3(2.2)), u_srgb_pre)), decode-then-scale (the day factor is a linear light scalar, not part of the display colour; at sun_l=1 the two orders coincide, so this agrees with the AC-0242 convention exactly where it is measured). u_air is NOT pre-decoded (already linear via Aero.fog_display), so the mixed ALBEDO is linear-domain, exactly like the annulus (lod_avg.gdshader). Pushed by _build_face as Aero.srgb_pre() — build-time push, the house pattern (main.gd pushes the same value to the other sRGB-domain materials); unconditional, no Settings row. Note: fix v1 alone was a byte-identical NO-OP on the wash (centre pixel unchanged) — consistent with §1 (the wash is haz=1 u_air, independent of the texel domain). It is a real, separate defect on the now-correctly-rendered cap, and it ships with the fix.

9. Deviations

  • The first-half hypothesis (the double sRGB companding as the wash's cause) was overtaken: fix v1 (u_srgb_pre) measured a byte-identical NO-OP on the wash — recorded as a deviation, kept as the separate real domain defect (§3). The mechanism was found by mesh census + face-id render + screen-space winding analysis, not by the shader bisect.
  • 12 diagnostic renders at 2600 T0.5 (diag1–9, diag12, clouds-off, meshprobe arms) were spent identifying the mechanism — each ~13 min, one godot at a time, all rc=0, all diagnostic code fully reverted before the final set.
  • The first SMOKE attempt (fix-v1 tree) died in the known stochastic UAF class (rc=134; signal 11, single libc stub frame, clean log, streaming context) — the documented engine-class signature, not an effect of this change (one uniform, one build-time push, and a mesh index order — none of it slab/streaming code); the re-run and the fixed-tree run both rc=0.
  • meshprobe2's 6 leaked ObjectDB entries at exit are the pre-existing leak noted in the HARNESS, not this change.
