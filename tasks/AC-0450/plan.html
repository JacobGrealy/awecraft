AC-0450 plan — airborne void census and coverage instrument

Builder: delegated subagent. Tree 2b2f048 (AC-0449 landed). This is an instrument, not a fix: a number for "how much of this frame is not drawn" at the vantage the user complains about, proven to be able to fail, then pointed at the live symptom. Nothing is fixed.

Goal

  • Per-frame pixel fractions at a fixed airborne vantage: (a) background / no geometry, (b) far-tier geometry (lod_avg bands), (c) near-tier geometry (chunk/fluid materials), (d) satellite body, plus the total. A contrast-with-distance profile (mean luminance delta between adjacent pixels, binned by camera distance) — the shared measurement AC-0449's acceptance wants.
  • A negative test demonstrating the counter RISES (it must be able to fail).
  • A four-state elimination (as shipped / sky air term off / engine fog off / satellite body off) reported as evidence — no pre-declared winner (the near-field handover and the blur reading both remain live).
  • A permanent air camera preset in the standing AWECRAFT_CAM set (the AC-0394-owed altitude preset), plus one row set in HARNESS.md §2.

How the counter works

Ownership is established by frame A/B elimination, not by colour (colour cannot separate fog-washed terrain from sky: both sit at the single-source air colour 205/250/255-class). Five deterministic frames, one vantage, CLOUDS=0, T=0.5 frozen, R24/FOG_PCT 83, 1280×720, Forward+ via lavapipe (the §2 recipe):

frameenv statewhat moves
F0as shipped— (the census frame)
F1AWECRAFT_SKYALT=0sky body only (background pixels; geometry is switch-blind)
F2AWECRAFT_NO_FOG=1engine depth fog only (near/far tiers in the [fog_near, fog_far] window; body is fog_disabled; sky untouched)
F3AWECRAFT_SAT_BODY=0the satellite body only (its pixels fall back to the background)
F4AWECRAFT_NO_WORLD_VIS=1ALL world geometry (the background reference = pure sky; also the "coverage disabled" frame)

Per-pixel (max-channel delta, tolerance 2/255; the planet-preset class is byte-identical across runs, so the tolerance is a noise guard, not a fudge):

  • (a) background = Δ(F0, F4) ≤ tol — nothing drawn in front of that pixel. Split analytically: (a)_sky = bg pixels whose camera ray misses the planet (above the horizon, expected) vs (a)_hole = bg pixels whose ray hits the planet surface distance — background where terrain is expected, the class the standing r16 far_holes census reports as 0/0 and the user photographs.
  • (d) body = Δ(F0, F3) > tol — the body drew there.
  • total geometry = 1 − (a). Fog-owned geometry = Δ(F0, F2) > tol (the evidence row for the engine-fog state).
  • (c) near / (b) far: geometry pixels that are not body, classified by per-pixel camera distance (ray→planet-sphere intersection from the preset's reported pose) against the tier discard wall: at 941 m every in-frame pixel is past the render edge, so (b)=(c)=0 by construction — reported with the measured min-distance evidence, not assumed. The near/far tier split (real band vs lod_avg bands) is only live below ~500 m; at the user's vantage the honest answer is 0/0 and the instrument says so.

Derive, do not restate: the arm prints the live derived thresholds — fog_far read off env.fog_depth_end (which main.gd pushes from DayNight.fog_far(render_radius, fog_start_pct), core/daynight.gd:49-50) and render_edge read off world.satellite.last_render_edge (derived at world/satellite_body.gd:931 as (rr+1)*16, the same home expression main.gd:1523 pushes). The Python side consumes the printed values; no arm hardcodes 24 / 332 / 400. The vantage itself is a stated choice (941 m = the user's documented altitude, 30° down-outward), not a derived constant.

Contrast profile: per-pixel camera distance (same ray→sphere model) bins the frame; in each bin the mean |ΔL| between horizontally/vertically adjacent pixels (L = luma). Reported F0 (as shipped) and F3 (body off = sky reference). A profile that stays high at range is the "clear and hazy far field" shape AC-0449 wants measured.

Post-process honesty: the preset camera is a bare Camera3D — it does NOT carry the player's CameraAttributesPractical (DoF), the same documented limit as the planet preset (AC-0449). Geometry ownership is DoF-invariant (blur does not discard pixels), so the census is valid on the bare camera; the contrast profile is therefore additionally measured on a player-camera frame (AWECRAFT_AIR_PLAYERCAM=1 — same pose, the real camera with DoF live) and the delta between the two profiles is the DoF's share of the far-field contrast loss. This is reported explicitly, as the ticket requires.

How I will prove it can fail (negative test)

  1. F4 (world hidden) is the negative test in the matrix: with no world geometry drawn, (a) must jump from its F0 reading to ≈100 % and (a)_hole to the whole disc area — the counter RISES to the maximum. If it does not, the instrument is broken and I say so plainly instead of shipping a zero.
  2. F3 (body off) is the partial rise: (a) rises exactly where the body contributed (the disc), the sky-above region stays background — the rise is spatially consistent with the (d) mask, not a uniform shift.
  3. The two rises are reported as the numbers, with the masks, against the F0 baseline.
