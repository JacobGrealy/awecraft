AC-0450 — airborne void census + coverage instrument

instrument, not fixvantage: 941 m radial, user's settings seed 44 · T=0.5 · R24 · FOG_PCT 83 · fog_far 332.0 m · render edge 400.0 m Forward+ lavapipe · 1280×720 · drain 60

What this is. A per-frame pixel-fraction instrument at a fixed airborne vantage, built exactly as the ticket spec demands: the fractions are measured by eliminating one world component at a time against the world-hidden reference frame — never by colour (colour cannot separate fog-washed terrain from sky: both sit at the single-source air colour (208,241,251), by design, AC-0401/AC-0429). The negative test (a counter that can be made to fail) and the four-state elimination (reported as evidence, no pre-decided winner) are the heart of the deliverable. Plus the camera preset the AC-0394 notes audit found owed: air is now a standing member of the AWECRAFT_CAM set.

3. Counter mechanics

Five frames at one pose (plus the elimination references at the context vantages); per-pixel, max-channel delta, tolerance 2/255:

FrameHookMasks it yields
F0 as shipped—baseline
F1 sky's air term offAWECRAFT_SKYALT=0 skybody = |F0−F1|>tol. Refines the background class: a pixel that reads as background and changes with the sky body is true sky; one that reads as background but does not change is fully-hazed geometry (the body's rim haze mixes toward the single-source u_air, which is the sky's air fill — indistinguishable by design, reported as its own class).
F2 engine fog offAWECRAFT_NO_FOG=1 fog-owned = |F0−F2|>tol (the body is fog_disabled, the sky is untouched by the fog — the delta isolates the fog's near/far-tier tinting).
F3 satellite body offAWECRAFT_SAT_BODY=0 (d) body = |F0−F3|>tol (the body's visible contribution).
F4 world hiddenAWECRAFT_NO_WORLD_VIS=1 (a) background = |F0−F4|≤tol (reads as background: nothing distinguishable drawn in front). This frame is also the “coverage disabled” frame — the negative test (no coverage pass exists yet to disable; hiding all world geometry is the maximum removal). The near/far tier classes are geometry-not-body; at 941 m they are measured, not assumed (their minimum distance vs the 332 m discard wall is in the output).

(a) also splits analytically: (a)_sky = background pixels whose ray misses the planet; (a)_hole = background pixels whose ray hits the planet — background where terrain is expected, the class the standing r16 far_holes census reports as 0/0 while the user photographs it. Documented model caveat: the ray model uses the bare R=4000 sphere; the actual surface is R+H, so the disc silhouette extends above the bare tangent by the terrain height at the tangent — measured at 941/30°: 44,736 px (4.85%) of visible geometry sit in that band (they count as geometry, correct; they only shift the sky/hole split).

The read: the sky reference is flat air (C ≈ 0 — no contrast exists in the background to borrow). The as-shipped profile therefore is the body's own far-LOD contrast: a ~3× collapse from the 961 m rim (1.96, nadir 3.49) to the 2728–2889 m edge (0.68, nadir 0.94). The user's far-field “wash” at 941 m is the body's baked far texture running out of contrast, not fog, not sky, and not (many pixels') DoF. At 300 m nadir the same instrument finds the far tier in frame: C = 10.38 at 300–370 m (the real band + band A, full-res) collapsing to 0.1–0.5 at 440 m+ (bands B/C, the 8×8/4×4 averages — near-flat in frame), with 11.5% of the frame in the tier class and 63.17% reading as background where the ray hits terrain. That is the AC-0451 handover-annulus shape, quantified, reported as context for the contested reading — not conflated with the user's 941 m vantage.

Honest reading, without a winner: at the user's vantage the frame is 67% background-reading / 33% visible body; the engine fog demonstrably owns zero pixels; the sky body owns 64.6% of the frame (it is the sky, not a leak); the tiers own nothing in frame because every in-frame pixel is past the 332 m wall; the remaining “pale” look the user reports is the body's own far-LOD contrast collapse (§5) plus the 2.38% by-design hazed band. Whether the user's complaint is that (the far LOD's lack of contrast — a texture/LOD problem) or the handover reading (the annulus — a tier-ownership problem, live at 300 m in §5) is a question this instrument now measures but does not answer: both frames and both counts are above, and the next ticket picks the fix to match the reading the user confirms.

7. Does the capture camera carry the player's post-process?

No. The preset camera is a bare Camera3D (main._make_camera(), fov/far set, nothing else) — no CameraAttributesPractical, no DoF, same documented limitation as the planet preset (AC-0449 proved it byte-identically). The ownership fractions are DoF-invariant, so the census is valid on the bare camera. The AWECRAFT_AIR_PLAYERCAM=1 frame (captures/f5-playercam.png, 90° base FOV — the frozen player gets no flight kick — with the DoF live) is the cross-check: it shifts 1.74% of the frame from visible-geometry to background-reading and moves the contrast profile by ≤ ~0.15 C units across all bins — the AC-0449 DoF fix (far wall 1500 m, 1400 m ramp) held; DoF is not the far-field collapse. (The playercam frames keep the game's own first-person model visibility; the model clips equally in F5/F5b/F5c and cancels in every A/B.)

8. Finding: the trapezoid (the instrument's own defect, caught and fixed)

First render set (R4 probe): a flat brown (96,68,38) trapezoid pinned to the frame top — present in every frame, including the world-hidden reference (it survived AWECRAFT_NO_WORLD_VIS). It is not world geometry and not the sky: it is the frozen player's head block, near-clipped — the preset camera sits at the vantage the player occupies. Left unfixed it would have sat inside the sky class of every census frame.

After the fix (player.visible = false in the preset-cam path only — the body's altitude uniform rides the player's position, not its visibility, so the hide is observation-free): the trapezoid is gone (0 brown pixels in the region, verified by scan) and the whole matrix was re-rendered clean. The lesson is in the HARNESS.md air row: a census camera and a census subject must not share a body.
