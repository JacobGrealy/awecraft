AC-0447 — night is readable but still too dark — plan

Goal

Night at true midnight (AWECRAFT_TIME=0.0, day factor exactly 0) must be dark and readable: ground luminance up out of the current ~7–13/255 band with the majority of ground pixels clearing 10/255, the day bit-identical, and the night keeping its depth (dark foreground, lit ridge). The single most important instruction governs measurement: establish the hour-and-day-factor curve first, because the hour that reads as dusk (t=0.75) still carries a day factor of 0.343 and a fix tuned against it is tuned against the wrong light.

Hour-and-day-factor curve (the condition)

Closed form, from core/daynight.gd (the one home): elevation(t) = sin((t−0.25)·TAU); day(t) = clamp((elevation(t)+0.12)/0.35, 0, 1). The moon's night weight is (1−day(t))·switch (world.gd block 6). AWECRAFT_TIME maps to Game.time_of_day = fmod(t,1) (main.gd, fenced — read only).

What the mapper is doing to the night values (the measurement)

The terrain is render_mode unshaded: the shader output is the mapper input. Pipeline (aero mode ON by default, core/aero.gd — not fenced): shader ALBEDO = (tex·faceShade·L)^2.2 (the AC-0242 sRGB pre-decode, u_srgb_pre=1.0 under Forward+) → engine ACES tonemap at exposure 0.85 → contrast 1.1 (pivot 0.5) + saturation 1.25 → sRGB encode → 8-bit. The light model at midnight: L = 0.20 + 0.25·moonLambert (u_amb term ×u_day = 0) → L ∈ [0.20, 0.45].

Hypothesis under test: ACES(exposure·x) under contrast 1.1 has a hard dead zone — display-linear input below ≈0.05 exits as 0/255 literally — and the night terrain's whole range (L 0.20–0.45, i.e. x ≈ 0.003–0.06 for the atlas greens) sits in or at the edge of it. So the input has structure (top faces 6–15× the side faces in L) and the output is mostly zero: the mapping is the ceiling, not the moonlight. Verified by: (1) the pure-python model of the exact pipeline (aero.gd constants + the shader's L formula + atlas texel values read from the texture); (2) pixel-verification of the model on measured day and night frames (a known face predicted vs its actual byte value); (3) the exposure question answered numerically — how much tonemap_exposure night would need to lift the L=0.20 floor out of the dead zone (≈×15 — which would white-out the day, so a shared exposure cannot be the fix; per-frame exposure adaptation would live in main.gd's per-frame sky update — fenced, reported not edited). The fix therefore goes where the values live: a night-only toe on the terrain's own display path, in the four unshaded chunk shaders (world/ — mine), identity by construction at day / switch-OFF (u_night_w = 0 → the exact pre-AC-0447 ALBEDO, IEEE bit-identical). Shape: a hue-preserving luma-scaled gain f = 1 + w·A·(d/(x+d))^p (decaying with luma — strong at the crushed floor, weak at the already-lit moonlit tops), parameters tuned numerically against the verified model + measured frames to land: night floor faces ≈ 8–18/255, moonlit tops ≈ 35–65/255, top/side display ratio ≥ 2.5, pure black (x=0) stays 0 (the sky and unlit voids stay black).
