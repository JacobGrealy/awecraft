Goal

  1. Local zenith. Re-key the day sky off the LOCAL zenith (radial away from the planet centre) instead of the world's up axis — horizon level, haze band, space blend correct on every face, not just the face centre.
  2. Atmosphere height A. One gameplay constant (starting 1200 m, the ticket's proposed point) every consumer derives from: sky's blue-to-space over [0.25A, A], stars full by A, sun glow dies with it, body's "fully established" (h_full) lands with "sky is space". The fog wall stays a render-distance artifact — said, not re-coupled.
  3. Minimal honest sky (spec C). Keep the sky pass, drop the fake gradient: two colours + one cone — below the local tangent cone (the R+SEA disc) the single-source air colour (Aero.fog_display, the value env.fog_light_color and the body's u_air take); above it SPACE_SKY; the cone keeps the existing limb term. Coverage gaps read as haze, not black.
  4. Switch (spec E). Reuses the existing sky_altitude setting (default ON): ON = the AC-0417 sky; OFF = the pre-AC-0402 expression bit-for-bit (incl. stars on the physics S, glow on the physics S). New env knob AWECRAFT_ATMO sweeps A without a save file. No new settings row.

Deviations (decided at plan time, to be proven in the results)

  • D1 — h_first stays fog-derived (the ticket lists it for re-derivation). The stated pairing in the ticket is "the planet is fully established" (h_full) with "the sky is space" (A) — h_full = A delivers it. h_first is the fog-wall crossing (a render-distance artifact, the same family the ticket says STAYS), and it is the AC-0401/AC-0400 seam: the body emerges exactly where the world fades. Setting h_first = 0.25A would gate the body node off until 300 m, but the per-fragment op is already 1 there (rim tangent distance √((R+0.25A)²−(R+SEA)²) = 1211 m > render edge 400/816 at both R24 and R50) — a full-opacity pop of a ~33°-wide disc patch, replacing the smooth 139–145 m fade-in. Proven by the satellite arm's ladder (unchanged rim case) + the measured op values in the results.

Acceptance measurements (the numbers, not an impression)

  1. Position pair (F1). Same altitude (700 m), same time, two well-separated positions — A = face centre (tilt 0.12°), B = +3400 m in-plane (tilt 58.2°, past the face edge): the sky strip at the SAME local elevation (rows above the disc limb, central columns). BEFORE: A bright pale (world-h≈0–0.10: horizon/haze class) vs B dark blue (world-h≈0.85–0.90: zenith class) — the tilt in pixels. AFTER: the two patches equal within noise (the sun-glow Δ computed per direction and subtracted). Repeated at 1200 m.
  2. Altitude ladder (F2), 5 altitudes up to A (300/400/600/900/1200). ws = the pushed u_space_sky (uniform, from the preset RESULT — the value the shader consumes) + the gap-ring pixel: at 300 m the ring (body op → 0) shows the pure air colour; at 400 m the same ring is darkened by (air−space)×0.0858 — the blend measured against altitude; at 1200 m the strip is the space colour (black above A). Star opacity on the same ladder (day 1: off at 300, full at 1200 — strip 99.9th-percentile) and the body's op_nadir (0 at 300, 1 at ≥526 — established by A) show the three land together at A instead of stragglng to 2000/3200 (the BEFORE ladder: pushed u_space_sky 0.016/0.310/0.874 at 700/1600/2600 — re-measured, plus stars on the physics S).
  3. Coverage gap (F3). Nadir-aim ring at 300 m: BEFORE = the old below-horizon branch (horizon×0.5, the dark class); AFTER = the haze air colour — and the ring pixels vs the engine-fog wall-band pixels in the same frame differ by ≤ a few bytes (the single-source air colour through both pipelines).

Protected, undisturbed. genhash 25/25 byte-identical pre/post (the placement/emit unchanged); farhigh leg 2 all FACET; farab h_mismatch 0; the fog-wall push untouched (code) + the wall band in the AFTER frames still on the air colour; the satellite arm's rim/dissolve cases at the unchanged h_first.
