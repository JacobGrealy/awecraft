Stated deviations and design decisions

  1. h_first stays fog-derived (deviation D1): the ticket says re-derive h_first/h_full against A. h_full = A is done (diagnostic-only — see F2). h_first gates the body's visibility (visible = h_band > h_first); re-deriving it against A gives sqrt((R+A)² + fog_far²) − R ≈ 1211 m — above the atmosphere top (A = 1200), so the rim would pop in at full opacity past the space transition. Kept at the fog window (139.3 m, unchanged).
  2. The fog wall stays render-distance-coupled (the ticket's own requirement, stated): op_nadir reaches 1.0 at 526 m (fog_far/render_edge = 332/400), not at A. A is a gameplay constant, not a render distance; no one re-couples them.
  3. Star opacity at day = the A window: the ON path uses mix(u_night, 1.0, u_space_sky) — at day (T=0.5) the stars' opacity is the A window (visible once the sky is space, as in the A10 frame); at night it is 1.0 exactly as before (bit-for-bit). The OFF path keeps u_opacity = (1−day)+day·S verbatim.
  4. The cone (clause C) is the R+SEA disc's local tangent cone: below it (hl < 0 or the camera inside the disc) the single-source AIR colour (u_air, display-space 8-bit sRGB in the sky pass's native space — the 8-bit source the env fog / body / clouds take srgb_to_linear of), above it SPACE; the cone keeps the limb term (now at the A shell). The OFF path is the pre-AC-0402 expression verbatim.
  5. u_air spaces: the sky pass takes the 8-bit DISPLAY value (the pass pre-decodes under Forward+, AC-0242); all other consumers take the linear of the same 8-bit source — one home, ≤ ~2–3 byte pipeline Δ between the sky-air and the fog-wall pixels (the pipeline is ACES-graded; not chased, per the ticket).
