AC-0417 — sky-local-zenith-and-atmosphere-height

Registry notes (verbatim, quoted):

1) User Story: As a player standing anywhere on the planet, the sky should behave like sky - the horizon should be level and the blue should thin out and give way to space at a believable height above my head, not three kilometres up. Today the day sky is painted as if the world were still flat: its gradient, its haze band and its blue-to-space blend are all keyed to the WORLD's up axis, which is only the local up at the one spot the flat world used to be centred on. Walk a few kilometres and the painted horizon tilts away from the real one; climb and the sky stays blue far longer than it should. The user's words: replace the day skybox with something that does not have a fake horizon since we are on a sphere now - possibly not a skybox at all, just a solid colour or a gradient - and make the blue-to-space transition happen closer to the planet.

2) Technical Details:

A) THE FAKE HORIZON - ONE LINE, AND IT IS THE WHOLE DAY SKY. godot/core/aero_sky_gradient.gdshader parameterises everything by the WORLD Y of the ray:
  - L90 `float h = dir.y;` (dir = normalize(EYEDIR), world space),
  - L91-101 the day gradient `mix(horizon_color, mid_color, smoothstep(0,0.30,u))` -> zenith, the below-horizon branch `if (h < 0.0) col = mix(col, horizon_color*0.5, clamp(-h*2,0,1))`, and the haze band `hz = exp(-abs(h)*9.0)*haze_amount`,
  - L102-107 the space blend `u = clamp(h,0,1)` and `ws = mix(mix(u_space^2, u_space, u), mix(u_space_sky^2, u_space_sky, u), u_sky_alt)`.
  World Y is the FACE-CENTRE radial. The world is a polyhedron of tangent facets (AC-0306/AC-0308/AC-0311), so the local zenith differs from world Y by the surface tilt asin(d/R) at in-plane distance d from the face centre: 5.5 deg at 384 m, 22 deg at 1.5 km, 51.7 deg at the home face edge (3141 m at R = 4000). The painted horizon, the haze band (+-11 deg wide) and the space blend are therefore correct only at the face centre and progressively wrong everywhere else.
  THE FIX IS A SUBSTITUTION, NOT A NEW MODEL: `h` becomes the LOCAL zenith - `dot(dir, normalize(cam_pos - planet_centre))` - and the shader ALREADY receives that direction: main.gd pushes `u_limb_center` = the planet centre RELATIVE TO THE CAMERA (main.gd:1412-1430), so `up_local = normalize(-u_limb_center)`. Everything keyed on `h` becomes locally correct on every face at once. The horizon DIP from altitude needs no new term: the limb term already owns the tangent geometry - L112 `b = length(cross(u_limb_center, dir))` with `u_limb_radius` = R+SEA and the `limb > 1.0 -> 1.0` clamp is the impact-parameter (tangent-cone) test, and it is already sphere-aware.

B) THE BLUE-TO-SPACE TRANSITION MUST HAPPEN CLOSER TO THE PLANET. Today the background's window is the AC-0402 stretch: BAND_WALK_MAX .. BAND_FLY_MIN*1.6 = 500 .. 3200 m (`u_space_sky`, pushed per frame by world.gd `_ac0401_push`), measured at ~0.04 / 0.44 / 0.92 at the 700 / 1600 / 2600 m frames - i.e. effectively black only around 3 km up. The user wants space reached much lower. DECISION TO MAKE IN THIS TICKET: define ONE atmosphere height A (a gameplay constant, not a physical one - R = 4000 with an Earth-like ratio would put space at ~60 km, far outside the flight range, which is why the current numbers are gameplay-shaped) and derive every consumer from it, so the visual and the control transitions agree (the AC-0386 principle):
  - the sky's space blend runs over roughly [0.25A, A] (curve to be stated);
  - the star field reaches full opacity by A, and the sun's glow (which already dies with the air under AC-0386/AC-0402) with it;
  - the satellite body's `h_first` / `h_full` (satellite_body.gd:560-561, today `sqrt((R+SEA)^2 + fog_far^2) - R` = 139 m and `SEA + render_edge` = 526 m at R24) are re-derived against A, so "the planet is fully established" and "the sky is space" land together instead of 700 m apart;
  - the fog wall stays where it is (it is a render-distance artifact: `fog_far = (render_edge)*pct/100`), but the ticket must SAY that, so nobody re-couples the atmosphere to the render distance again.
  Proposed starting point for the user to confirm or move: A ~ 1200 m - blue to ~300 m, transition 300-1200 m, black above.

C) DO WE STILL NEED A SKY? YES, BUT A MINIMAL ONE. Recommendation: keep a sky pass, drop the fake gradient. The minimal honest model is TWO COLOURS AND ONE CONE: below the LOCAL tangent cone, the single-source air colour (the AC-0400 identity - `AeroLib.fog_display`, the same value main.gd puts on `env.fog_light_color` and satellite_body.gd puts on the body's `u_air`); above it, the space colour (`AeroLib.SPACE_SKY`, AC-0386); the cone itself keeps the existing limb term. Reasons not to delete the sky pass outright: (1) it is the background wherever the near tiers draw nothing - the user's own coverage-gap frames are exactly that case, and a black or arbitrary background there would turn a haze gap into a hole; (2) the SUN's disc lives in it (L123-128) - the sun is a sky term, not an object, which is why its visibility was diagnostic at AC-0411; (3) the stars are already geometry (main.gd's 320 m shell + core/star.gdshader with its own R+SEA occlusion test), so they do not need the sky. If the user wants a gradient rather than a flat air colour below the horizon, it should be keyed on the LOCAL zenith with 1-2 stops (zenith -> horizon); the flat colour is the degenerate case of the same expression, so this is a parameter, not a second code path.

D) WHAT MUST NOT CHANGE: the AC-0400 single-source air colour (fog colour = the body's `u_air` = the sky's horizon), the AC-0401 limb gate (`u_limb_gate * u_space`: no rim below the flight band), the AC-0386 contract that the space gradient touches the BACKGROUND only (no double darkening of the ground seen from orbit), and the body's own `haz_a`/`haz_d` model.

E) SWITCH (decided at filing, per the house rule): this DOES change how the game looks, so it ships behind the EXISTING `sky_altitude` setting (AC-0402's, DEFAULT ON) rather than a new one - ON = the new local-zenith sky with atmosphere height A, OFF = the pre-AC-0402 expression bit-for-bit - plus the env override the arms already use. The user asked for the new behaviour, so the default is the new one. Add a second env knob for A so the arms and renders can sweep the height without a save file.

F) EVIDENCE OWED: (1) a render pair at the SAME altitude at the FACE CENTRE and at ~2 km in-plane distance on the same face - the painted horizon must be level in both (today it is tilted by the surface angle: 22 deg at 1.5 km); (2) the space blend measured against altitude on the new curve, reported at 4-5 altitudes up to A, and the star opacity and the body's `op_nadir` on the SAME ladder to show the three agree; (3) the background seen through a coverage gap at the haze colour, not black; (4) G0 zero SCRIPT/SHADER ERROR, shaderforce 12/12 (this touches a shader), and no change to generation (genhash 25/25).

RELATED: AC-0386 (the space transition), AC-0401 (the limb gate = an orbit instrument), AC-0402 (the stretched background window this replaces), AC-0400 (the single-source air colour), AC-0416 (the far tier's placement - unrelated mechanism, same session's finding).
