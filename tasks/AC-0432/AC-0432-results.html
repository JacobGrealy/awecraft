AC-0432 — results (black faces along the chunk seam)

Seed 44, r4, the user's view (spawn pose, chunk (0,−1) seam). Defect: the tree's faces along the seam render pure black while the open ground 200 px away renders bright.

1. Mechanism — the light floor under the crown (the split, explained)

The original UV→texel hypothesis is refuted: the slab-8 mesh of (0,−1) is complete (live == fresh, 343 = 343 quads; all 67 scan-decided trunk faces present, incl. the 12 seam-V-extended +x faces at x≈16.6), the UVs are plain-atlas-space and correct, and the bound texture (ShaderMaterial param tex, 1024²) holds the right pixels at those UVs (grass top (56,108,33,255), log side (116,90,54,255)). A tf=400 ray probe in the render world confirms it at the snapshot frame: every blob ray finds its quad within d<0.7 of the first solid cell. Geometry and texels are exonerated in both worlds.

The vertex-color R channel carries the sky-light scalar s (clamped [20/255, 1] at mesh time). Every blob-region face sits at the floor (R = 0.0784, G = 0.0) — the canopy's leaves occlude the sky pull for the trunk cells and the grass tops the crown overhangs — while the open-ground faces 200 px away are at R = 1.0. The shader is ALBEDO = t.rgb * COLOR.b * L, L = 0.20 + 0.80·max(u_day·R, G): floor faces render at L = 0.2627, sunlit faces at L = 1.0; after the tonemap, bark × 0.5647 shade × 0.26 lands at (0,0,0)–(0,23,0) on screen. The (0,0,0) blob pixels are those floor-rendered faces plus space sky seen through the canopy's cutout holes and grazing gaps. The split is sky exposure: faces whose column the sky pull reaches are bright; faces under the crown are black — same mesh, same frame, same texture, only the light scalar differs. Same trunk, same slab: the seam-extended +x face at x=16.6 (sky-exposed) renders bright green-brown, its floor-lit siblings render black.

2. What changed

AC-0426 had measured and shipped exactly this lift as Settings ambient_sky (pushed as u_amb; L += u_amb·u_day·step(m_direct, 0.08) — the step fires only on floor faces, so sunlit faces are bit-identical at any hour; 0.0 at night), but it defaulted OFF. The fix: godot/autoload/settings.gd — "ambient_sky": true (default ON; comments refreshed in settings.gd and world.gd). At noon the floor faces go L 0.2627 → 0.6127 (AMB_SKY_L = 0.35, the AC-0426 value: "dark brown, readable as bark, in-the-shade not in-the-hole"; sunlit neighbours untouched). Colour-only push — no mesh, no bake, no data. AWECRAFT_AMBSKY=0 and the saved setting restore the OFF look per process / per user. No C++ change: the mesher is exonerated; all temporary debug code (harness.gd arm, world.gd tf=400 audit, mesh.cpp dbg hooks) was removed and the .so rebuilt + copied to godot/bin.

6. Deliberately not done

  • No re-tune of AMB_SKY_L (0.35 is the AC-0426 measured value; the ticket asks for the measured fix on, not a new magnitude). The 0.20 light floor (user-directed, AC-0128/AC-0135) is untouched.
  • No mesher/light-engine change: starlight.cpp (fenced, test-only anyway) computes the sky pull; the canopy occlusion is the light model working as designed — the defect was that the measured mitigation shipped off.
  • No per-face "fully lit" forcing: sunlit-look faces under a canopy would break the lighting model and the night path; the ambient lift is the scoped fix.
  • No ARCHITECTURE.md change: no structural change (a settings default flip is not an architecture move — the switch, the push seam and the env override all pre-date this ticket).
  • Residual (0,0,0) pixels in the blob = space sky through the canopy's cutout holes and grazing gaps — correct rendering (the before frame's sky-through-holes is the same pixels); not chased further.
