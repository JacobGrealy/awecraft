AC-0432 — plan (black faces along the chunk seam) — mechanism established

Goal

Opaque faces around the tree at the (0,−1) chunk seam render pure (0,0,0) while the neighbouring open-ground faces render their texture. The fix must explain the split — why those faces are black and the rest correct — and make the faces carry texture like their neighbours.

Mechanism (established by the probes — the UV/texel candidate set below is REFUTED)

The original plan hypothesised a UV→texel miss onto empty canvas (a subset of faces sampling transparent atlas pixels). The probes refuted it completely: the mesh is complete (live == fresh, 343 = 343 quads in slab 8 of (0,−1); all 67 scan-decided trunk faces present, incl. the 12 seam-V-extended +x faces at x≈16.6), the UVs are plain-atlas-space and correct (trunk → log tile, grass top → grass tile), the bound texture (ShaderMaterial param tex, 1024×1024) holds the correct pixels at those UVs (grass (56,108,33,255), log side (116,90,54,255)). Geometry and texels are exonerated in BOTH the arm and render worlds (ray probe at the snapshot frame: every blob ray finds its quad within d<0.7 of the first solid cell).

The established mechanism — the light floor under the crown. Vertex-color channel R carries the sky-light scalar s (clamped to [20/255, 1] at mesh time). The blob-region faces (trunk sides + the grass tops the crown overhangs) sit at the floor — R = 0.0784, G = 0.0 — because the canopy's leaves occlude the sky pull for their cells (the AC-0425/AC-0426 "black silhouette", documented in chunk_lit_opaque.gdshader). The open-ground faces 200 px away are at R = 1.0. The shader is ALBEDO = t.rgb * COLOR.b * L with L = 0.20 + 0.80 * max(u_day*R, G): floor faces render at L = 0.2627, sunlit faces at L = 1.0. After the tonemap, bark × 0.56 shade × 0.26 lands at (0,0,0)–(0,23,0) on screen — the black blob; the (0,0,0) pixels are the floor-lit faces plus space sky seen through the canopy's cutout holes and grazing gaps. The split is sky exposure: faces whose column the sky pull can reach (R = 1.0) are bright; faces under the crown (R = 0.078) are black. Same mesh, same frame, same texture — only the light scalar differs.

Fix (AC-0432)

AC-0426 measured and shipped exactly this lift as the Settings ambient_sky switch (pushed as u_amb by world.gd _ac0401_push, colour-only: L += u_amb * u_day * step(m_direct, 0.08) — the step fires only on floor faces, sunlit faces are bit-identical at any hour; 0.0 at night). It defaulted OFF. AC-0432 turns it ON by default (godot/autoload/settings.gd "ambient_sky": true) — the user's ticket is that the OFF look is the defect. At noon the floor faces go L 0.2627 → 0.6127 (AMB_SKY_L = 0.35, the AC-0426-measured value: dark brown, readable as bark, "in-the-shade not in-the-hole", sunlit neighbours untouched). AWECRAFT_AMBSKY=0 and the saved setting still restore the OFF look per process / per user. No mesh, no bake, no data: genhash is untouched by construction.
