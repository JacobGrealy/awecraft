AC-0441 — cloud-deck banding/moire at oblique distance

Sat Oct 17 2026 · Forward+/lavapipe proxy renders (1280×720, R24/F83 user config, seed 44, T frozen, CLOUD_T0=411200) · the AC-0384 r24 recipe verbatim · evidence frames in captures/ beside this page.

VERDICT — SHIPPED (switch cloud_jitter, DEFAULT ON). The A/B established that the banding is coherent screen-space phase alignment of the fixed raymarch grid (candidate C2), not step coarseness alone: raising the march from 24 → 48 steps softened the pattern (residual stripes at 4× zoom, +39 % measured render wall) while per-pixel grid jitter at the same 24 steps removed it (no visible parallel structure, wall ≤ baseline). Both acceptance frames re-taken settled (drain 1200) with no banding/moire at either altitude; brightness spread, volume, cloud_gate and the AC-0436 moon-lit look all measured/inspected intact; render cost measured before and after and NOT increased.

1 · The defect

Shallow-angle views of the raymarched cloud deck (the AC-0405 volume on the outer shell, annulus [R+275, R+450], 24 count-based steps per annulus segment) show parallel stripes — an interference pattern, not cloud structure. Filed from the AC-0384 §29 assessment: the 120 m frame (upper-left mass) and the 300 m frame (horizon band) — the player's main flight altitudes.


120 m, switch OFF (= current look). Stripes across the upper-left mass. Controlled re-render of the assessment weather (same seed/T/CLOUD_T0); spectral signature matches the assessment frame to the digit.

300 m, switch OFF. Striped horizon band, weather-identical to tasks/AC-0384/ac0384-r24-300-day-rerun.png (side-by-side during calibration).

2 · Method — cause established by measurement, not reasoning

Three candidate causes named in the ticket: (a) fixed step too coarse for distance traversed; (b) no jitter/dither to break screen-space alignment; (c) sampling density not adapting to layer thickness. The ticket's own method decides it: render the same view with (i) the step count raised, (ii) jitter enabled, (iii) both, and see which removes the pattern rather than merely softening it. Four diagnostic renders (drain 120, the r24 ladder condition — the cloud deck is world-fixed and independent of terrain streaming, so the A/B on it is valid) plus three settled acceptance renders (drain 1200/600), one godot at a time, Forward+ boot line asserted, snapshot file-existence checked (the SNAP FAIL trap), no AWECRAFT_LOGIC arm.

2.1 · The instrument

.scratch/AC-0441/stripe.py (pure PIL, no numpy): 2D power spectrum of 64×64 crops, periods 16–4 px band, coherence = top-bin power over the same-radius envelope (a smooth gradient's radial falloff cancels; a true stripe peak far exceeds its own-radius neighbours), conjugate-pair check, ripple_rms (oscillation amplitude) and cloud_scale_lfsd (16-px block-mean std — the volume/brightness-spread guard). Calibration on the assessment frames showed the 21 px / 16 px / 10.7 px bins are reproducible pipeline constants of this box (present in the cloudless clean sky of every frame at ~0.26 ripple amplitude — tonemap/driver, not the cloud march); the defect separates on ripple_rms 2.2–8.2 in cloud regions vs 0.23–0.36 clean sky (8–30×). The instrument is a screening + amplitude/preservation tool; the ticket's acceptance is visual on the re-taken frames, which the zoom crops below adjudicate.

The visual adjudication:


baseline | steps 48 (4× zoom). The pattern is softened: faint residual parallel traces persist in the densest mass and above it.

baseline | jitter (4× zoom). The parallel structure is gone — smooth gradient + fine uncorrelated grain. Removed, not softened.
Why, mechanistically: the shipped grid is t = t0 + (i+0.5)·dl with dl = chord/24. t0 and dl vary smoothly across the screen with the ray's impact parameter, so adjacent pixels march the same phased grid against the world-fixed density field — the aliasing accumulates coherently into parallel stripes, worst at shallow angles (long chord, coarse dl, slowest phase change). Doubling the count halves dl (less aliasing amplitude — the softening) but the grid stays screen-coherent (the pattern survives, finer). Shifting each pixel's grid by an independent random in [0,1) turns the coherent phase offset into uncorrelated per-pixel randomness — the structured artifact becomes noise the eye tolerates, at the identical sample count. The ticket's "trades structured artifact for tolerable noise at the same sample count" is exactly what was measured.

9 · Deliberate non-actions

  • No adaptive step count / thickness-scaled density (candidate c): the A/B showed coarseness alone is not the cause (48 steps softened, didn't remove) and any sample-count change costs render time against the 1191/1200 s allowance. Jitter removes the pattern at zero added samples — the ticket's preference ("costs nothing or less") wins.
  • No jitter on the light-march taps: their grid is anchored to the sample's world position (not screen-coherent), and the AC-0436 moon-lit look is a committed recent fix — untouched.
  • No per-frame time-varying jitter: the offsets are stable between frames (quantized world cell) — the deck does not shimmer as the camera moves; the grain moves with the clouds.
  • AWECRAFT_VOLSTEPS is not a Settings option: a boot-only A/B cost instrument (the AWECRAFT_SPEC pattern). The shipped option is the jitter switch.
  • Fenced files untouched (main.gd, game.gd, drop.gd, debug.gd, harness.gd, the other sessions' task dirs); no git add -A, nothing committed (coordinator's closeout).

10 · Residuals and caveats

  • The grain is real at high zoom (the 3×/4× crops show fine speckle). At 1× viewing distance it reads as cloud texture; it is the ticket's stated trade ("tolerable noise at the same sample count"). The 300 m band's ripple_rms rose ~20–40 % vs baseline (5.6→10.1 at the densest crop) — the noise amplitude the fix buys, measured, not hidden.
  • The (30,100)-class residual in the near-white flat-top crops (bandCoh 7.4 in the jitter frame): the same low-amplitude bins the cloudless-sky controls carry (pipeline gradient/quantization class, not the deck stripe) — the visible mass stripes are gone in every crop.
  • Software-proxy renders: these prove the Forward+ path, the shader, geometry and the look; authoritative look/speed is the user's Windows build. The cost statement is per-frame march work (zero added samples), which is renderer-independent.
  • r24-recipe deviation (disclosed): the diagnostic arms rendered at drain 120 (the r24 ladder condition) — the acceptance frames at drain 1200 (the r24 rerun condition). The cloud deck is world-fixed, so the A/B at drain 120 is valid; the settled frames are the acceptance evidence.
