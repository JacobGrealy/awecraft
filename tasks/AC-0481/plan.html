In the capture a faint but clear straight vertical line runs from the top of the frame down through the cloud layer into the sky, well above the horizon; the clouds themselves read as thin smeared streaks rather than volumes.

2. ⚠ RE-CHECK FIRST: the capture PREDATES a major sky change

AC-0483 landed in the round before this one and REMOVED THE SYNTHETIC SKY HORIZON from core/aero_sky_gradient.gdshader — the shader went 459 → 332 lines, three uniforms were deleted (27 → 24), and the local-horizontal split, the below-horizon floor, the horizon anchor compensation and the hl-stripe were all removed. So step 1 is: does the vertical seam still exist? Reproduce the user's vantage on the current tree and look. If it is gone, the seam half of this ticket is already resolved by AC-0483 and must be recorded as such rather than "fixed" twice. If it remains, continue to §3.

3. Separate the two complaints

(a) THE SEAM. It is straight, thin, and runs through the SKY as well as the clouds — which argues against the volumetric marcher (whose artifacts are soft and blobby) and toward something tiled or coordinate-based: a tile boundary in a sky/cloud texture, a projected grid edge, or a UV wrap. THE DECISIVE TEST, and it costs one render: does the seam persist with the CLOUDS SWITCHED OFF?

  • Sky alone, seam present → the cause is in the sky path, not the cloud path.
  • Seam only with clouds on → the cause is in the cloud volume or its projection.
  • Also distinguish: is the seam at a fixed SCREEN position or a fixed WORLD direction? Fixed screen position implies a fullscreen/post or quad edge; fixed world direction implies tiling or a UV wrap. Move the camera and look — the seam either follows the view or stays in the world.

(b) THE CLOUD FORM. The word "now" matters: AC-0405 introduced the raymarched volume, AC-0406 added the ambient fill and multiscatter, AC-0407 added the skyline and inside-cloud fill, AC-0412 changed the far-tier sampler, AC-0441 added jitter. AC-0406's notes record that an over-high ambient weight (0.7) FLATTENED the cloud spread and was rejected — and the final calibration was against the single-source air colour (u_vol_air) precisely so form survived. So the first thing to check is whether the shipped ambient/multiscatter weights are still the calibrated ones or have drifted, and whether a later ticket (AC-0407's skyline, AC-0441's jitter) changed the spread.
