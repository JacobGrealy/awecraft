Registry notes (verbatim, quoted):

1) User Story: Looking up from the ground and down from orbit should show a few big, three-dimensional clouds rather than a dense even speckle, the air near the ground should be hazy without a glowing rim around the world, the layers should stop fighting each other where they overlap, and from high up you should see the planet rather than the patch of ground you just left.

2) Technical Details:
FOUR ITEMS, reported by the user directly after flying the current build, in their words, and all of them are the same subsystem - the LOD tiers and the shells, how they are shaded and when they are drawn. Two of them independently match what the AC-0384 round 12 assessment found and named, which is recorded here so the evidence is not re-derived.
(1) CLOUDS: "there are too many clouds, there should be less of them but the individual clouds should be bigger and they should be volumetric". So: fewer, larger, and reading as volumes rather than as a flat patterned membrane. The assessment's D1 says the same thing with numbers - the deck's brightness spread measures 16.9 against 32.4 for the reference look, and its colour is close to pure grey (222,222,222) where the reference carries saturation (181,230,194). Treat this as a look change with a switch, per the project's standing rule.
(2) THE SHADERS ARE SHARED AND SHOULD NOT BE: "I think that low lod and satellite lod are sharing the same shader, they should not. We do not want low lod to have the limb effect, only fog." So the near/low LOD must fade into FOG ONLY - no atmospheric limb treatment - while the satellite body keeps the limb. This is very likely the same finding as the assessment's D2 from the other direction: it measured that in cloudy weather the limb the eye actually sees is the CLOUD DECK's crisp silhouette (sky 145,198,212 to deck 201,201,201 across about 5 pixels) because the deck's silhouette at 65.35 degrees hides the planet's tangent at 55.72 degrees, so the glow is only visible in clear weather. Separate the shading paths and verify BOTH states: near ground must show haze with no rim; orbit must show the limb glow.
(3) Z-FIGHTING between the clouds, the low LOD and the satellite LOD. The assessment's D3 describes a related symptom - a dark deck core smudging the limb, 5507 against 5505 dark pixels, present in weather and not the previously removed rim. Find where the layers' surfaces coincide and separate them by construction (a radius ordering with a real gap, or depth handling) rather than nudging one until the flicker hides.
(4) THE HIGH LOD SHOULD BE HIDDEN ABOVE A CERTAIN HEIGHT: "you shouldn't be able to see it from space / high in the atmosphere." So the near-detail terrain must dissolve out as altitude rises, in the same way the satellite body already dissolves in below the flight band, so that from orbit the player sees the planet and its atmosphere rather than the patch of ground they lifted off from. That existing dissolve is the pattern to follow - it is the AC-0310 no-pop contract - and whatever threshold is chosen should be checked against the satellite body's own so the two never both vanish or both show.
ACCEPTANCE IS RENDERS AT THE USER'S CONFIGURATION (radius 24, fog fully opaque at 332 m) across a descent AND a takeoff ladder at 2600, 1600, 700, 300 and 120 m, showing: few large volumetric clouds rather than dense speckle; no limb rim on the near terrain at low altitude with the glow intact from orbit; no z-fighting or flicker where layers overlap; and the high detail terrain absent from the high-altitude frames. Re-use the frames in tasks/AC-0384/ (the round 12 assessment captures ac0384-final-*.png and its section 25) as the before set so the comparison is direct.
CONSTRAINTS: genhash 25/25 byte-identical if generation is untouched, and if the tier visibility change implies generation must move, that is a FINDING to report rather than a re-baseline; G0 zero SCRIPT ERROR and zero SHADER ERROR via tasks/scripts/gate_census.py; the standing set green (farab h_mismatch 0, ladder 20/20, meshprobe 1.0, halo ok, lightstate 0/0, r16 all sub-flags with far_holes 0/0 both bands, boundary r4 in band, shaderforce 12/12); the cloud shell radii and centre stay exactly 4275/4330/4400 about (0, -R, 0) because they are verified correct and the user has not asked for them to move; AC-0399's view-weighted fill ordering and AC-0398's interpolated lighting must keep working; AC-0400's seam fade must keep targeting the single-source air colour. Look changes ship with a switch, defaults conservative, short-circuiting before the work.
