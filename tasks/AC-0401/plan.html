AC-0401 plan — cloud-material-tier-split-and-layer-conflicts

Goal (1 sentence)

Make the LOD tiers and shells behave per the user's four reports: a few large volumetric clouds, a near field that fades into FOG ONLY (no limb rim near the ground, limb glow intact from orbit), layers separated by construction (no z-fighting), and the near-detail hidden above the fog-wall altitude so orbit shows the planet, not the patch of ground left behind.

The one mechanism behind three of the four items

All three layer-conflict items reduce to the same contract violation: the satellite body already owns the rule “the body is only where the world's depth fog has already fully faded the world (d ≥ fog_far)”, but the near tiers (band A/B/C) keep drawing past that wall — at 100% fog colour over the body's terrain, coplanar with it (the fog-colour diamond patch in the 1600/2600 frames, and the flicker in the 332–400 m band where both are partially drawn). The fix makes the near field obey the SAME wall it always rendered into:

  • lod_avg.gdshader (the annulus) and the four chunk_lit_*.gdshader materials and the two fluid_anim_*.gdshader (ring water): if (d >= u_fog_far) discard; — the strict partition. The body's own haz_d rim at the wall is exactly the single-source air colour, so the seam target (AC-0400) is preserved, now carried by the body.
  • satellite_body.gdshader: new vertex stage — the displaced surface sits 2 m radially inside the near tiers' terrain, so wherever they still coexist (terrain relief), the near tiers win the depth test by construction. Radius ordering with a real gap; no flicker, no nudging.
  • world.gd _process: pushes u_fog_far (DayNight.fog_far(render_radius, fog_start_pct) — the same value main.gd sets on env.fog_depth_end) to the shared chunk/fluid materials, change-gated.

Consequence for item (4): the “threshold” is not a new constant — it IS the satellite body's own fog wall (332 m at the user's R24/F83). As the camera climbs, the engine fog fades the near field to air (the existing no-pop dissolve), and at the wall the near field stops drawing exactly where the body's existence ramp begins — the two never both vanish or both show, by construction. From orbit the disc is the body alone: the planet, not the patch.
