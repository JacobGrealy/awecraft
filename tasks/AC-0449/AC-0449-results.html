AC-0449 results — "the default blur amount is insane"

This page supersedes the prior run's results page, which worked the withdrawn air-colour hypothesis (its captures/ folder remains as that run's record). The correction governs: it is a blur — an actual image blur destroying the near field — and the effect must stay, made subtle and far out. The prior run's uncommitted u_fill_key sky-fill change was reverted to HEAD before this fix landed.

Verdict: the blur is the engine depth-of-field (the only actual image blur in the tree — Forward+ CameraAttributesPractical on the player Camera3D). It was added for the ground (far wall 82.62 m, calibrated for a grounded player). Since AC-0445 made free vertical flight, the user lives at 941 m altitude, where the entire visible world (943 m at the nadir → 2.9 km at the horizon) sits past the 82.62 m wall, so every fragment renders at the maximum blur: "completely blurred out even things right in front of you". The effect was not removed and its strength (0.08) was not cut — the far distance moved to the flight near boundary (1500 m) and the 5 m ramp was broadened to a 1400 m ramp, so the softening now accumulates out at the distance (full 0.08 at ~2900 m = the local horizon of the user's documented view) while the near field is sharp.

9. What was deliberately NOT done

  • Not removed, not strength-cut: the DoF effect stays (toggle + strength 0.08 unchanged); only its distance moved. The user asked for "subtle and far out", not "gone".
  • The below-horizon air-fill colour wash is a separate look issue (the withdrawn hypothesis of the prior run) — the correction says it's blur, and the blur is proven; the flat u_air fill (visible in u1/u3) is untouched by this fix and was not "fixed" here.
  • The cloud shell's depth behaviour is untouched: it rides its geometric depth (the nadir view proves the 541 m disc-centre clouds sharpen with the fix); the horizon view's outer cloud band (1500–2247 m) staying softly accumulated is the ramp working as designed, not a defect.
  • The planet preset was not changed — its camera carries no attributes by design (AC-0421); it simply cannot exhibit DoF.
  • No Settings row for the switch (env-only, AWECRAFT_SPEC pattern — the legacy state is an A/B instrument, not a user setting); the user-facing controls remain the existing DOF toggle + far-distance spinbox (now 1–8192) + strength spinbox.
