Registry notes (verbatim, quoted):

1) User Story: Walking across the world, the moment the streaming ring recenters should not stutter - and right now it does, because the ground generation that makes the far terrain match the carved world is expensive and the recenter sweep pays for it on the main thread.

2) Technical Details:
THE GATE IS RED AND IT IS A REAL COST, NOT A FLAP. The standing R24 crossing-burst gate is `crossing_burst_p50 <= 10 ms`. Across my own capped stages it has read 4.6 ms (tree eee561e, immediately after AC-0387's reuse fix), 9.6 ms (tree fb9644d), and 14.2 ms (tree 7e3614e), with the crossing frame p95 at 79 ms. The trend is upward and the last value is 42% over the gate. This is the deliberate price of AC-0387: making the far lane produce the CARVED top - which the user chose knowing it costs "whatever it costs" - took the far lane from 95 to ~3,330 us/chunk, roughly 35x, and the recenter sweep is where a synchronous burst of that work lands on the main thread.
WHAT IS ALREADY DONE, so this ticket does not redo it: AC-0387's follow-up made the sweep REUSE the resident far payload instead of recomputing it (the payload is a pure function of seed, cx, cz and is never saved), which took the burst from 34.8 ms back to 6.7 ms at the time, with the reuse proven byte-identical on 400 chunks (0 mismatches) and now reported through the standing `gen_far_reuse` census field so a silent relapse shows up as a zero. The remaining cost is the COLD fraction: columns that never rode the far band and therefore have no resident payload to reuse - measured at 55 cold recomputes out of 285 demotes (81% reuse) in that run, at 3.32 ms each.
THEREFORE THE WORK IS ONE OF THESE, and the first step is to measure which: (a) SHRINK THE COLD SET - the 55 cold recomputes are spawn-region and freshly-promoted columns; if the far payload can be produced at the moment a column is demoted FROM a full chunk (rather than recomputed later), the cold set approaches zero and the sweep becomes lookups only; (b) MOVE THE REMAINING COLD WORK OFF THE MAIN THREAD - the thread-generation lane exists and the AC-0382 satellite bake proved a WorkerThreadPool slot is available for a pure C++ call, so a cold far payload could be produced there with the sweep accepting a one-frame-old far column; (c) IF NEITHER CLOSES IT, say so with numbers and propose the honest bound, because the alternative - quietly raising a gate that exists to catch exactly this - is what this project forbids.
NOTE THE SECOND THREAD-GEN / DRAIN CONTEXT: AC-0381's review found DRAIN at 57.6-59.9% of the worst-20 frames and the engine-side gap at 62.6-71.9% of the max frames with no counter reaching inside it, so anything that adds main-thread work here lands next to the largest unattributed share of the worst frames. That is an argument for (b) if (a) does not close the gap.
MEASURE WITH THE SAME INSTRUMENT THE GATE USES - `crossing_burst_p50`, its max, the crossing frame p95, and the storm p99 - and report them across at least two runs, because this value has moved by 3x across my stages for reasons that are not all explained (the same run-to-run variance this session has met repeatedly, where a field riding a variable process flaps and must be reported as a range rather than a single number). Say plainly whether the gate is met afterwards, and if it is not, what the honest bound is and why.
CONSTRAINTS: the far lane's OUTPUT must not change - H stays the full path's carved top and the repaired farab gate stays green with h_mismatch 0 (it is negative-tested and would catch a regression); genhash 25/25 byte-identical; the standing set green - G0 zero SCRIPT ERROR (not rc=0), ladder 20/20, meshprobe 1.0, halo ok, lightstate 0/0, r16 all sub-flags with far_holes 0/0 both bands, boundary r4 in band; the promotion contract in ARCHITECTURE.md section 6 read before touching promotion; native rebuild + re-stage mandatory if the C++ lane is touched; the full battery and the Windows rebuild are the coordinator's.
CONTEXT: read tasks/AC-0387/AC-0387-results.html sections 15-18 (the reuse fix, the cold-set numbers, the census field), tasks/AC-0381/AC-0381-results.html (the DRAIN and engine-gap shares), and grep world.gd for the recenter sweep, `_demote_to_far` and `gen_far_reuse`.
