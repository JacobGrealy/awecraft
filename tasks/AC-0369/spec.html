Registry notes (verbatim, quoted):

1) User Story: The window right after loading lasts about twenty seconds, and the only reason it lasts that long is that each unit of world is dispatched on the main thread at a cost of about 4.5 ms. Cutting that cost is the one change that shortens the window without making the long frames worse - every other knob trades one against the other.

2) Technical Details:
WHERE THIS COMES FROM (AC-0364, measured on the current tree, 2026-09-26): the post-load fill window is ~20 +/- 4 s from gate-lift, bounded by the MAIN-THREAD DRAIN stage at ~4.5 ms per discharge unit (a 30 ms/frame time cap yielding only ~75-130 units/s) plus ~20 ms/frame of non-drain overhead, with COLLIDE averaging ~25 ms/frame and 841 staged drops. It is NOT generation (thread-gen is starved at 1-2 inflight against 545/s capacity), NOT the 3.5 ms drain pace (already saturated), and NOT the mesh pool. The budget curve is FLAT: 2x/4x/8x the fill budget moves the window only 18.0/17.0/16.5 s while storm p99 goes 287/503/935 ms, because the wall rate saturates at ~222 units/s.
THE JOB: find where the ~4.5 ms per unit actually goes and cut it. Start from the wprof stage split inside DRAIN (the instrument already exists and AC-0352/AC-0363 both used it), and remember AC-0363's finding that the drain is a FIXED wall-clock pace with a count-capped handoff - so the win must come from making a unit cheaper, not from discharging more of them per frame. Candidates worth measuring before touching: what a "unit" costs in the low versus high lane and whether one lane's units are much more expensive than the other's; whether the per-unit work includes anything that could be hoisted, cached, or moved to the thread-generation worker (which has enormous spare capacity - 1-2 inflight against 545/s); and the COLLIDE share, which at ~25 ms/frame is comparable to the drain itself and is (per AC-0337) the per-slab collision build. AC-0291's Voronoi aquifer search (~1.6 ms/chunk) and AC-0292's drip pass are named per-chunk costs from AC-0363's attribution and are candidates for the same treatment.
CONSTRAINTS: behaviour-preserving - genhash must stay byte-identical (or, if a real change to the world is unavoidable, rebase deliberately and record it); the mesh/light invariants hold (meshprobe 1.0, ladder 20/20, halo green, lightstate 0/0); boundary r4 walk p95 inside its standing band; the crossface and sphere arms stay green; and the R24 fill window must be RE-MEASURED after the change with the same instrument AC-0364 used, so the win is stated in the same units (window seconds, storm p99, fps) rather than as a stage percentage.
GATES: the wprof DRAIN split before and after with the cost per unit named; the R24 window before/after; G0 zero SCRIPT ERROR; genhash 25/25; farab h_mismatch 0; ladder 20/20; meshprobe 1.0; halo green; boundary r4 in band; the battery green.
CONTEXT: AC-0362 (the seam fill) and AC-0306/0307/0308/0309 (the planet pieces) are all recent and touch placement/streaming, so read their results pages before changing the dispatch path; and note that this box's llvmpipe is degraded, so renders are not available as evidence.
