Registry notes (verbatim, quoted):

1) User Story
Nothing visible today, but this is why nothing is visible: the world only pre-loads terrain ahead of the player on four of the eight chart faces. On the other four the pre-loading window is broken - one face pre-loads a single corner column, two faces pre-load the entire chart, and two never pre-load at all. It does not show as a hole because the per-frame player query builds the ground synchronously the moment you need it, so the cost is paid as a hitch instead of seen as a gap. Fixing it turns "the world keeps up" from an accident into a property.

2) Technical Details
FOUND BY AC-0469 stage C, the twelve-chart lap (25 of 26 boundaries crossed, mesh and body present at every destination, no holes). That is exactly why this needs its own ticket: the lap proves the world is never missing, and simultaneously shows that the face lane's PRESTREAM is degenerate on six of the eight chart faces, so "the world is there" is currently guaranteed by synchronous on-demand builds rather than by streaming.
THE DEFECT, per the lap's deviations C5: the z-face band window (world.gd:15199) doubles the along-edge index because the z faces' u axis charts at 1.0 m/cell with stride 0.5. Measured consequences: faces 8 and 10 degenerate to a single corner column (footprint 8:392:384-392); faces 9 and 11 over-stream the whole chart from ccx 0; faces 2 and 3 never stream at all. The x faces (4-7) behave.
WHY IT MATTERS BEYOND TIDINESS: the game's own per-frame player column query (get_block_key -> _ensure_face_chunk, the is_inf-sentinel sync mesh build) is what keeps every crossing solid. That is a synchronous build on the critical path, which is the class of cost the whole streaming design exists to keep off the frame - and the same pattern that produced the AC-0145 free-fall finding when a wait was unchecked.
VERIFY: after the fix, each of faces 2-11 has a non-degenerate prestream window of the expected size; the lap still shows mesh and body at every crossing; the residency arm stays bounded (the prestream must not reintroduce the leak fixed at ee123a3); and the boundary walk's crossing latency must not regress. Report the per-face window sizes as the evidence, since that is the quantity that is wrong today.
