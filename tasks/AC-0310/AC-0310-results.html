AC-0310 PIECE 2 — the whole-body satellite tier above the atmosphere, dissolving into voxels

Bounded piece 2 (shared ticket, no TASKS.yaml edits). Piece 1 (the 12 per-face 1024² bakes + the on-disk variance guard) is done and verified — this page records the TIER. Renders are infeasible on this box (llvmpipe degraded, 3-D screenshots have failed for weeks), so the no-pop criterion is defined numerically (§3) and the tier's behaviour is gated headless by AWECRAFT_LOGIC=satellite. Evidence: G0 output, RESULT JSON, measured values — values, not prose.

1. What draws the body, and from where

New subsystem godot/world/satellite_body.gd (node SatelliteBody, a child of World) + godot/core/satellite_body.gdshader. It draws the planet as ONE closed body: the 12-face great-circle chart (SphereMath.uv_to_world, the same machine-diffed chart piece 1 baked against) as 12 face meshes (48×48 grid each, ~29k vertices / ~110k triangles total) at radius R + SEA (4000 + 126 = 4126 m), centred on the planet-frame origin (global (0, −R, 0)), textured with the 12 per-face 1024² satellite PNGs (shipped at godot/assets/satellite/ for the canonical seed 44; for any other seed they are baked at runtime — §4). The body is unlit: the piece-1 bake already carries the fixed-sun lambert in the pixels; the shader only adds the day/night brightness modulation and the atmospheric model below.

The per-fragment model (the shader, satellite_body.gdshader) — view distance d = length(VIEW) per fragment:

  1. Existence (the no-pop contract): opacity = smoothstep(fog_far, render_edge, d). The body only EXISTS where the world's depth fog has already fully faded the world (d ≥ fog_far = 709.92 m @R50), and is fully established by the render edge (d ≥ 816 m). The rim therefore emerges exactly as it passes the fog wall, and on descent the body's near region dissolves per-fragment under the drawn disc — the spec's clause (b), with the fade per-fragment by view distance, not one global alpha.
  2. Haze: haze = clamp(limb² + near-edge term, 0, 1) blended to the air colour (DayNight.sky_display — the same value the env fog colour uses, so the body reads as the fog colour itself just outside the disc's edge, whose fragments are 100% fogged — continuous across the edge, no hard ring). The limb (grazing views) is always hazy; the distance term hugs the render edge (clear by 4×render_edge).

Visibility is derived per frame from the player's radial altitude h = |pos − C| − R (the AC-0145 band reference): the node is hidden for h < h_first (zero draw cost on the ground), shown above it. The camera far plane (default 4000 m) would clip the body's far limb (h + 2·(R+SEA) out), so it is extended while the body is visible and restored when hidden.

3. No pop, measured — the angular-size criterion

Renders are not available, so the transition is defined numerically. The natural criterion (as the coordinator's brief suggested): the satellite texel covers a known area of surface and the drawn world's cell sizes are known, so their angular sizes at the transition altitude are computable:

  • satellite texel: 16.0 m² per texel (piece 1 §5.6 — the equal-area partition, 4πR²/12 per face / 1024² px) → side 4.0 m;
  • drawn chunk: 16 m; (supporting detail: the LOW 4×4×4 band's cell is 4 m × 4 m — the SAME 16 m² as the texel, mesh.cpp:1511 "a cell = 4x4x4 blocks" — so at the transition the two representations are angularly like-for-like in the outer band, not merely "smaller than a chunk");
  • at view distance d: texel angular size = 4.0/d rad, chunk = 16.0/d rad — the ratio is exactly 1/4 at every distance (both scale 1/d), i.e. the texel is always smaller than the chunk by the comfortable factor 4, and the dissolve happens inside the band where the geometric difference is smallest (d ∈ [709.92, 816], where both are 4.9–5.6 mrad ≈ 4.3–5.0 screen px at 1080p/70° — at the sub-pixel/low-pixel edge, where the haze term dominates and the colour, not the size, carries the difference).

Continuity measure (the stronger one, adopted instead of a pure size assertion): opacity continuity at the disc's edge. The disc's edge fragments are at view distance ≥ render_edge = 816 m > fog_far (709.92 m), i.e. 100% fogged — they render exactly the fog/air colour. The body fragment just outside the edge reads mix(texel·day, air, haze) with the near-edge haze term ≈ 1 at d = render_edge — i.e. the SAME air colour. So across the disc edge the composite is air-colour on both sides (coverage continuity: disc-weight + body-weight = 1 at every ground point, the spec's "consume the halo/far tier" clause), and the only residual is the haze's distance ramp just outside the edge, which is a smooth gradient, not a step. The arm checks the opacity values on both sides of the edge at four altitudes (§5).

4. The bake — sliced across frames (off-thread is OWED)

The runtime bake (any seed other than the shipped 44, or a wiped cache) runs the exact piece-1 pipeline in-engine: all 196,196 far payloads via AweGen.generate_far (the same call _demote_to_far makes; the piece-1 keying — home plain seed, faces 2–11 at (face·64+ccx, face·64+ccz) with seed^(face·1000003)) — then the piece-1 colour recipe (fcc top colour × fixed-sun lambert, per-chunk-clamped home gradient, per-face gradient for faces 2–11, sRGB out), writing the 12 PNGs to user://satellite/p{planet}_r{R}_s{seed}/ keyed (planet_id, R, seed) per the spec's REUSE clause.

Mode: sliced across frames on the main thread (the prompt's second option), budget 6 ms/frame, driven from World._process (so it runs during the load, when streaming is idle). The main-thread stall is measured, not asserted: per-frame step cost (max/mean) AND the full frame delta during the bake (which includes the streaming work sharing the thread) — see §6 for the numbers. OWED: the off-thread variant — generate_far is only reachable from the main thread in-tree (the TG pool's tasks are C++-side; a new C++ task type would be the clean path). Measured (forced rebake, R50 headless, 196,196 columns): generation 18,628 ms CPU (95.0 µs/chunk — matches piece-1's 91.2 µs/chunk), colour 63,043 ms (pure GDScript), PNG write 1,791 ms, texture load 241 ms — total wall 533 s because headless R50 runs ≈15–16 fps under streaming contention (frame mean 60.7 ms). One-time per seed, cached thereafter; for the canonical seed the shipped res:// PNGs skip it entirely (measured 295–360 ms wall, §7).

5. Finding — the shipped home-pair textures were wrong (fixed, re-shipped)

While proving the in-engine bake reproducible (the arm's rebuild_vs_shipped check: forced rebake, seed 44, pixel-compared against the shipped res:// PNGs), faces 2–11 came back byte-identical (0/1,048,576 px per face) but the home pair (faces 0–1) differed at ~69% of pixels (max channel diff 40–61) — a per-pixel shading difference with the height/top data correct. Targeted in-engine probes (per-pixel H, top-id, gx, gz at the differing pixels) showed the in-engine inputs matching an independently recomputed piece-1 recipe exactly — so the divergence was in the SHIPPED texture, not the bake.

Root cause (piece-1 script, tasks/AC-0310/ac0310_bake_satellite.py): the home gradient grids dHdx/dHdz live in [cx+197, cz+197, lz, lx] space, but the script indexed them with dHdx[I − 197, J − 197, ...] — i.e. raw cxi/czj ∈ [−197, 196]. numpy silently wraps the negative indices, so every home pixel was shaded with a different chunk's slope (a chunk ~197 away in the wrapped sense) instead of its own. The heights/tops on the same line were indexed correctly (Hh[I, J, K]), which is why the terrain placement machine-diff (piece 1 §2) passed and only the shading was wrong. Faces 2–11 were never affected (their gradients are per-face with no offset axis).

Fix and verification: the script's indexing is corrected (dHdx[I, J, Lj, Li], with a documenting comment); the in-engine bake (which was correct all along) was verified full-resolution against the corrected recipe — 0/1,048,576 px on each home face — and its 12 PNGs re-shipped to godot/assets/satellite/ + tasks/AC-0310/ (faces 2–11 pixel-identical to the previous shipment; only the two home faces' shading changed). The arm's rebuild_vs_shipped check now closes the loop: the in-engine bake must match the shipped textures pixel-for-pixel (expected 0 diff — the final gate run). Visual effect: the home faces (the nadir at spawn) now carry true local-slope shading instead of a grainy wrong-slope tint; the variance guard passes on all 12 faces (16 rows/cols × 16 px, floors 4 distinct / 8 spread).

8. What is still owed (stated plainly)

  • Off-thread bake — the sliced bake is in; the TG-pool task (C++) is owed (§4).
  • Sun-tracking day/night — the bake carries ONE fixed sun (piece 1); the shader modulates brightness only (u_day), it does not re-shade for the sun's position. A full day/night re-bake or a shader-side sun is owed (the limb/air model reuses the existing sky, per the spec's ATMOSPHERE clause).
  • Spec clause (c) in its strong form — "the real disc fades out entirely at altitude down to sub-pixel": today the disc is fully fogged (renders exactly the air colour) above fog_far altitude, i.e. it fades out visually via the EXISTING fog (measured, §3) but is still DRAWN (a cost, not a visual). An explicit sub-pixel cull of the disc at altitude is owed as an optimization (it would be a no-op visually — every fragment is 100% fogged — which is what makes it safe to defer).
  • Render-gate substitution — the spec's GATES name renders at surface/mid/orbit; renders are infeasible on this box. The numerical criterion (§3) + the satellite arm's opacity/visibility ladder + the variance guard is the substitute evidence; the round-limb claim is structural (the mesh IS the chart sphere — the limb comes out right by construction).
