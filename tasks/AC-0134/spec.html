AC-0134 — spec: random-lit-chunks-at-night regression (user-reported)

Registry note (verbatim): "AC-0128 regression: random fully-lit chunks next to pitch-black chunks at night with no light source for either. Probe midnight light_at / chunk bake eff shows alternating 15 vs 0. Root likely sky-scale caching/batching (AC-0077 eff cache) or AC-0128 dayFactor not per-chunk. Fix so night sky light is consistent per cave depth, no lottery. Gate: midnight unlit cave uniformly dark, no 15/0 lottery across chunks." — user-reported 2026-08-28T08:13 (same batch as AC-0133). User bug → high priority, queue position 2 (after AC-0133).

Goal

At midnight, an unlit cave must be uniformly dark across all chunks (web :746 formula at u_day≈0: L = 0.12 + 0.88·max(0·sky, blk) = 0.12 floor wherever sky is blocked and no block light) — no "random fully-lit chunks next to pitch-black chunks" lottery. The user's midnight probe shows eff alternating 15 vs 0 across chunks of the same unlit cave. Two candidate root causes from the registry (the audit must distinguish, not assume): (a) stale/wrong eff — sky 15 leaking into cave chunks across the boundary (AC-0077's eff cache / AC-0129's pull-kernel edge / a bake that ran before sky settled); (b) material-assignment gap — some built chunks' mesh instances still on the OLD lit material (no night scale, full-bright day look) instead of the four AC-0128 chunk_lit_* unlit materials (u_day-scaled). Both fit "fully-lit next to pitch-black, no source for either"; a chunk with eff=15 on the OLD material is fully bright at night, while the same eff on a chunk_lit material is 0.12-dark. The fix must make BOTH consistent: correct eff (no sky-15 lottery in unlit caves) AND every built chunk on a night-scaled material.
