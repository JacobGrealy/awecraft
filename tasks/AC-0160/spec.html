Spawn fast-drain (the AC-0160 fix): root causes — (a) shared 6-thread pool: gen saturated the low-prio slots and builds starved; (b) drain sync-fallbacks (270–1235 ms main-thread builds) on every cap-drop/missing-neighbor; (c) recenter slice racing the spawn window. Fixes: TM builds always high priority / TG data low (caps TG=4, TM=6, env-overridable); drain never sync-builds (all-8-neighbor-data gate, else defer); 5×5 spawn data dispatched as one high-priority 3-wide group at recenter, held until fully landed, then the 9 spawn chunks dispatch in one frame (TM cap 9 during startup); recenter slice pauses only for the one-shot _spawn_fast window; burst group task ids tracked so slot arrays are stable under running workers. Result: spawn3x3_ms 11.7 s → ~3.2–4.0 s (measured machine floor: 5×5 gen 1.35 s + 9 worker builds 0.92 s + (0,0) handoff face-cache/eff cascade ~1.3 s (AC-0134 machinery) + handoffs ~0.5 s — every component at its per-chunk floor; the ≤ 2 s model assumed ~0.5 s gen and omitted the cascade).
