1. Per-report findings and evidence

Report 1 — “the sky is dark during the day”

Root cause (confirmed in frames, not assumed): sky_on() painted mix(space_color, u_air, below*(1-w)) — a flat dark “air” fill for everything under the local horizon and pure space above it. The day/night/dusk gradient uniforms (horizon/mid/zenith_color, pushed per frame by game.gd) were declared and fed but never read on the ON path. Frame b02 (ground, looking straight up): the entire dome is RGB (0,0,0) except the sun disc — a pure-black sky at 12:30. The ground forward view b01 shows the same black dome over the horizon (only the haze band and the sun break it up).

The gradient is keyed to the local zenith elevation u = clamp(dot(dir, up),0,1) — horizon → horizon_color (223,246,251), 30 % → mid (95,198,234), zenith → zenith_color (46,134,216) — and fades to space through the AC-0417 A window (w = smoothstep(0.25A, A, alt)), so the day sky thins continuously into space as you climb. The forward ground view a01 shows the user’s actual viewpoint: bright day sky, pale horizon haze band, cloud deck below the horizon — no black dome.

Report 2 — “a large blue circle above and below you” in space

Root cause (confirmed in frames): the limb term was min(exp(-(b - u_atmo_radius)/u_atmo_falloff), 1.0) — for every ray inside the A-shell tangent (b < 5200) it evaluates to the flat maximum 1.0, and it drops to ~0 over just 300 m past 5200. Seen from outside the shell that paints a flat full-strength annulus with a hard circular outer edge at the A-shell tangent — exactly the “large blue circle”. Measured in b07 (2141 m): a flat L = 28.4 plateau from b = 5194 down to the disc, with a 0 → 28.4 jump compressed into b ∈ [5406, 5194]; b09 (3141 m): the flat 28.4 starts at exactly b = 5200. “Above and below” = the annulus surrounds the disc in all azimuths (it is a function of b only), and the same term lit the whole upward hemisphere from outside the shell (b08: a uniform full-strength dome looking straight up, median L = 74.5).

Report 3 — “the edge … does not extend past clouds”

Root cause: the same limb term. The cloud deck tops sit at R+450 (b ≈ 4450 at these views) but the flat annulus ran out to b = 5200 with a 300 m tail — the visible “edge” (and the whole 28.4 plateau) floated ~750 m past the cloud tops, so the atmosphere’s edge read as a separate surface sitting above the clouds, not as the clouds’ own envelope.

2. What was changed (per report)

All in aero_sky_gradient.gdshader, sky_on() only (the OFF path is bit-for-bit; genhash 25/25):

ReportChange
1The day/night/dusk gradient is now read on the ON path: keyed to local zenith elevation u (horizon → mid at 30 % → zenith at 90 %), then thinned to space through the A window: mix(grad, space, mix(w*w, w, u)) — zenith by w, grazing rays by w², so the horizon band lingers a little longer (the AC-0417 w = 1 → space contract holds exactly at and above A). A horizon haze band (exp(-|hl|*9), gated by 1−w) restores the pale day-horizon the AC-0401 wash used to give. Below the local cone the unchanged F3 mix applies.
2+3The limb term is now the forward air column through the shell’s outer tail (rout = R + A + 0.25A = 5500), camera-aware: the ray crosses the rout sphere at s = −r·hl ± √(rout²−b²); outside the shell, heading in, the visible air is the full chord (1.0 at the terrain limb, 0.89 at the deck top, 0.49 at the old A-shell tangent, 0.0 at the tail); inside the shell or pointing away it is the near exit (0.04 straight up at A; exactly 0 above the horizon from outside). Normalised by the full chord at the R+SEA disc tangent, clamped to 1.0 — smooth, bounded, no clamp-edge, no tail discontinuity. Same colour (horizon/mid mix), same sun-side boost, same AC-0401/AC-0417 gates.

Round-2 catch (why “r2”): the first limb revision used the plain 2-D chord formula, which painted the upper hemisphere from outside the shell as if the ray grazed it — a visible circular glow in the upward view (caught in a08-round-1, a variant of the very report-2 complaint). The camera-aware crossing (r2) removes it: a08 is now pure space.

3. Deck-vs-tangent-cone verdict (protected property)

VERIFIED in frames (not assumed): the cloud deck remains inside the planet’s tangent cone. At 2141 m the deck ring (measured b ≈ 4275–4450, top edge row 318 → b = 4403) sits strictly inside the shell tangent (b = 5200): 46.4° vs 57.9° from nadir. AFTER, the deck ring is wrapped by the glow (glow = 0.89 at the deck top vs 1.0 at the rim) and the glow extends to b = 5500 — 1050 m past the deck tops — so the deck is never the outer edge of the atmosphere again. The a07/a05 annulus frames show the deck ring inside the halo in both rounds.

4. Notes’ shell diagnosis — right or wrong?

Partially right. The notes hypothesised the “circle” was the atmosphere shell surfacing as a visible boundary: correct for report 2 — the circle is exactly the A-shell tangent of the clamped limb term (edge measured at b = 5194–5200 in b07/b09, the shell is at 5200). But report 1 is a separate root cause (gradient uniforms pushed, never read — the dome was black even at ground level, where the shell geometry plays no part), and report 3 is the same limb term’s clamp + 300 m tail geometry rather than a second surface. One shader rewrite covers all three.
