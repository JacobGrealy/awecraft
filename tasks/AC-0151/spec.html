Water grey 31/32 frames blue flicker frame0 only. Root cause data.gd:203 bake only base 32x32 but water anim 32 strip 1024px (blocks_atlas.json anim 32 at 224,0 fluid_anim.gdshader TILE_F 1/32). Fix loop f over anim_frames tint rc.y+f*32. Scope data.gd _bake + apply_atlas.

Goal
