AC-0448 — the options menu: every option reachable, the developer section filled

Verdict: the defect is confirmed exactly as suspected (growth without a container) and fixed: every tab page of the options panel is now a bounded, scrollable container, and the 12 feature-flag / internal switches sit under the Developer section where the user said they belong. Every setting keeps its key, default and env override; the settings arm is green with its row count reported; genhash is 25/25 byte-identical; the acceptance frame shows the bottom-most entry reached at the user's resolution (1280×720, the shipped default — and the worst case), with a 1920×1080 frame proving the fix at a larger size.

1. The defect, confirmed and measured

The options panel is a full-rect dim whose OptionsBox/Center was a CenterContainer holding one TabContainer. The TabContainer's minimum height is its tallest page's minimum; a CenterContainer centers a child that is taller than its own rect and — measured, not assumed — the Control then grows past the anchor rect to its minimum size (layout probe, variant B, at 1280×720: root_b.size=(1280,1339), tabs_b.min=(560,1339)). So the panel was 1339 px tall in a 720 px window, centered, with ≈309 px of the top (the tab bar) and ≈309 px of the bottom (the last rows, Load Texture Pack, Back) outside the window. Nothing clipped (no ScrollContainer anywhere), nothing scrolled: the off-screen rows were unclickable. The pre-fix frame (section 7, first image) shows it: the visible list starts at "Hunger" (rows above are above the screen) and ends at "Sky: space below the horizon" (rows below are below the screen), and the tab bar is cut off the top. The tscn comment from AC-0260 is the same disease at 12 px separation ("the centered container clipped the tab bar off the top of the screen"); the 12→6 separation cut was mitigation, not a fix.

2. The mechanism — why this one

Chosen from the ticket's four options (scrolling / paging / taller panel / moving entries): scrolling per tab page, with the restructure reducing the surface. The four alternatives, rejected:

  • Paging: new state machine + page chrome the visual language never had; it would also have to be added three times (all three pages overflow at 720 p).
  • A taller panel: 720 p is the shipped default and the smallest of the user's own RES_MODES (1280×720/1600×900/1920×1080/2560×1440) — no fixed height fits all four, and a panel taller than the window is the defect.
  • Moving entries off the top level alone: moving the 12 flags takes Settings from 41 to 29 rows (1009 px) — still taller than the 610 px page at 720 p. Restructuring is the polish (job 2); it does not remove the container disease.

The implemented chain, per tab page: ScrollContainer (vertical auto-scroll, horizontal off) → CenterContainer (vertical expand-fill) → the page's existing VBox. Two layout facts were measured before coding (a temporary standalone SceneTree probe, in and out in this task — its numbers, 1280×720 root):

  • A ScrollContainer page keeps the TabContainer's minimum compact (tabs.min=(568,31) — tab bar only), so the frame can actually bound it. (Pre-fix the TabContainer min was the full content height — that is why bounding the frame alone would not have worked and why the ScrollContainer is the load-bearing piece.)
  • A ScrollContainer sizes its child to the child's min size on the non-scroll axis and to max(child min, viewport) on the scroll axis. The CenterContainer (expand-fill) therefore gives: short page → exactly the viewport, content centered (the AC-0257 casualty — a bare ScrollContainer wrap broke the centering — is restored: measured vb2.pos=(260,146) = dead-centre for a 316 px page in a 609 px viewport); tall page → the content height, and the page scrolls (measured: scroll offset clamps to content − viewport = 699 for the 1308 px test page in a 609 px viewport).
  • The non-scroll-axis min-size behaviour would left-align the 560 px list in a wide tab area, so _sync_options_frame sets each page's CenterContainer minimum width to the frame's inner width — centreing holds at every resolution.

The tab area itself is bounded: OptionsBox/Center is now a MarginContainer (same node name; the tscn node type change is the only scene edit). Margins are computed at boot and on every window resize (resolution change, fullscreen toggle, manual resize): 40 px top/bottom, sides centering a panel of ~62 % of the window width (capped 820 px, floored at the list's 560 px min). Measured at 1280×720: the tab area is (243,40,801,640) — 40 px margin top and bottom, centred horizontally; the tab bar is visible for the first time. The engine's own thin scrollbar is the only new chrome — the existing Godot visual language, no paging state, no redesign.

Judgement for every row: could a player plausibly want to change it for their own comfort — not whether it exists. 12 rows moved under a new "Feature flags (internal switches)" group label on the Developer page; 15 switches + the classic sliders stay.
