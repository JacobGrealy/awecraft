Design note — why sources don't fall (stationary natural water)

MC wiki: oceans/rivers generate as stationary water and do not flow until block-updated — oceans never drain into caves on their own. AweCraft follows that: natural sea = fl=0 source field = zero writes, 100% stable invariant (asserted as sea-surface equality). Only water that receives an explicit flow state (fl=1..8, e.g. bucket place at 8) is dynamic and falls/spreads. Two intermediate findings are recorded because they shaped the fix:
