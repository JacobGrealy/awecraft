AC-0458 deviations (findings for the coordinator)

1) MIRROR FRACTION — plan §3.2 literal formula is a sawtooth; the continuous
   inverse was applied (chunk.gd _warp_face_slabs and tasks/AC-0433/warp_probe.gd).
   The plan's literal form — iu = ccx*16 + (15 - floor(lx)) with u = (iu + frac_x)/1024 —
   maps local slot s+f to chart coordinate ccx*16 + 15 - s + f. But the shipped data
   layout (sphere_math.gd:441-448, AC-0309) runs local +X AGAINST the face's u on the
   mirror class, so the slot's x=s edge sits at the cell's HIGHER-u edge: the exact
   inverse is u1024 = ccx*16 + 16 - lx, i.e. u = (iu + (1 - frac_x))/1024. The literal
   formula differs by (2*frac_x - 1) chart cells inside each slot — exactly one chart
   cell (~6.14 m at R=4000, 1024 cells per 6283 m face) at every local integer x,
   including the seam edges: the mirror-class mesh would fold one cell at every local
   metre. The continuous form applied here agrees with the plan's formula on ALL
   non-mirror faces and at every cell centre (where the legacy LSQ fit anchors, the
   M1-measured sites), and is the affine the legacy face_chunk_transform actually fits.
   Both variants give exactly 0 across-chunk seam gap, so the probe's expected value
   is unchanged — this corrects the in-cell placement, it does not relax a check.
