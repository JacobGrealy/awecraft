Registry notes (verbatim, quoted):

1) User Story: Today two chunks that meet at a seam place their shared edge with two different independent fits, so the ground disagrees across the join by up to 2.19 m at a crossing and 3.73 m at a corner. That is the visible cliff/stretch a player sees when standing on a seam. When this lands, the two sides of every seam evaluate the SAME surface point, so the join is exact at equal height and the cliff is gone - without changing the block grid, the chunk keys or the save format.

2) Technical Details:
   PREMISE (measured, AC-0433 M1 - tasks/AC-0433/m1_probe.gd, .scratch/AC-0433-gates/m1-probe.log):
   switching from the legacy per-chunk LSQ fit (SphereMath.face_chunk_transform, sphere_math.gd:470-549)
   to pointwise shared-vertex placement moves the ground a mean 0.955 m on the non-home interiors
   (max 1.861), 0.978 m at the home/face seam (max 2.003), 0.054 m on the home pair. Every maximum is
   at h=140; the h=0 displacements are centimetres (0.156 m at the documented corner site). The
   displacement IS the radial-shear term of the legacy rigid tilted frames.
   WHY THIS IS FIRST: it removes the seam defect the user photographed while keeping the 1024 grid,
   the "face:ccx:ccz" keys and the save format intact, so it is reversible and cannot desynchronise
   saves. The uniform-grid work is a later ticket in this series.
   DEPENDENCY: the verification instrument already exists and is verified - PlanetGrid
   (godot/core/planet_grid.gd, dormant, tasks/AC-0433/p2a..p2c2 probes).
   SCOPE (decided by the coordinator in the ticket plan, not here): placement emission only.
   VERIFY: the seam-V/AC-0423 probe set (ac0423probe must keep its standing overlaps), genhash 25/25,
   light gate set, plus a seam probe showing the across-seam gap at equal height going to 0.
   NOTE: chunk.gd/world.gd/native lane all consume placement; the plan must state which lanes change.
