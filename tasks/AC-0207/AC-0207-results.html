3. The one C++ bug the probe caught (fixed, re-verified 100%)

GDScript packed-array negative-index wrap. First probe run: blk + blk_b + faces all 32/32 exact, but eff 0/32. Root cause: the c=1 half of the E/N side strips and the a/b=1 half of the E/N corner strips compute a negative index — E: (y<<8)|(t<<4)|(0-1) = -1 (all bits set); N: (y<<8)|((-1)<<4)|t = t-16. GDScript wraps negative packed-array indices from the end (a[-k] = a[size-k] — verified empirically on 4.7.1: a[-1]=255, a[-13]=243 on a 256-byte array; high-side OOB is a fatal error that cannot occur here). So the E far-margin column wraps to the neighbor's LAST cell and the N far-margin column wraps to the neighbor's TOP row — a shipped quirk of the original, replicated verbatim by the new narr_idx() (wrap-then-bounds). Second probe run: 512/512 + 128/128 exact.
