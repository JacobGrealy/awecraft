AC-0451 P1 — deviations from the plan

1. The plan's A/B recipe is nondeterministic; the proof scene was redesigned
   (evidence-driven, not cosmetic).
   The plan's recipe (default seed 44, air preset alt 300, first-person
   spawn, air alt 300 pitch 55, drain 600) cannot carry a byte-identity
   gate: same-tree re-runs (no-op controls, zero code change between the
   two runs) do NOT reproduce their own frames. Measured on the unmodified
   post tree: aerial control 10,689 px (1.16%), ground and edge controls
   differ as well; at the corrected scene (below) a slanted edge view
   differs 151,067 px (16.4%) between two runs whose measured camera
   pose, aim, up-vector and every pushed uniform are BIT-IDENTICAL
   (the RESULT lines). The POST tree alone yields three different aerial
   hashes in five runs (10042ff6 x2, 5abe8626 x2, f4b3a5d3) while the
   PRE tree yields one hash in three runs (5b77ff02) — so even the
   pre/post aerial delta (242,908 px) is a per-run render state, not a
   tree effect. Three noise sources were isolated:
   (a) the TIME-driven animated fluid surface (fluid_anim.gdshader)
       wherever near-field water is in frame (the spawn pond sits inside
       the near zone of the plan's aerial view);
   (b) the shot is a FIXED frame count after body-LOADED (harness.gd
       air/planet branches) while world streaming is wall-clock budgeted
       (Time.get_ticks_msec) — the streamed/LOD state at shot time
       varies per run; the R24 world never reaches the build-drained
       state inside any sane budget ("SNAPDRAIN not fully drained" hits
       its cap even at 3000 frames);
   (c) sub-ulp floating-point state in the body mesh build (threaded,
       non-associative merges): amplified through UV sampling at slanted
       incidence into terrain-contour lines on every view that shows the
       textured body (four experiments fail their own no-op controls:
       pitch55/drain600, pitch55/drain2400, pitch70/drain600,
       nadir@325m).
   The redesigned scene (all views on SEED 45, whose spawn vicinity is
   flat and water-free within the near zone):
   - AERIAL (the ticket's mandatory annulus view): air preset
     AIR_ALT=471, NADIR. NOTE THE ALTITUDE FIX: AIR_ALT is the TRUE
     RADIAL altitude above the R sphere (harness.gd:1599-1601) and the
     spawn terrain sits ~142 m above the R sphere, so the plan's
     "h ~= 300 m" requires AIR_ALT=471, NOT 300 (AIR_ALT=300 is only
     159 m above surface and the animated pond stays in the near zone).
     At 471 the near zone is the ~44 m core disc (water-free), the
     annulus stays wide (44-221 m) and the d=332 wall, the [332,400]
     fade window and the d=400 render edge are all in-frame.
   - GROUND: first-person spawn aimed STRAIGHT DOWN (AIM 8.5,143.8,8.5,
     0,-1.57 — seed-45 spawn [8.5,142,8.5] + 1.8 m eye) so the frame is
     the core 3x3 chunks only (no water, no streaming ring, no body).
   - RENDER EDGE: air preset AIR_ALT=471, PITCH=55 (the [332,400]
     transition band crossed mid-frame). This view is proven
     nondeterministic (case 1c) and is reported as a noise-floor pair.

3. The predicate is inert by construction (expression-level audit).
   All 12 shader hunks: every removed
   `if (EXPR >= u_fog_far) { discard; }` was replaced by
   `float d = EXPR; if (d >= u_fog_far && !ac0451_covered(d))
   { discard; }` with EXPR byte-identical (distance(v_world_pos,
   u_cam_pos) in the two fluid shaders; the existing `d = length(V)`
   in lod_avg; distance(CAMERA_POSITION_WORLD, v_world_pos) in the
   eight chunk shaders + lod_avg_seam). `ac0451_covered(d)` is a marked
   stub returning `u_coverage > 0.5 && false` ≡ false, so the discard
   fires exactly where it fired pre-ticket. world.gd only pushes
   `u_coverage` (env-only, change-gated, AC-0445 precedent — no Options
   row) onto the same material sets as u_fog_far plus _lod_avg_material
   (AC-0443 latch pattern); both lod variants declare the uniform so
   the AC-0446 material-shader swap keeps the stored parameter valid.
   The body's own discard (satellite_body.gdshader) is untouched.

4. lod_avg_seam.gdshader was included (12 shaders changed, not 11):
   the plan's "every u_fog_far discard" rule — it carries the clause.

5. The plan's anchor main.gd:1477-1478 is stale: fog_far is pushed at
   main.gd:1538 (home: DayNight via daynight.gd) and the render-edge
   expression at main.gd:1523. The push sites were matched to the
   current code, not the line numbers.

6. Gate round 1 failed with SHADER ERRORs (light arm: 5 lines,
   g0: 2): the Godot shading language rejects the C-style
   `(void) d;` unused-parameter cast ("Void value not allowed in
   expression"); the casts were removed (unused function parameters are
   legal as-is). Round 2: all arms ok — g0/genhash/light rc=0,
   script_errors=0, shader_errors=0; genhash 25/25 byte-identical to
   the AC-0450 reference (verified by diff, .scratch/AC-0451-gates/
   genhash-verify.txt).

8. Fence compliance: no git add/commit/push, no registry edits, no new
