AC-0451 — no-discard-without-coverage

Registry notes (verbatim, quoted):

1) User Story:
When the player is above the ground - flying, standing on a ridge, or coming down out of the sky - there is a ring of world under and around him that no layer is responsible for drawing: the near field stops drawing at a fixed distance on purpose, and the far field refuses to start drawing until a second, larger fixed distance. From the ground that ring is the horizon, and the fog hides it. From the air it is directly underneath him, hundreds of metres wide, and through it he sees whatever is behind the world. The gap is not a loading artefact and waiting longer does not close it: it is the handover between two layers, and this ticket makes the handover a RULE ("stop drawing only where something else is already drawing") instead of a distance, so that no camera position can see through the world between them.

2) Technical Details:

THE TWO HALVES OF THE HANDOVER, both keyed to CAMERA distance, both read from the code rather than assumed:
- NEAR-FIELD PARTITION. Every near material discards its fragments at `d >= u_fog_far` (AC-0401): the four `world/chunk_lit_*.gdshader` kinds, `core/lod_avg.gdshader` and the two fluid shaders; `world.gd _ac0401_push` pushes the value change-gated and it is the same number `main.gd:1477` puts on `env.fog_depth_end`.
- BODY EXISTENCE. `core/satellite_body.gdshader:177-179`: `op = smoothstep(u_fog_far, u_render_edge, d); if (op <= 0.001) { discard; }`, with `ALPHA = op` (:251) and `render_mode cull_back, depth_draw_always, fog_disabled` (:87). `u_render_edge` is `(render_radius+1)*16` (`main.gd:1478`) - 400 m at the user's radius 24, against `fog_far` 332 m.
So the two layers are separated by construction: near dies at `fog_far`, body begins at `fog_far` and only becomes solid at `render_edge`.

THE CONSEQUENCE, and it is ARITHMETIC FROM THOSE TWO LINES, not yet measured - say so in the results page. For a camera at height h over the ground, ground within horizontal radius `sqrt(fog_far^2 - h^2)` is the near field's, ground past `sqrt(u_render_edge^2 - h^2)` is the body's, and the annulus between belongs to neither. At h ~ 2 m that annulus is the horizon and the fog hides it, which is why the defect survived every ground-level arm. At h ~ 300 m it is a wide disc directly under the player; at h >= `fog_far` the near field owns nothing at all in the downward direction, so the body is the only thing that can be there and it is discarding. THE USER'S FRAME IS CONSISTENT WITH THIS (built patch : pale region ~ 1.7-1.8, which is the ratio this predicts near h ~ 300 m at radius 24 / fog 332 / edge 400) - but the same frame is ALSO consistent with the open blur regression in AC-0449 (user correction: "not blue, I meant blur"). THE IDENTIFICATION IS A HYPOTHESIS TO BE SETTLED BY AC-0450's CENSUS, NOT A FINDING, and this ticket must not be built as though it were settled - the rule it lands is correct independently of which defect owns that frame.

THE RULE TO LAND. Replace "discard at `d >= u_fog_far`" with "discard only where COVERED", where coverage is asked of the layer that would take over. P1 lands the predicate with the coverage query stubbed to "never covered": behaviour must be BYTE-IDENTICAL to today's build, which is the inertness proof (the AC-0159 pattern - the off state is the strongest statement available that the change is surgical) and makes P1 independently committable and revertable. P2 switches the query on against the coverage surface filed as AC-0451. Do not collapse the two phases: P1 changes nothing the user can see or the gates can measure beyond byte-identity, P2 is the look change.

OWNERSHIP MUST BE DETERMINISTIC. Where two surfaces coexist in a frame the finer one must win by GEOMETRY, not by draw order - the construction the body itself already uses: its vertex stage pulls the displaced surface 2 m radially inward so that "wherever the same terrain still coexists the near tiers win the depth test by construction - no flicker" (ARCHITECTURE.md, SatelliteBody bullet). The handover must also be SAME-FRAME (AC-0234's rule: a placeholder yields in the frame the real thing lands, never a frame of coplanar fighting) and must reuse the existing stale-LOD cache (`low_cache_kept_n` / `low_cache_hit_n`, the "keep showing the old surface until the new one is ready" path) rather than inventing a second one.

SWITCH DECISION, made at filing per the standing rule that every look change ships with a player-facing switch. This change prevents holes and therefore must NOT default to showing holes, so: `AWECRAFT_COVERAGE=0|1` for the arms to render both states, and NO Options row - recorded explicitly, by precedent: AC-0445 decided the column fade-in was "a visual nicety, not a switchable feature" (user 2026-10-17, toggles are for visual elements and only when explicitly asked). The OFF path stays byte-identical to today, which is what makes the A/B renders meaningful.

WHAT THIS TICKET DOES NOT FIX, and must not be widened to: anything before residency (a column that is not streamed in has nothing to draw at any fidelity - that is AC-0399's half, the fill rate and the required set). It must not move the body's op window with respect to the FAR field beyond the render edge (AC-0428 closed "the planet is opaque again"), must not touch the far tier's shading (AC-0443 / AC-0444 / AC-0446), and must not touch the home/face lane split (AC-0433). If any of those appears to be required, stop and file it separately.

VERIFY. AC-0450's census at the airborne vantage: background fraction (a) at or near zero in the same frame the user photographed, with the negative test proving the counter can rise; the A/B with `AWECRAFT_COVERAGE=0` must REPRODUCE the region; genhash 25/25 (a partition and ordering change cannot move generation - any delta is a FINDING); G0 zero SCRIPT ERROR and zero SHADER ERROR via `tasks/scripts/gate_census.py` AND the exit status; the standing set green (farab h_mismatch 0, ladder 20/20, meshprobe 1.0, halo ok, lightstate 0/0, r16 all sub-flags with far_holes 0/0 both bands, boundary r4 in band, shaderforce 12/12); renders at radius 24 / fog 332 / edge 400 across the AC-0399 ladder (2600 / 1600 / 700 / 300 m) because a visual feature verified at one set of settings is verified at one set of settings - the AC-0394 haze bug was invisible to every arm for exactly this reason.
