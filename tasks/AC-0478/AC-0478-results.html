1. Provenance — how the shipped PNGs were produced (and the path still exists)

The assets are the in-engine bake worker's own output, re-shipped in-process by the harness-only AWECRAFT_SATELLITE_SHIP=<abs dir> env. Run: AWECRAFT_LOGIC=satellite + AWECRAFT_SATELLITE_REBAKE=1 (forces the full bake through the sharded pipeline, sat_preload default ON) — at assembly SatelliteBakeWorker.ship_faces (satellite_bake_worker.gd:645-663, called from satellite_body.gd:808) byte-copies the 12 cache PNGs into the ship dir, then the .ctex imports are regenerated by the engine. The Oct 4 set was produced by exactly this path (AC-0384 r2/r3: "satellite (REBAKE + SHIP, seed 44): bake wall 793.9 s, guard 12/12, rebuild_vs_shipped identical (589,824 samples, diff 0), 12 faces shipped", re-imported; the 793.9 s is the pre-sD bake cost). THE PATH STILL EXISTS, UNCHANGED — no approximation was invented. (The historic AC-0310/AC-0311 python rebake over a dumped far payload is superseded; the in-engine ship is the canonical mechanism.)
