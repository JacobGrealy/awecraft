Registry notes (verbatim, quoted):

1) User Story: As a player travelling around the planet I want the far side of a face boundary to be the same world I was standing in - caves, ores and biomes flowing across it instead of stopping at a seam.

2) Technical Details: The seam end-state (piece P7 of the epic; docs/planet-epic.html sections 07 C1 and 08 P7). QUEUED LAST BY USER DECISION 2026-09-17: nothing else depends on it, and the cave tickets were deliberately left unconstrained, so this ticket owns its own field-coordinate design work.

CONTENT: make the surface, cave, ore and biome fields functions of a global coordinate pair - a face-independent surface parametrisation (the direction vector, which is continuous everywhere on the sphere) plus a radial depth so that cave layering stays vertical at every latitude - sample them per column in the column's local frame, then DELETE the blend band that the cross-face piece introduced.

FLAT-FRAME CONSTRUCTS THAT MUST BE RE-DERIVED (read the cave tickets' SHIPPED implementation, not their specs, since this ticket translates a known-good design): carvers are Bezier trunks with world-space Y ranges (-56..180) (AC-0290); aquifers are a Voronoi cell grid in the flat frame (AC-0291); 3D cave biomes plus surface rules and pillars/veins (AC-0292); the surface height fields and the cheese/ore density fields are 3D fbm3 sampled on flat lattice points (gdext/src/gen.cpp).

COST: AC-0042 section 08 estimated +2-3x generation for sphere-domain sampling. Re-measure gen ms/col and the far lane (~92 us per column-chunk) before and after; streaming throughput depends on both.

THIS IS THE ONLY PIECE IN THE EPIC ALLOWED TO MOVE GENHASH, and it may only do so deliberately.

GATES: a new genhash baseline, two runs byte-identical; boundary terrain continuity (a data-level assertion that both faces' columns agree at the shared edge, across all 24 boundary segments - testable headlessly, before any placement work); generation cost re-measured and the standing values re-established deliberately (godot/HARNESS.md section 3); boundary r4 unchanged or better.

DEPENDS: the cave series (AC-0288..AC-0292) complete - it rewrites gdext/src/gen.cpp, which this touches; and the cross-face piece (to remove the band). Owes ARCHITECTURE.md sync (generation/sampling domain).

CONSEQUENCE FOR PLAYER BUILDS (recorded from the AC-0309 edit-interaction question, 2026-09-17): deleting the blend band CHANGES the terrain in that strip, so any player edits inside it are re-applied over different ground - a build in the band could end up floating or buried. The strip is narrow (tens of columns) and sits 3141 m from spawn, so this is acceptable under the same "fresh worlds only" precedent the cave series uses (user 2026-09-15) - but state it in the ticket rather than discovering it, and either accept it explicitly or migrate the affected edits.

RESOLVED (user, 2026-09-17): "it's fine for changes to break saved worlds at this point of development." So the band's removal breaks builds inside the strip BY DESIGN and owes NO migration - do not build edit migration for it. What is still owed: bump SAVE_VERSION (or the column blob version) so an old world is rejected cleanly with a log line and starts fresh, rather than half-loading. The standing policy now lives in godot/ARCHITECTURE.md section 5 ("Save compatibility policy") and godot/world/AGENTS.md.
