1. The design

1.1 The global pair

Every generated point is a point P in the planet frame (planet centre = origin; the shipped world frame is the planet frame shifted by (0, −R, 0), so P = world-position + (0, R, 0)). The global coordinate pair is:

  • Direction d = P / |P| — the unit vector. A single global function on the sphere, continuous across every face boundary by construction (it does not know about faces). This is the "face-independent surface parametrisation" of the ticket.
  • Radial depth δ = |P| − R — metres above the base radius (the flat world's y = 0 sits on the radius-R facet plane of its chunk, so δ is exactly the column's local y at the chunk centre and within the facet sagitta d²/2R ≈ 0.02 m (d ≤ 8√2) across a 16-m facet at R = 4000). Cave layering stays vertical at every latitude because local y IS the radial: both placements (SphereMath.column_transform home, face_chunk_transform faces 2–11) point local +Y along the radial at the chunk centre.

1.2 How a column's local frame samples it

Generation keeps its exact shape — per chunk, per column, the fields are built on the existing local-frame lattices (the 441-point coarse grid, 4-block x/z cells × 48-block y rows; the 2,401-point C48 cave grid, 8-block y rows) and read by the existing tril/tril_c. The ONLY change is the coordinate fed to each noise call. For each lattice point L = (lx, ly, lz) in the column's local frame:

P  = T_chunk · L            // the chunk's shipped placement transform (3×3 affine)
d  = normalize(P + (0, R, 0))
δ  = |P + (0, R, 0)| − R

and the substitution applied to every noise source is:

tangential:  x_flat → R·d.x        z_flat → R·d.z      (flat metres → arc metres)
radial:      y_flat → δ            (flat height → radial depth above R)

Each field then applies its existing scale/offset to those coordinates unchanged, e.g. the surface c-field goes fbm3(x/220, y/64, z/220, seed, 2) → fbm3((R/220)·d.x, δ/64, (R/220)·d.z, seed, 2); a multiply-family field vn3(x·sx, y·sy, z·sx, …) → vn3((R·sx)·d.x, δ·sy, (R·sx)·d.z, …). In the home-centre limit R·d.x ≈ x (the flat embedding), so the shipped field is the limit of the new one — the re-baseline drift is second order in patch radius and concentrated at the patch edge, where the old field's coordinates were already far outside the developable range.

Cost note on the map itself: the (d, δ) pair is a function of the lattice point only — 2,401 unique points per full chunk (the 441 coarse points sit on the C48 rows: 48-block y rows ⊂ 8-block rows) — so it is precomputed once per chunk and shared by all 16 field builds (10 C48 + 6 coarse). The per-field per-point cost is the same count of multiplies as before (a divide became a multiply — actually slightly cheaper). No per-block direction computation exists anywhere: the scan reads lattice values, not noise.

1.5 The continuity argument

After the move, every field value at a block is a single-valued function of (d, δ). d is continuous across every face boundary (a global function on S²); δ is continuous (radial coordinate). The chart used to find (d, δ) from a flat position is C⁰ across edges by the grid-lock's gapless construction (the per-coordinate pre-warp makes the edge coordinates of adjacent faces agree), so the same physical point read from either face gets the same (d, δ). Therefore the generated columns on the two sides of any of the 24 boundary segments are the same world up to (a) the lattice quantisation (4/8-block cells — a domain-wide sampling granularity, not a boundary artifact) and (b) the per-chunk scoping of the carvers and the aquifer's per-block search (per-chunk by design on both sides today). The 24-segment data-level assertion of the ticket's GATES becomes: for each shared edge, the two faces' edge columns' (d, δ) samples agree and their generated H / cell content agree.

2.2 Where the +2-3× estimate's uncertainty actually sits

AC-0042 §08's estimate ("sample FBM at p = normalize(cube_pos)·scale … ~2-3× cost") priced the dense reading: re-evaluating the 3-D noise at a normalised direction for every sample, in contrast to a flat 2-D per-face heightmap. Its uncertainty is entirely in the reading density, not in the domain: the lattice is orthogonal to the coordinate choice. The design keeps the per-column local-frame lattice (the ticket's own words: "sample them per column in the column's local frame"), so the octave-evaluation count — 100 % of the field cost — is invariant, and the only new term is the 2,401-point (d, δ) precompute (one normalisation per point, amortised across 16 fields). Had the fields gone dense-per-block, the estimate would have been the right one (2-3× the field stage and more); that is the form the design explicitly rejects on cost grounds. The measurement replaces the estimate: 167.7 ns per dense sphere-domain H evaluation (the probe's C++ bench, 20,000 evals, the home-hemisphere analytic form) decomposes into 6 vnoise3 × 23.0 ns = 138 ns of field work (unchanged from today) + 29.7 ns of (d, δ) precompute (2 tan + normalise + the coordinate math) — i.e. the new work is 17.7 % of one field evaluation, and it is paid once per lattice point across all 16 fields, never per block.

4. The deletion plan (blend band)

Recorded so a later run can delete it without archaeology. The band is the C1 interim (epic §07: "a blend band near the boundary … End state: sphere-domain generation (P7), after which the band is deleted").

4.1 Why it exists (the C1 contract)

Faces 2–11 are independent salted flat patches (generate_face: seed ^ (face·1000003) on a face-disjoint 2D domain offset face·64 chunks). Without the band, the far side of a home edge is a different random world with a 383-m cliff potential; the band (32 face cells ≈ 196 m) fades each boundary column's height only into the home-extended field (the home pipeline's per-column H at the cell's extended flat position, LRU-cached 512 entries) with weight 1 − d/band = 1 on the edge — so at the edge the face terrain IS the home terrain continued: the far side is ground and the boundary is continuous in HEIGHT. It is height-only by contract (composition/caves stay the face's own — "blend field VALUES — height — only"), which is exactly why the residual is the along-seam V-crack (gradient kink, −4.69 m at a patch boundary, AC-0309/AC-0368) and the un-blended strip seen from orbit (AC-0310).

4.2 What removing it requires

  1. The sphere-domain fields must be live first (AC-0311 pieces 2+): the band is deleted only once both sides of every boundary read the same (d, δ) field — i.e. the per-construct moves of §1.3 have landed and the 24-segment data-level continuity assertion passes. Deleting the band before that puts the cliff back (the C1 "far side must be ground" contract breaks).
  2. Code to delete (all in godot/world/generator.gd unless noted): const FACE_BLEND_BAND; _home_h_cache / _home_h_order / _HOME_H_CACHE_CAP; _home_ext_height(); _col_top(); _blend_column(); _blend_face_chunk(); the if R > 0.0 and face >= 4: _blend_face_chunk(…) branch in generate_face(). In godot/core/sphere_math.gd: face_cell_band() (band query) — but check consumers first: godot/world/world.gd uses face_cell_band at ~:12874/:12969 (the face-stream edge window + the along-edge distance) and WorldGen.FACE_BLEND_BAND at ~:13118 (the face-stream evict margin win + FACE_BLEND_BAND·S + FACE_STREAM_EVICT_MARGIN); harness.gd uses both in the crossface arm (~:29226, :30234, :30255, :30324, :30335). home_flat_ext() is consumed by face_cell_band and possibly the face-stream — re-check before deleting; the stream window itself survives (the band width just becomes 0 and the evict margin drops to the plain window + margin). The crossface arm's blend assertions (the b-edge delta, the d3/d5 feel checks that read _home_ext_height) must be re-based to the no-band continuity (which is then the field's own continuity, so the arm's crossing expectations tighten, not loosen).
  3. Gates that change deliberately: crossface (walks the seam — the chasm numbers are the band's, they re-baseline to the placement-split-only class); boundary r4 (walks a boundary — must be re-run and re-established, "unchanged or better" per the ticket); the satellite bake (the un-blended strip at the home edge disappears — the AC-0310 textures are a re-bake, the rebake pixel-compare re-baselines); genhash is UNTOUCHED by the band itself (it only runs on faces ≥ 4 — outside the 5×5 home window) so the band deletion owes no genhash re-baseline; the sphere-domain re-baseline of pieces 2+ already owns that. farab / ladder / meshprobe / halo / lightstate / r16 / battery are home-window instruments — unchanged by a face-4-11-only band deletion.
  4.
