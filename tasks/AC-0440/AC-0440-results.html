AC-0440 — results: the spawn "16×16 m dark square" on flat grass

Verdict: the dark square was never a lighting fault. It is the DDA aim highlight (the target indicator, player.gd _build_highlight) — a 1.02 m BoxMesh filled 28% black — drawn over the spawn cell while the r24 assessment camera looks straight down. Shipped fix: the indicator is now a black wireframe edge outline (no filled face), behind Settings "aim_wire" (default ON) + AWECRAFT_AIMWIRE=0|1 + an options-menu row. All light gates pass; the tree is pristine (temporary probe removed and re-gated).

1) What shared one value across 16 m — the mechanism

Nothing in the lighting. The ticket's premise (a lighting value shared across 16 m, chunk-boundary granularity) is falsified by measurement:

  • The frame lies wholly inside chunk (0,0). No seam is in the frame at all; there is no 16 m structure anywhere in it. The frame spans only ~10.3 m of ground (nadir distance 2.61 m at the live camera; 1 m ≈ 118 px).
  • The terrain is flat. All 25 cells of the 5×5 around spawn: top = 140, top block id = 1 (grass). No pit.
  • The mesh is fully lit. Every top-slab vertex of chunk (0,0) in the square region carries R=1.0, G=0.0, B=1.0 — full sun, no occlusion, no darkened data.
  • The one value shared across the square is the highlight's constant 28% black fill — over 1.02 m, not 16 m. Measured darkening ratio inner/ring ≈ 0.73–0.80 (the 0.28-alpha overlay in linear light is 0.72; ~0.75–0.80 after ACES at mid-tones).

Projection proof (the decisive measurement): the r24 frame was shot in fly mode, where the player camera's live FOV is 98.99° — player.tscn base 90° × the 1.10 fly boost (the 75° figure in main.gd _make_camera is a different camera). With the live transform (pos 8.787, 143.602, 8.787; fwd −0.002, −0.997, −0.073; FOV 98.99; aspect 1280/720), the highlight's top face projects to px (578, 321)–(701, 443); the observed dark square edges are (580, 320)–(703, 442) — within ±2 px on all four sides. The "16 m" reading was a scale misjudgement of a 1 m UI element; "the same problem remains inside chunks" is consistent — it is one cell mid-chunk by construction. The scene-graph census (temporary probe, reproducing the r24 AIM state) reports the highlight visible=true at world [8.78, 140.48, 8.78].

Since the product ships on Windows, the outline is real geometry so it renders identically on any rasterizer.

9) Findings (harvest candidates)

  • StandardMaterial3D.wireframe is ignored on this dev pipeline (Forward+/llvmpipe, Godot 4.7.1 build): a box with the flag renders as a solid fill. Measure outline geometry, don't flag it, if the look must be verified on this box.
  • This engine build's MultiMesh is the Godot-3-style API: the mesh rides MultiMesh.mesh (not base_mesh), instance_count (not transform_count), and the material rides the base mesh (MultiMesh has no material property; the particles pool does exactly this — godot/entities/particle_pool.gd). get_instance_transform() read-back returns the identity even after set_instance_transform(); the write path renders correctly — don't trust the read-back for diagnostics.
  • The r24 "16×16 m square" was a scale misjudgement: fly FOV 99° makes 1 m ≈ 118 px, so a 1.02 m UI box fills a tenth of the frame. (Also: the player camera's base FOV is 90 in player.tscn, not the 75 in main.gd _make_camera — a different camera.)
