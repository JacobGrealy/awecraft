AC-0440 — plan: the spawn "16×16 m dark square" on flat grass

Goal

The assessment (AC-0384 §29.3 item 4) found "one 16×16 cell renders distinctly darker than its neighbours with sharp 16 m chunk boundaries" on the flat-grass ground-down-day frame and filed AC-0440 on the premise that a lighting value is shared across 16 m inside chunks. Forensics on this ticket falsify the premise and identify the square: it is the DDA aim highlight (player.gd _build_highlight, a 1.02³ BoxMesh with an unshaded Color(0,0,0,0.28) material — the target indicator) drawn over the spawn cell while the camera looks straight down. Terrain is flat (all 25 cells of the 5×5 around spawn top=140, grass), the top-slab vertex colours in the region are all R=1.0/B=1.0 (full sun, no occlusion), and the highlight's projected top face matches the observed dark rectangle within 2 px on all four edges once the camera is modelled with its live transform (fly FOV 99° = 90° base × 1.10 fly boost — the 75° figure from _make_camera is a different camera; at 99° the nadir scale is 118 px/m, so the 1.02 m box is 120 px, exactly the observed square). The "16 m" reading was a scale misjudgement of a 1 m UI element; the frame spans only ~10.3 m of ground, wholly inside chunk (0,0). Measured darkening ratio inner/ring = 0.750 — the 0.28-alpha black overlay (linear 0.72, ~0.75 after ACES at mid-tones).

What shared one value across 16 m: nothing in the lighting. The one value shared across the square is the highlight's constant 28% fill — over 1.02 m, not 16 m. No lighting, meshing, bake or seam path is involved, and none is touched by this fix. The ticket's "don't smooth/blur" fence is therefore met trivially (there is no boundary to blur); the square disappears by making the indicator stop darkening the aimed face.

Deliverable: ship the indicator as a black wireframe outline (no fill — the aimed face keeps its terrain lighting; the mark reads as an outline, not a differently-lit patch), behind Settings "aim_wire", default ON (the look the user reported is the defect — the AC-0426/0432/0436 re-ruling: the fix ships in, OFF is the exact pre-fix 28% fill), env-overridable (AWECRAFT_AIMWIRE=0|1, the viewmodel_occlude preload pattern) and wired into the options menu. Re-take the r24 flat-grass frame in both states (+ one more hour), measure the luminance profile across the former boundary before/after, re-measure the AC-0427 seam twin result (190/191 level twins 0.000) with an extended temporary probe, and check wider-scene contrast. Light gates only; heavy gates are the coordinator's.
