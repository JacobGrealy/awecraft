3. Edit shape

3.1 The occluder. Today a slab adds OccluderInstance3D with a 15×15×15 BoxOccluder3D at local (8, y0+8, 8). That position is only meaningful while the chunk node carries the legacy placement; under the warp the node is identity, so the box lands in the wrong place and would occlude the wrong volume (a correctness bug that can hide geometry).

Change the occluder condition to also require not _warp_on: with the warp on, s.occluder is left null and no occluder node is added. Add one comment line saying why (the box is authored in the legacy frame; occlusion culling is an optimisation and is off under the warp until a later ticket re-derives it). Do not attempt to reposition the box — a single axis-aligned box cannot represent a warped slab.

3.2 Collision — verify, do not change. _build_slab_collision reads mesh.surface_get_arrays(0) and adds the body as a child of the chunk, so when the mesh vertices are world-space and the node is identity, the shape is world-space. Confirm that by reading the function and state it in the deviations; make no edit.

3.3 The probe (new tasks/AC-0461/warp_space_probe.gd). Pure math, no world: it reproduces the §3.2 warp construction of AC-0458 for a face chunk and asserts the space it lands in.

  • For face chunks on faces 2..11 (both mirror classes), take the chunk's 16×16 cell corner lattice and compute the warped world position of every corner from local coordinates.
  • A1 world-space magnitude: the warped positions must lie on the sphere shell, i.e. |P + (0,R,0)| - R is 0 within 1e-3 m for h=0 corners (report max shell error). A legacy-framed vertex would sit at a few metres from the origin and fail this by kilometres — that is the point of the check.
  • A2 neighbour agreement: for each pair of adjacent chunks, the shared edge's corner positions computed from each side's own locals must agree (max gap 0), re-using the AC-0458 result so a regression is visible from this ticket too.
  • A3 no local-frame leftovers: assert no corner of a warped chunk has |y| < 1 AND |x|,|z| < 40 simultaneously — i.e. nothing is still sitting near the origin in chunk-local metres on a chunk whose canonical position is far away. Report the count of suspicious points (must be 0).
  • Negative control: skip the warp for one chunk (use the legacy transform product) and confirm A1 fails on it (negative_detected true).

RESULT keys: ok, chunks, corners, max_shell_error_m, max_paired_gap_m, local_frame_suspects, negative_detected, fails (JSON, one line).
